# Listed derivatives execution

> Generated by `tools/build_domain_views.py`; edit `overview.md`, `company-questions.md` or the registers instead.

This domain describes how a client's order to buy or sell listed futures and options becomes executed trades, split across the right accounts and handed to clearing, either the firm's own or another clearing broker through a give-up.

## How it flows

[Order](concepts.md#order) through a handling channel ([Order Handling Channel](concepts.md#order-handling-channel)) → [Execution](concepts.md#execution) on a [Trading Venue](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#trading-venue) → [Trade](concepts.md#trade) → [Average Price Group](concepts.md#average-price-group) → [Allocation Instruction](concepts.md#allocation-instruction) before the [Cut-off](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#cut-off) → [Allocation](concepts.md#allocation) → internal, or give-up ([Give-up / Take-up Process](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#give-up--take-up-process)) under a [Give-up Agreement](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#give-up-agreement) → the clearing page takes over at member take-up ([Clearing Member Acceptance](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#clearing-member-acceptance)) → charges ([Client Execution Fee](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#client-execution-fee), [Executing Broker Brokerage Charge](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#executing-broker-brokerage-charge), [Exchange Fee Charge](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#exchange-fee-charge))

## Concepts at a glance

- **[Order](concepts.md#order)** — A request to buy or sell a quantity of a listed contract, under conditions such as a price limit or how long it stays valid.
  - **[Client Order](concepts.md#client-order)** — The order as the client gave it to the broker, before the broker splits or routes it.
  - **[Child Order](concepts.md#child-order)** — A smaller order the broker cuts from the client's order and sends to the market.
  - **[Pre-trade Control Check](concepts.md#pre-trade-control-check)** — An automatic check that stops an order going to the market if it breaks a limit.
- **[Order Handling Channel](concepts.md#order-handling-channel)** — How one order was worked and reached the venue: by a person at the broker, by the broker's algorithm, by the client through the broker's systems, or by the client directly under the broker's membership.
- **[Execution](concepts.md#execution)** — The moment all or part of an order is filled at a price, by matching on the venue or by the venue registering a privately negotiated trade.
  - **[Venue Execution](concepts.md#venue-execution)** — A fill as the venue records it, against the order the member entered there.
  - **[Client Execution](concepts.md#client-execution)** — A fill as the broker reports it to the client.
  - **[Block Trade](concepts.md#block-trade)** — A large trade agreed privately between two parties and then registered on the venue under special rules.
  - **[Exchange for Related Position](concepts.md#exchange-for-related-position)** — A swap of a futures position against a matching position in the underlying or in an OTC derivative, agreed privately and registered on the venue.
  - **[Strategy Leg Execution](concepts.md#strategy-leg-execution)** — The fill in one leg contract that results when a spread or option combination is filled.
- **[Trade](concepts.md#trade)** — The agreed deal between two counterparties on a derivative contract, with its terms; later steps such as clearing create new linked trades rather than changing it.
  - **[Executed Trade](concepts.md#executed-trade)** — The trade as it exists right after execution, before the CCP steps in.
- **[Transaction](concepts.md#transaction)** — An ambiguous word that is not used as a concept for now: ask whether the speaker means an execution, a trade, a lifecycle event, a cash movement or a regulatory transaction report.
- **[Average Price Group](concepts.md#average-price-group)** — A set of fills combined so that the client gets one average price, calculated either by a venue or CCP facility or by the broker.
  - **[Average Price Residual](concepts.md#average-price-residual)** — The small leftover amount created when an average price is rounded.
- **[Allocation Instruction](concepts.md#allocation-instruction)** — The client's instruction saying which accounts, and which clearing brokers, should receive which part of the executed quantity.
  - **[Standing Allocation Instruction](concepts.md#standing-allocation-instruction)** — A split rule the client sets once, which the broker applies to every matching trade.
- **[Allocation](concepts.md#allocation)** — The actual split of executed quantity across the target accounts, one line per account.
  - **[Allocation Line](concepts.md#allocation-line)** — One row of a split: so many lots, at this price, to this account.
- **[Executing Broker](concepts.md#executing-broker)** — The firm that provides execution to the client and answers to the client for it, whether or not it is itself a member of the venue.
- **[Trading Venue Member](concepts.md#trading-venue-member)** — A firm admitted under a venue's rules to enter orders and register trades there in its own name.
  - **[Third-party Executing Member](concepts.md#third-party-executing-member)** — A venue member that another broker uses to reach a venue where it is not a member.
- **[Execution Correction](concepts.md#execution-correction)** — A later fix or cancellation of a fill, recorded as a new event that points back to the original.
