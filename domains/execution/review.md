## 1. Concepts you approve

| Concept | Definition | Your verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **[Order](concepts.md#order)** | Instruction from one party to another, or to a trading venue, to buy or sell a specified quantity of an instrument under stated execution conditions (such as order type, price limit, time in force and handling instructions). An order given by a client to its broker and each order the broker derives from it to work it are separate orders, linked parent to child. | |
| **[Order Handling Channel](concepts.md#order-handling-channel)** | Governed classification of how a given order is worked by the executing broker and reaches the venue: care (worked by a person at the broker), broker algorithm, direct market access (orders generated by the client and routed through the broker's order infrastructure and controls) or sponsored access (orders sent by the client directly to the venue under the venue member's identity). It describes one order at a point in time and can change while the order is worked; it is not the client's permission to use direct electronic access, which belongs to the market access arrangement, and it is unrelated to a CCP's sponsored clearing model. Whether the client itself uses an algorithm, and how the price was formed (execution method), are separate attributes. | |
| **[Execution](concepts.md#execution)** | Event by which all or part of an order is filled at an agreed price and time, either by matching on a trading venue or by the venue's registration of a privately negotiated trade under its rules (such as a block trade or an exchange for related position). An execution is recorded per order concerned: a match on a venue yields an execution for each participating order, and a broker's venue-facing and client-facing executions are separate events. Corrections and cancellations are later events that refer to it. | |
| **[Trade](concepts.md#trade)** | Agreement between one pair of counterparties on a derivative contract with stated economic terms (instrument or bespoke terms, direction, quantity, price and trade date), resulting from one or more executions or from a later deriving event such as CCP interposition, allocation or transfer. A trade is distinct from the records that represent it; clearing, compression and porting create successor trades linked to it rather than changing it. Which clearing tier is meant (pre-clearing, CCP-facing or member-client) must be stated. | |
| **[Transaction](concepts.md#transaction)** | Business operation affecting rights, obligations or balances; this broad working definition requires validation and must not become a catch-all synonym. | |
| **[Average Price Group](concepts.md#average-price-group)** | Set of executions, normally in one instrument and on one side for one client or order, that are combined so that the resulting trades or client-facing executions carry a single average price, under an averaging method applied either by a venue or CCP average-price facility or by the broker. Which executions are eligible, how the average is rounded and how any residual is handled follow the facility's rules or the client agreement. | |
| **[Allocation Instruction](concepts.md#allocation-instruction)** | Instruction, given before or after execution by a client or its investment manager, specifying how an executed or average-priced quantity is split across target accounts and, where relevant, clearing brokers; versioned and amendable until the applicable cut-off. | |
| **[Allocation](concepts.md#allocation)** | Assignment of a quantity or amount to target beneficiaries or accounts; instruction and completed event are later specialisations. | |
| **[Executing Broker](concepts.md#executing-broker)** | Role of a party that provides an execution service to a client for identified orders and is responsible to that client for how they are executed, whether it enters the orders on the venue itself as a trading venue member or through another member. It is not necessarily the venue member, the executing entity named in a regulatory transaction report, or the clearing member to which the trade is first registered. | |
| **[Trading Venue Member](concepts.md#trading-venue-member)** | Role of a party admitted under a trading venue's rules, as member or participant, to enter orders and register trades in its own name; distinct from clearing membership and from the client-facing Executing Broker role. | |
| **[Client Order](concepts.md#client-order)** *(level 2)* | Order given by a client, or by a party authorised to act for it, to its executing broker, carrying the client's instructions on quantity, price limit, validity and handling; it is the order against which the broker reports client executions and is accountable to the client. | |
| **[Child Order](concepts.md#child-order)** *(level 2)* | Order derived by a broker, a trader or an algorithm from a parent order to work part of its quantity, on a venue or through another broker; a child order sent to a venue is entered by a trading venue member and carries that member's identity. It keeps a link to its parent and inherits the parent's handling channel unless re-routed. | |
| **[Pre-trade Control Check](concepts.md#pre-trade-control-check)** *(level 2)* | Check applied to an order before it reaches the venue, or by the venue on entry, against limits set by the executing broker, the venue member, the clearing member or the venue (such as price collars, maximum order size, credit or position limits), with the outcome pass, reject or hold. | |
| **[Venue Execution](concepts.md#venue-execution)** *(level 2)* | Execution of an order entered on a trading venue by a venue member, formed by matching or by registration of a negotiated trade, as recorded by the venue with its own identifier and time. | |
| **[Client Execution](concepts.md#client-execution)** *(level 2)* | Execution reported by the executing broker to the client against the client order, for a quantity and price that reflect one or more venue executions, at their own prices or at an average price; it is the execution on which client execution fees and client confirmations are based. | |
| **[Block Trade](concepts.md#block-trade)** *(level 2)* | Execution of a privately negotiated trade at or above a venue's minimum size for the contract, agreed away from the order book and registered on the venue under its block trade rules (price conditions, reporting window, eligible participants); it is then cleared like any other listed trade. | |
| **[Exchange for Related Position](concepts.md#exchange-for-related-position)** *(level 2)* | Execution of a privately negotiated exchange of a listed futures or options position for an opposite related position in another instrument (cash, OTC or physical), registered on the venue under its rules; the related position is a separate trade or holding outside the venue that must exist and be documented. Eligibility, minimum size and reporting window are set by the venue's rules for the contract. | |
| **[Strategy Leg Execution](concepts.md#strategy-leg-execution)** *(level 2)* | Execution of one leg of a listed strategy, produced by the venue when a strategy order is filled, in the leg's own instrument with a leg price assigned under the venue's rules; allocation, give-up and clearing usually operate on leg executions, not on the strategy. | |
| **[Execution Correction](concepts.md#execution-correction)** *(level 2)* | Event by which a venue, under its error-trade rules, or the broker toward its client, under the client agreement, cancels or corrects a previously recorded execution (price, quantity, account or other detail), at any time, referring to the original execution, which is kept. If the trade is already registered at a CCP, the CCP records the resulting change to the registered trade, which may lead to a Trade Amendment or raise an Operational Exception. | |
| **[Executed Trade](concepts.md#executed-trade)** *(level 2)* | Trade at the pre-clearing tier: the trade as agreed at execution between the two parties on the venue or, for the client-facing side, between the executing broker and the client where the broker acts as principal, before or at the moment of CCP interposition. Where the clearing service's rules provide for open offer, it exists only as the record of what was matched, and the CCP-facing trades arise at matching. It is succeeded by the CCP-facing trades and, under the principal model, the client back-to-back trades. | |
| **[Average Price Residual](concepts.md#average-price-residual)** *(level 2)* | Amount left over when an average price is rounded for booking, so that the value booked at the rounded average differs from the value of the underlying executions, attributable to an identified average price group; its owner and treatment follow the averaging rules or the client agreement and remain an open question for Finance and Operations. | |
| **[Standing Allocation Instruction](concepts.md#standing-allocation-instruction)** *(level 2)* | Allocation instruction agreed in advance with a client for a defined scope of activity (such as contract, account or strategy), with an effective period, applied to executions in that scope unless replaced by a specific instruction. Its place as a kind of Allocation Instruction depends on the sponsor accepting the widened Allocation Instruction definition. | |
| **[Allocation Line](concepts.md#allocation-line)** *(level 2)* | One part of an allocation that assigns a stated quantity, at a stated price, from an identified execution or average price group to one target account, with its route (internal, or give-up to a named clearing member). An allocation line routed to another clearing member gives rise to exactly one give-up line, whose status is tracked on the give-up page. The allocation line's own status covers allocation states only. When the target account is carried by another clearing member, it is designated by an Account Identifier (give-up reference). | |
| **[Third-party Executing Member](concepts.md#third-party-executing-member)** *(level 2)* | Role of a trading venue member that enters orders on the venue for an executing broker that is not a member of that venue, under an agreement between them and a Third-party Execution Arrangement; the executing broker remains responsible to its client and owes the member brokerage (brokerage paid, distinct from executing brokerage received). The wider case of a member that chooses not to use its own membership is a sponsor decision. | |

## 3. Open questions on meaning

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### Trade, transaction and execution (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Establish identity and lineage; Transaction is provisional and must not collapse all lifecycle events.

**Proposed answer:** Three identity levels linked by directional lineage, never by a fixed cardinality: the execution event, the trade, and successor trades after clearing. Each level carries its own identifiers (for example venue trade IDs, UTI ISO 23897). The MiFIR "transaction" (Art. 26) is a regulatory reporting unit and an ambiguous alias, never Transaction, which stays deferred. Confidence high. [01, 03, 06, 07, 08]

<sub>reference LD-A0008</sub>

### Successful give-up (Decided 2026-11-01 (sponsor; see decision log))

**To decide:** Define completion, partial acceptance, rejection, correction and current versus historical status.

**Proposed answer:** States per give-up line: pending, accepted (in full or in part), rejected, lapsed (not taken up by the cut-off, so the trade stays with the giving member), cancelled, reversed where permitted, and unknown (no record), which is not "pending". Proposed definition of complete: the full allocated quantity is registered to the receiving clearing member's CCP account, as evidenced by the CCP record; partial take-ups are reported separately and both member acceptance time and registration time are kept. Where the take-up is made in the CCP's system the two coincide. Current versus historical follows the open question “Current, as of, as known at”. Confidence high on the pattern, medium on state names. Firm decision: definition of complete and source of record. **Decided 2026-11-01: the proposed definition is accepted and the CCP record is authoritative; discrepancies with internal records are reported as breaks** (see the [decision log](decision-log.md)). [01, 02, 07]

<sub>reference LD-A0010</sub>

### Product, instrument and OTC contract (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Validate identity boundaries for listed series and bespoke OTC agreements.

**Proposed answer:** Proposed (implemented in the Draft definitions of Product Family, Instrument and Contract Specification): Product Family is a classification (CFI ISO 10962 and the UPI ISO 4914 are classification and product identifiers); Contract Specification is a venue's versioned listing template; Instrument is a standardised, counterparty-independent specification at the level at which it is listed or assigned an instrument identifier (a listed series; an OTC ISIN where assigned, with the Trade still carrying full terms); a bespoke OTC contract is a Trade, not an Instrument. Confidence high. Reference Data and OTC Product to confirm the meaning change of Instrument. [01, 03, 07, 08]

<sub>reference LD-A0016</sub>

### Fill (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Venue-facing fill versus client-facing fill versus allocation line versus execution-report message; counts differ.

**Proposed answer:** Contextual alias of Execution from an order's perspective; a broker's venue-facing and client-facing executions are separate Executions linked through the order hierarchy, and allocation lines are not fills. Confidence high. [01, 08]

<sub>reference LD-A0024</sub>

### The trade (clearing tier) (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Pre-clearing (executed) trade versus CCP-facing trade versus member-client trade; which tier, identifier and counterparty view a question uses.

**Proposed answer:** State the tier: pre-clearing trade, CCP-facing trade, member–client trade (principal model). Successor trades are linked by and may carry new identifiers. Confidence high. [03]

<sub>reference LD-A0025</sub>

### OTC and listed (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Regulatory "OTC" (derivatives not executed on a regulated market or equivalent) versus off-venue versus not exchange-listed; cleared is a separate dimension.

**Proposed answer:** Under EMIR Art. 2(7) a derivative not executed on a regulated market or an equivalent third-country market is OTC, so MTF and OTF trades count as OTC; venue type is a Regulatory Classification of Trading Venue. Scope modules by listing and clearing structure, not by that label. Confidence high. [06, 02, 03]

<sub>reference LD-A0032</sub>

### Volume (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Per side versus round turn versus trades versus notional; single- or double-counted; executed versus cleared; published versus firm data.

**Proposed answer:** State unit (contract side, round turn, trade, notional), counting basis (single or double), stage (executed or cleared) and source (published or firm). Confidence high. [05, 01]

<sub>reference LD-A0033</sub>

### Executing broker, firm, entity (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Client-facing executing broker versus trading venue member entering the order versus regulatory executing entity in transaction reports versus giving clearing member.

**Proposed answer:** Executing Broker is the client-facing service role; the Trading Venue Member enters the order in its own name; the executing entity of a transaction report is a regulatory reporting role; the giving clearing member is. One party may hold several of these roles; never infer one from another. Confidence high. [01]
