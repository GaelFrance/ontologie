### Usage notes and required context

- **LD-C0025 Lifecycle Event**
  - Note: Carries occurrence time, effective time and recording time, plus business dates by role. Corrections and cancellations reference the original; nothing is deleted. Event kinds are a taxonomy facet. [07, 03]
- **LD-C0028 Settlement Obligation**
  - Note: Purpose facet includes price alignment (never part of VM) and cash or physical settlement. [04, 02]
- **LD-C0030 Operational Exception**
  - Note: Type State kept. Exceptions are usually handled as cases with owner, priority and lifecycle; the case is a record of handling the exception. A break or a rejected give-up may raise one but is not one (LD-A0034). [07]

## 3. Ambiguities and proposed answers

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### LD-A0002 — Fee, revenue and cash received (Open, narrowed 2026-09-20, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate charges, management revenue and cash settlement; define timing, gross/net, pass-through treatment and the boundary with contribution after costs.

**Proposed answer:** A charge accrues at its chargeable event (LD-R0078), is then invoiced or debited and finally settled (LD-C0029); amount certainty (estimated or final) and billing stage are facets of LD-C0031. Collateral income is a separate stream whose inclusion in clearing revenue is undecided. Gross or net treatment of pass-through charges is a Finance decision; accounting principal-versus-agent guidance is only an analogue. Confidence high on stages, medium on treatment. [05, 04, 02]

### LD-A0008 — Trade, transaction and execution (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Establish identity and lineage; Transaction is provisional and must not collapse all lifecycle events.

**Proposed answer:** Three identity levels linked by directional lineage, never by a fixed cardinality: the execution event, the trade, and successor trades after clearing (LD-R0034). Each level carries its own identifiers (for example venue trade IDs, UTI ISO 23897). The MiFIR "transaction" (Art. 26) is a regulatory reporting unit and an ambiguous alias, never LD-C0019, which stays deferred. Confidence high. [01, 03, 06, 07, 08]

### LD-A0012 — Variation margin (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify collateral versus settlement treatment under the governing arrangement.

**Proposed answer:** Settled-to-market: the daily payment settles exposure (LD-R0067) and is accompanied by a price alignment amount. Collateralised-to-market: VM is collateral to be returned, with price alignment interest. The treatment is a versioned fact per clearing service and account type; some OTC CCPs converted some or all accounts to settled-to-market (dates per service to verify). Price alignment is never part of VM. Confidence high on concepts, medium on per-CCP facts. Legal/Finance decision per service for internal reporting. [04, 03, 06]

### LD-A0015 — Business date (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Distinguish trade, clearing, value and accounting dates and calendars/time zones.

**Proposed answer:** Every date has a role (trade date, CCP clearing date, value date, booking date, reporting date, contract key dates such as last trading day or expiry, as-of date), resolved against a named Business Calendar (LD-C0066) and time zone; event timestamps are kept in UTC. Evening or overnight sessions can carry the next day's trade date (venue-specific). Extended trading hours move cut-offs, margin runs and give-up deadlines relative to the clearing date. Confidence high. Firm decision: default role and time zone of "today". [01, 02, 07]

### LD-A0023 — Current, as of, as known at (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Valid time versus record time: valid now and latest non-superseded record, valid at a business date, or known by a timestamp.

**Proposed answer:** Current = valid now and latest non-superseded record from the stated source; as of D = valid at business date D; as known at T = only records received by T. Every answer states source, as-of date and freshness. See the time convention in [modelling guidelines](../docs/modelling-guidelines.md). Confidence high. Firm decision: whether "as known at" history is needed. [07, 08, 06]

### LD-A0034 — Break, exception, reject, fail (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Reconciliation break versus operational exception versus rejected or lapsed give-up versus settlement fail versus technology incident.

**Proposed answer:** A reconciliation break says records disagree, not which is wrong; an operational exception (LD-C0030) is a situation requiring handling and may be raised by a break; a rejected or lapsed give-up is an event and may raise an exception but is not a trade failure; a settlement fail concerns LD-C0028; a technology incident is out of scope. Confidence high. [07, 02]
