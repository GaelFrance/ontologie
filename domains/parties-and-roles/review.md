## 1. Concepts you approve

| Concept | Definition | Your verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **[Party](concepts.md#party)** | Person or organisation that can be identified and can act, or hold rights and obligations, in a business relationship: a natural person, a legal entity, or a unit without its own legal personality that is named separately in agreements or records (for example a branch or an investment fund). The firm's internal teams, desks and systems are not parties. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[Legal Entity](concepts.md#legal-entity)** | Organisation, fund or other arrangement recognised under an applicable legal or regulatory framework as a distinct legal person, able to hold rights and obligations or to be identified in its own name (for example a company, a partnership, a public body or an investment fund); usually identified by a Legal Entity Identifier. A branch is part of its legal entity, not a separate one, even when it holds its own identifier. | |
| **[Party Role](concepts.md#party-role)** | The holding of one function, such as client, executing broker or clearing member, by one party toward identified other parties, within a service and, where one exists, under an agreement, for a stated period. Client, Broker, Clearing Member and the other role names are each a kind of Party Role. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[Client](concepts.md#client)** | Role held by a party that receives a service from an identified provider under an agreement with that provider (the contractual client). The party that instructs on its behalf, the party that bears the economic result and the Commercial Group it belongs to are recorded separately and are not the client by default. | |
| **[Broker](concepts.md#broker)** | Role of a party that provides execution of orders, or clearing or carrying of trades and positions, as a service to clients under an identified agreement, whether it acts as agent or as principal toward them. Executing Broker and Clearing Broker are its kinds. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[Commercial Group](concepts.md#commercial-group)** | Grouping of parties that an identified provider manages as one commercial relationship over a stated period, for coverage, pricing and performance views; the provider sets its membership, which need not follow legal ownership. It carries no legal or regulatory status and is distinct from ownership groups and regulatory consolidation groups. | |
| **[Account](concepts.md#account)** | Record-keeping unit, opened and maintained by an identified party for an identified holder and a stated function, in which trades, positions, cash, collateral or charges are recorded and attributed; it carries identifiers issued by the maintaining party, a status and an effective period. The firm's record of an account maintained by another party (for example a settlement account at a settlement bank) refers to that real account; an identifier alone, such as a give-up reference, is not an account. | |
| **[Natural Person](concepts.md#natural-person)** *(level 2)* | Party that is an individual human being, acting for itself or for another party. | |
| **[Branch](concepts.md#branch)** *(level 2)* | Establishment of a legal entity, located in a jurisdiction and without legal personality of its own, through which the legal entity conducts business; it may be named in agreements, memberships and records and may hold its own identifiers, but the rights and obligations remain those of the legal entity. | |
| **[Investment Fund](concepts.md#investment-fund)** *(level 2)* | Collective investment arrangement, or a separately identified sub-fund or compartment of one, that pools investors' money under a stated investment policy and is managed by an investment manager; it may or may not be a legal entity, and it is usually the contractual client for its own derivatives business. | |
| **[Authorised Representative](concepts.md#authorised-representative)** *(level 2)* | Role of a party authorised by a client, for a stated scope and period, to give orders, allocations or other instructions on the client's behalf toward an identified provider; acts of the representative bind the client within that scope. | |
| **[Investment Manager](concepts.md#investment-manager)** *(level 2)* | Role of a party that manages the assets of one or more funds or mandates under a management agreement and, by that mandate, places orders and allocations for them with brokers; it is usually not the contractual client for the funds' derivatives business, though it can be a client in its own name for other services. | |
| **[Economic Beneficiary](concepts.md#economic-beneficiary)** *(level 2)* | Role of a party that bears the economic gains and losses of positions or assets recorded in an account when it differs from the account holder, as asserted by a stated source for a stated period. | |
| **[Indirect Client](concepts.md#indirect-client)** *(level 2)* | Client role held by a party that receives clearing services from a client of a clearing member (or from a further party in the chain), not from the clearing member itself, within an identified clearing service. | |
| **[Account Identifier](concepts.md#account-identifier)** *(level 2)* | Code issued by an identified party, under its own scheme, that designates one account for a stated use and period; it is unique only within that scheme, an account can carry several, and a code used to designate another firm's account (such as a give-up reference) identifies that account without making it an account of the sender. | |
| **[Parent Entity](concepts.md#parent-entity)** *(level 2)* | Role of a legal entity that owns or controls another legal entity on a stated basis (accounting consolidation, regulatory consolidation or control), either directly or as the ultimate parent, as asserted by a stated source for a stated period. | |
| **[Contracting Entity](concepts.md#contracting-entity)** *(level 2)* | Role of the provider's legal entity that is party to the agreement with a client for a service and is the provider in that client relationship. It is one candidate observing entity for revenue; the observing entity is stated by the Management Reporting Basis. | |
| **[Intra-group Client](concepts.md#intra-group-client)** *(level 2)* | Client role held by a party that belongs to the same ownership group as the provider's contracting entity, on a stated ownership basis (accounting consolidation, regulatory consolidation or control). Revenue elimination follows the perimeter of the Management Reporting Basis of the observing entity. | |
| **[Non-Clearing Member](concepts.md#non-clearing-member)** *(level 2)* | Client role, within a clearing service, held by a trading venue member that is not a clearing member of that service and has its venue trades cleared by a general clearing member, where the CCP's rules recognise that status. | |
| **[Sponsored Clearing Participant](concepts.md#sponsored-clearing-participant)** *(level 2)* | Role of a party admitted directly to a clearing service under a CCP's sponsored or direct-client model, with trades registered in its own name, sponsored by a Sponsoring Clearing Member under a Sponsored Clearing Arrangement. It normally also holds the Client role toward its sponsor. It holds the Clearing Member role as well only where that CCP's rules call it a member (to verify per rulebook). | |
| **[Client Regulatory Category](concepts.md#client-regulatory-category)** *(level 2)* | Regulatory classification of a client by a provider for an investment or clearing service under a named regime, attached to the client role toward that provider (not to the legal entity in general), with its basis (per se, on request, by agreement), evidence and period; the same entity can be categorised differently by different providers or for different services. | |
| **[Counterparty Regulatory Status](concepts.md#counterparty-regulatory-status)** *(level 2)* | Regulatory classification of a legal entity under a named regime that determines its own obligations as a counterparty (for example whether it is subject to a clearing obligation or margin rules), possibly per asset class, with basis (self-declaration, calculation, authority decision), evidence and period; it attaches to the legal entity, never to a client role or a commercial group. | |
| **[Client Account](concepts.md#client-account)** *(level 2)* | Account opened and maintained by a provider in its own books for one client and a stated function (for example execution, clearing, collateral or cash), linked by an explicit, dated mapping to the CCP account, or to the CCP position account where the CCP keeps one, in which the activity is held at each clearing service; for indirect clients, through the account of the client that clears for them. | |
| **[Market Operator](concepts.md#market-operator)** *(level 2)* | Role of a legal entity that operates one or more trading venues under their rules, for a stated period: it issues the venue's rulebook and fee schedule and is the creditor of exchange fees. The exchange group it belongs to is a Parent Entity relation, not this role. | |
| **[Settlement or Custody Agent](concepts.md#settlement-or-custody-agent)** *(level 2)* | Role of a party through which an account holder settles cash or holds, delivers or pledges assets, held toward that account holder for an identified account and a currency or asset: a settlement bank (including a concentration bank or payment agent), a custodian or a triparty agent. | |

## 3. Open questions on meaning

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### Client (Open, narrowed 2026-09-20, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate contractual role, legal entity, commercial group and beneficial owner.

**Proposed answer:** Entity-level statuses attach to the Legal Entity, relationship-level statuses (such as MiFID client category) to the role assignment (Party Role); revenue rolls up from the contractual client of each service relationship to a Commercial Group. Regulatory texts use "client" differently: EMIR Art. 2(15) is a clearing relationship with a clearing member, the MiFID II client (Art. 4(1)(9)) is broader, and the US "customer" is the FCM relationship. Confidence high. [06, 05]

<sub>reference LD-A0006</sub>

### Account (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify function, maintaining party, holder, beneficiary and account-to-account relationships.

**Proposed answer:** Three levels: the CCP Account of each clearing service; the member's internal client account; execution-side and give-up references, which are identifiers rather than accounts. Levels are related only by explicit, time-bounded. Segregation regime and regulatory account statuses are separate, time-bounded dimensions. Confidence high. [02, 07, 06]

<sub>reference LD-A0007</sub>

### Position (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify gross/net, quantity/notional/exposure, account, instrument, CCP and as-of basis.

**Proposed answer:** Key: account (CCP or internal) × instrument × clearing service × side or net basis, as of a business date and cycle, with the asserting source (CCP statement or internal). House accounts are generally net; client omnibus accounts are net or gross by account type. CCP-reported and internal positions are two records of one position; a difference is a break. OTC positions are sets of open cleared trades measured by count, notional or risk sensitivity. Confidence high. Firm decision: default basis and source, or return both labelled. [02, 03, 07]

<sub>reference LD-A0013</sub>

### Revenue by client (Open, narrowed 2026-09-20 and 2026-11-01, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Use the approved management perspective; specify client attribution, consolidation, internal eliminations, measurement period, reporting currency and any separately defined cost deductions.

**Proposed answer:** Sponsor decision 2026-11-01: execution-only, clearing-only and execute-and-clear are all offered; give-up and give-in are both supported; revenue is attributed per service. Proposed: attribute to the contractual client of each service relationship, show the service mix, roll up to Commercial Group, flag intra-group clients for elimination; mandatory context is observing entity, management basis, period and currency. Open: cost perimeter for Contribution, pass-through and collateral income treatment. [05, 01, 04]

<sub>reference LD-A0014</sub>

### Clearing broker, clearing member, FCM (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Service role toward the client versus admission at a CCP versus a regulatory registration (FCM in the US).

**Proposed answer:** Clearing Broker is the service role toward the client and may exist without membership of the clearing service; Clearing Member is admission at the CCP; FCM is a US regulatory registration held by a legal entity, recorded as a Regulatory Classification, and only a contextual alias. Confidence high. [02, 03, 05, 06, 08]

<sub>reference LD-A0019</sub>

### Omnibus, house and client (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** EMIR omnibus (net or gross) versus US customer omnibus versus indirect omnibus versus internal pooled account; CCP account type versus economic relationship of affiliates.

**Proposed answer:** EMIR omnibus and individual client segregation (Art. 39(2) and (3), to verify), US futures-customer omnibus, indirect-client omnibus account types (Delegated Regulations (EU) 2017/2154 and 2017/2155, to verify) and internal pooled accounts are different things; the CCP account type does not determine the economic relationship (affiliates may be cleared as clients or in the house account). Confidence high. [02, 04, 03]

<sub>reference LD-A0028</sub>

### Group (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Regulatory consolidation group versus commercial group versus CCP affiliate versus legal parent in public entity-relationship data.

**Proposed answer:** A regulatory consolidation group (used in some threshold calculations), a Commercial Group, a CCP's "affiliate" and a legal parent in public entity-relationship data are different; regulatory statuses never attach to a commercial group. Confidence high. [06, 05, 07]
