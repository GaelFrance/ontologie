# Parties, roles, clients and accounts

> Generated by `tools/build_domain_views.py`; edit `overview.md`, `company-questions.md` or the registers instead.

Every question the business asks starts with "who": who is the client, who executed the order, who clears it, who owes the fee, whose position is this? This domain gives one shared answer. It describes the people and organisations involved (parties), the functions they hold toward each other (roles), how a provider groups its clients commercially, how clients are classified, and the accounts in which their activity is recorded. If this layer is wrong, revenue goes to the wrong client or a give-up lands in the wrong account.

## Concepts at a glance

- **[Party](concepts.md#party)** — Anyone who can act or hold rights in a business relationship: a person, a company, a branch or a fund.
  - **[Legal Entity](concepts.md#legal-entity)** — A party the law treats as a distinct person, able to sign contracts and owe money in its own name.
    - **[Branch](concepts.md#branch)** — An office of a company in another place that acts for the company but is not a separate company.
  - **[Natural Person](concepts.md#natural-person)** — A party that is a person rather than an organisation.
  - **[Investment Fund](concepts.md#investment-fund)** — A pool of investors' money run by a manager, which trades and clears derivatives in its own name.
- **[Party Role](concepts.md#party-role)** — One party doing one job toward someone else, in a given service, for a given period.
  - **[Client](concepts.md#client)** — The party that has signed up to receive a service from a provider.
    - **[Indirect Client](concepts.md#indirect-client)** — The client of a client, in a clearing chain.
    - **[Intra-group Client](concepts.md#intra-group-client)** — A client that belongs to the same ownership group as the provider.
    - **[Non-Clearing Member](concepts.md#non-clearing-member)** — A venue member that trades on the exchange but has another firm clear its trades.
  - **[Broker](concepts.md#broker)** — A firm that executes orders or clears trades for its clients as a service.
  - **[Authorised Representative](concepts.md#authorised-representative)** — Someone allowed to instruct the broker on a client's behalf.
    - **[Investment Manager](concepts.md#investment-manager)** — The firm that runs funds' money and trades for them.
  - **[Economic Beneficiary](concepts.md#economic-beneficiary)** — The party that ultimately gains or loses on the positions, when it is not the account holder.
  - **[Parent Entity](concepts.md#parent-entity)** — The company that owns or controls another company, directly or at the top of the chain.
  - **[Contracting Entity](concepts.md#contracting-entity)** — Which of a provider's legal entities has the contract with the client for a given service.
  - **[Sponsored Clearing Participant](concepts.md#sponsored-clearing-participant)** — A client that becomes a direct participant at a CCP with a clearing member as its sponsor.
  - **[Market Operator](concepts.md#market-operator)** — The company that runs a trading venue.
  - **[Settlement or Custody Agent](concepts.md#settlement-or-custody-agent)** — The bank, custodian or triparty agent a party uses to pay, deliver or hold collateral.
- **[Commercial Group](concepts.md#commercial-group)** — The set of clients a provider treats as one relationship for sales, pricing and revenue.
- **[Account](concepts.md#account)** — A place in someone's books where a holder's trades, positions, cash or charges are recorded for a stated purpose.
  - **[Client Account](concepts.md#client-account)** — The account a broker keeps in its own books for one client.
  - **[Account Identifier](concepts.md#account-identifier)** — A code that points to an account, unique only in the books of whoever issued it.
- **[Client Regulatory Category](concepts.md#client-regulatory-category)** — How a provider has categorised a client under conduct rules, for example professional client or eligible counterparty.
- **[Counterparty Regulatory Status](concepts.md#counterparty-regulatory-status)** — The regulatory status a company has in its own right, which decides rules such as whether it must clear.
