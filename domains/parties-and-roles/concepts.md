### Party

*Anyone who can act or hold rights in a business relationship: a person, a company, a branch or a fund.*

**Definition.** Identifiable actor that can participate in a business relationship.

**Example.** Fund F, its investment manager M, Clearing Broker Y and CCP Z are all parties.

**Not to be confused with.** Party Role: the party is who it is; the role is what it does toward someone, for a while.

**Breaks down into:** [Legal Entity](#legal-entity) (kind of), [Natural Person](#natural-person) (kind of), [Investment Fund](#investment-fund) (kind of)

**Connected to:**

- *has role* [Party Role](#party-role) — Use a contextual, time-bounded role assignment; not an unqualified permanent property.
- [Fee Charge](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#fee-charge) *has debtor* Party — Debtor may differ from actual payer.
- [Fee Charge](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#fee-charge) *has creditor* Party — Creditor may differ from collector or ultimate beneficiary.
- [Rule Provision](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#rule-provision) *issued by* Party — Regulator, CCP, venue operator or the firm.
- *member of* [Commercial Group](#commercial-group) — Time-bound commercial grouping; never used for regulatory threshold or consolidation calculations (the open question “Group”).
- [Account Identifier](#account-identifier) *is issued by* Party
- [Rulebook](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#rulebook) *is issued by* Party

### Legal Entity

*A party the law treats as a distinct person, able to sign contracts and owe money in its own name.*

**Definition.** Organisation, fund or other arrangement recognised under an applicable legal or regulatory framework as a distinct legal person, able to hold rights and obligations or to be identified in its own name (for example a company, a partnership, a public body or an investment fund); usually identified by a Legal Entity Identifier. A branch is part of its legal entity, not a separate one, even when it holds its own identifier.

**Example.** Client A Ltd, identified by its LEI, signs a clearing agreement with Clearing Broker Y.

**Not to be confused with.** Commercial Group: a legal entity is a legal person; a commercial group is the firm's own way of grouping clients.

**Kind of:** [Party](#party)

**Breaks down into:** [Branch](#branch) (part of)

**Words people use:** “market operator” (MiFID II); “exchange” (Any)

**Note.** Identified by LEI (ISO 17442); certain international branches can also hold an LEI (to verify). An LEI never identifies an account, desk or commercial group, and public parent data describe accounting consolidation, not a commercial group. Holder of entity-level regulatory classifications. [06, 07]

**Connected to:**

- [Trading Venue](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#trading-venue) *operated by* Legal Entity — The venue is distinct from its legal operator; identify the venue by ISO 10383 MIC (operating versus segment MIC, to verify per use).
- [Parent Entity](#parent-entity) *is held by and points to another* Legal Entity
- [Counterparty Regulatory Status](#counterparty-regulatory-status) *classifies* Legal Entity
- [Identifier Scheme](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#identifier-scheme) *identifies through lei* Legal Entity
- [Regulatory Authorisation](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#regulatory-authorisation) *is held by* Legal Entity

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Client” (open, narrowed 2026-09-20, answer proposed); “Group” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Branch

*An office of a company in another place that acts for the company but is not a separate company.*

**Definition.** Establishment of a legal entity, located in a jurisdiction and without legal personality of its own, through which the legal entity conducts business; it may be named in agreements, memberships and records and may hold its own identifiers, but the rights and obligations remain those of the legal entity.

**Example.** Bank B's London branch holds Bank B's membership of Venue V; Bank B remains legally bound.

**Not to be confused with.** Subsidiary: a subsidiary is a separate legal entity owned by a parent; a branch is part of the same legal entity.

**Part of:** [Legal Entity](#legal-entity)

**Words people use:** “establishment” (regulatory usage); “international branch” (LEI usage)

**Before using it, know:** Which legal entity it belongs to, the jurisdiction in which it is established, and whether a role or membership is held by the legal entity acting through the branch.

**Connected to:**

- *may bring the business under a further* [Regulatory Regime](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#regulatory-regime)

### Natural Person

*A party that is a person rather than an organisation.*

**Definition.** Party that is an individual human being, acting for itself or for another party.

**Example.** Trader T, an employee of Investment Manager M, is authorised to place orders for Fund F.

**Not to be confused with.** Authorised Representative: being a person says what the party is; being authorised to instruct is a role it holds.

**Kind of:** [Party](#party)

**Words people use:** “individual” (any); “private client” (when holding a client role, wealth management usage)

**Before using it, know:** Personal data rules apply: an agent must not expose personal identifiers; only the role and the party type are needed for most questions.

**Connected to:**

- *can hold the role of* [Authorised Representative](#authorised-representative)
- *can hold the role of* [Client](#client)

<sub>Entity · level 2 · Draft · reference LD-C0075 · approved by the Reference & Client Data</sub>

### Investment Fund

*A pool of investors' money run by a manager, which trades and clears derivatives in its own name.*

**Definition.** Collective investment arrangement, or a separately identified sub-fund or compartment of one, that pools investors' money under a stated investment policy and is managed by an investment manager; it may or may not be a legal entity, and it is usually the contractual client for its own derivatives business.

**Example.** Sub-fund F2 of umbrella fund F has its own LEI, its own client accounts and its own clearing agreement, signed for it by Investment Manager M.

**Not to be confused with.** Investment Manager: the fund owns the positions and is the client; the manager instructs for it.

**Kind of:** [Party](#party)

**Values (draft list):** umbrella fund; sub-fund or compartment; standalone fund

**Words people use:** “fund” (any); “sub-fund” (umbrella structures); “compartment” (EU fund usage)

**Before using it, know:** Whether the question concerns the umbrella or a sub-fund, and which investment manager acts for it in which period.

**Connected to:**

- *is managed by* [Investment Manager](#investment-manager)
- *usually holds the role of* [Client](#client)

### Party Role

*One party doing one job toward someone else, in a given service, for a given period.*

**Definition.** The holding of one function, such as client, executing broker or clearing member, by one party toward identified other parties, within a service and, where one exists, under an agreement, for a stated period. Client, Broker, Clearing Member and the other role names are each a kind of Party Role. — *meaning changed in 2026-11, accepted as Draft*

**Example.** Bank B holds the clearing member role at CCP Z for listed futures and, separately, the client role toward Executing Broker X.

**Not to be confused with.** Market Access Arrangement: the arrangement is the set of agreements that grants a role; the role is the function held.

**Breaks down into:** [Client](#client) (kind of), [Broker](#broker) (kind of), [Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-member) (kind of), [Central Counterparty](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#central-counterparty) (kind of), [Trading Venue Member](../execution/concepts.md#trading-venue-member) (kind of), [Authorised Representative](#authorised-representative) (kind of), [Economic Beneficiary](#economic-beneficiary) (kind of), [Parent Entity](#parent-entity) (kind of), [Contracting Entity](#contracting-entity) (kind of), [Sponsored Clearing Participant](#sponsored-clearing-participant) (kind of), [Market Operator](#market-operator) (kind of), [Settlement or Custody Agent](#settlement-or-custody-agent) (kind of), [Giving Clearing Member](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#giving-clearing-member) (kind of), [Receiving Clearing Member](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#receiving-clearing-member) (kind of), [Back-up Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#back-up-clearing-member) (kind of), [Sponsoring Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#sponsoring-clearing-member) (kind of), [Margin Provider](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#margin-provider) (kind of), [Margin Taker](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#margin-taker) (kind of), [Fee Collector](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#fee-collector) (kind of), [Regulatory Authority](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#regulatory-authority) (kind of), [Regulatory Reporting Role](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#regulatory-reporting-role) (kind of)

**Note.** Read as the role assignment (party-in-role); there is no separate role-assignment concept. Regulatory relationship statuses attach here. Alignment to verify: FIBO party-in-role pattern, CDM party role. [08, 06]

**Connected to:**

- [Party](#party) *has role* Party Role — Use a contextual, time-bounded role assignment; not an unqualified permanent property.
- *governed by* [Agreement](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#agreement) — Applicable to the role assignment where a governing agreement exists.
- [Market Access Arrangement](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#market-access-arrangement) *confers* Party Role — For example a Trading Venue Member, Clearing Member or Client role assignment; the arrangement and the resulting role are distinct.
- [Market Access Arrangement](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#market-access-arrangement) *intermediated by* Party Role — The intermediary's role assignment (venue member, clearing member or clearing broker); zero or one.
- *within service* [Clearing Service](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-service), [Service Offering](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#service-offering) — Zero or one; for example a Client role assignment within a Service Offering, a Clearing Member role within a Clearing Service.
- [Regulatory Obligation](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#regulatory-obligation) *is held by parties in* Party Role

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Client” (open, narrowed 2026-09-20, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Client

*The party that has signed up to receive a service from a provider.*

**Definition.** Role held by a party that receives a service from an identified provider under an agreement with that provider (the contractual client). The party that instructs on its behalf, the party that bears the economic result and the Commercial Group it belongs to are recorded separately and are not the client by default.

**Example.** Fund F is the client of Clearing Broker Y for clearing at CCP Z, even though Investment Manager M sends its orders.

**Not to be confused with.** Commercial Group: revenue rolls up to the group, but the contract is with the client.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](#party-role)

**Breaks down into:** [Indirect Client](#indirect-client) (kind of), [Intra-group Client](#intra-group-client) (kind of), [Non-Clearing Member](#non-clearing-member) (kind of)

**Words people use:** “EMIR client” (EMIR Art. 2(15)); “MiFID client” (MiFID II Art. 4(1)(9)); “CFTC customer” (US)

**Note.** Sponsor definition unchanged. Linked to a Service Offering through. Client segment is a Participants taxonomy axis. [05, 06]

**Connected to:**

- *submits* [Order](../execution/concepts.md#order) — Distinguish contractual client, representative and technical sender.
- [Natural Person](#natural-person) *can hold the role of* Client
- [Investment Fund](#investment-fund) *usually holds the role of* Client
- [Authorised Representative](#authorised-representative) *acts for* Client
- [Contracting Entity](#contracting-entity) *is the provider toward* Client
- [Sponsored Clearing Participant](#sponsored-clearing-participant) *normally also holds toward its sponsor* Client
- [Client Regulatory Category](#client-regulatory-category) *classifies* Client
- [Client Account](#client-account) *is held for* Client
- [Offering Eligibility Criterion](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#offering-eligibility-criterion) *is tested against* Client
- [Client Order](../execution/concepts.md#client-order) *is given by* Client
- [Client Back-to-Back Trade](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#client-back-to-back-trade) *has as the other party* Client
- [Segregation Election](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#segregation-election) *is made by* Client
- [Back-up Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#back-up-clearing-member) *is named by* Client
- [Client Margin Uplift](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#client-margin-uplift) *applies to* Client
- [Client Pricing Terms](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#client-pricing-terms) *is agreed with* Client

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Client” (open, narrowed 2026-09-20, answer proposed); “Revenue by client” (open, narrowed 2026-09-20 and 2026-11-01, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Indirect Client

*The client of a client, in a clearing chain.*

**Definition.** Client role held by a party that receives clearing services from a client of a clearing member (or from a further party in the chain), not from the clearing member itself, within an identified clearing service.

**Example.** Fund F clears through Broker Q, which is itself a client of Clearing Member Y at CCP Z; Fund F is an indirect client.

**Not to be confused with.** Economic Beneficiary: an indirect client has a clearing relationship in the chain; a beneficiary may only bear the result.

**Kind of:** [Client](#client)

**Words people use:** “indirect client” (EMIR usage)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service, the client that provides its clearing, the regime and the account structure chosen.

**Connected to:**

- [Economic Beneficiary](#economic-beneficiary) *is often an* Indirect Client
- *is cleared within* [Clearing Service](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-service)
- *may be recorded in indirect client accounts of* [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account)
- *reaches clearing through* [Market Access Arrangement](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#market-access-arrangement)
- [Indirect Clearing Arrangement](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#indirect-clearing-arrangement) *confers* Indirect Client

### Intra-group Client

*A client that belongs to the same ownership group as the provider.*

**Definition.** Client role held by a party that belongs to the same ownership group as the provider's contracting entity, on a stated ownership basis (accounting consolidation, regulatory consolidation or control). Revenue elimination follows the perimeter of the Management Reporting Basis of the observing entity.

**Example.** Broker B's asset management subsidiary clears through Broker B's clearing entity and is flagged as an intra-group client.

**Not to be confused with.** Commercial Group: the provider's own grouping. And eligibility for a regulatory intragroup exemption, which is a Regulatory Relief with its own group test and conditions.

**Kind of:** [Client](#client)

**Words people use:** “affiliate client” (any); “internal client” (ambiguous: may mean another desk of the same entity)

**Before using it, know:** Ownership basis and period; observing contracting entity; regime and CCP rules on affiliates.

**Connected to:**

- *is determined through* [Parent Entity](#parent-entity)
- *may be recorded in house or client accounts of* [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account)
- *is flagged for elimination in* [Revenue](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#revenue)

### Non-Clearing Member

*A venue member that trades on the exchange but has another firm clear its trades.*

**Definition.** Client role, within a clearing service, held by a trading venue member that is not a clearing member of that service and has its venue trades cleared by a general clearing member, where the CCP's rules recognise that status.

**Example.** Firm N is a member of Venue V and clears all its trades at CCP Z through General Clearing Member Y.

**Not to be confused with.** A party that reaches a venue through a Third-party Executing Member under a Third-Party Execution Arrangement is not a venue member; a non-clearing member is a venue member whose trades are cleared by a general clearing member.

**Kind of:** [Client](#client)

**Words people use:** “NCM” (listed markets); “trading member without clearing rights” (venue usage)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service and the CCP's rules: whether the status is formally recognised there or the firm is simply a client.

**Connected to:**

- *is held together with* [Trading Venue Member](../execution/concepts.md#trading-venue-member)
- *clears through a general* [Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-member)
- *is conferred by* [Market Access Arrangement](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#market-access-arrangement)
- [Non-Clearing Member Arrangement](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#non-clearing-member-arrangement) *confers* Non-Clearing Member

### Broker

*A firm that executes orders or clears trades for its clients as a service.*

**Definition.** Role of a party that acts on behalf of others to execute orders and/or to clear or carry their trades under an identified agreement.

**Example.** Executing Broker X executes Client A's order on Venue V; Clearing Broker Y clears the resulting trade.

**Not to be confused with.** Clearing Member: a broker serves clients; a clearing member is admitted to a CCP, and the two need not be the same firm.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](#party-role)

**Breaks down into:** [Executing Broker](../execution/concepts.md#executing-broker) (kind of), [Clearing Broker](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-broker) (kind of)

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Clearing broker, clearing member, FCM” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Authorised Representative

*Someone allowed to instruct the broker on a client's behalf.*

**Definition.** Role of a party authorised by a client, for a stated scope and period, to give orders, allocations or other instructions on the client's behalf toward an identified provider; acts of the representative bind the client within that scope.

**Example.** Trader T and Investment Manager M are both authorised to send orders for Fund F to Executing Broker X; only M may send allocations.

**Not to be confused with.** Client: the representative instructs; the client is bound and owes the fees.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](#party-role)

**Breaks down into:** [Investment Manager](#investment-manager) (kind of)

**Values (draft list):** investment manager; authorised trader; introducing broker or introducer (transmits orders); other agent under power of attorney

**Words people use:** “authorised person” (any); “instructing party” (any); “order originator” (execution usage, ambiguous)

**Before using it, know:** Which client, which provider, which kinds of instruction and which period the authority covers.

**Connected to:**

- [Natural Person](#natural-person) *can hold the role of* Authorised Representative
- *acts for* [Client](#client)
- *sends* [Order](../execution/concepts.md#order)
- *gives* [Allocation Instruction](../execution/concepts.md#allocation-instruction)

### Investment Manager

*The firm that runs funds' money and trades for them.*

**Definition.** Role of a party that manages the assets of one or more funds or mandates under a management agreement and, by that mandate, places orders and allocations for them with brokers; it is usually not the contractual client for the funds' derivatives business, though it can be a client in its own name for other services.

**Example.** Investment Manager M places one order for 300 lots and allocates it across Funds F1, F2 and F3, each a separate client.

**Not to be confused with.** Investment Fund: the fund holds the positions and is the client; the manager decides and instructs.

**Kind of:** [Authorised Representative](#authorised-representative)

**Words people use:** “asset manager” (any); “fund manager” (any); “account manager” (US allocation usage)

**Before using it, know:** Which funds it manages for which period; whether it is also a client in its own name.

**Connected to:**

- *manages* [Investment Fund](#investment-fund)
- *gives* [Allocation Instruction](../execution/concepts.md#allocation-instruction)
- *often anchors* [Commercial Group](#commercial-group)

### Economic Beneficiary

*The party that ultimately gains or loses on the positions, when it is not the account holder.*

**Definition.** Role of a party that bears the economic gains and losses of positions or assets recorded in an account when it differs from the account holder, as asserted by a stated source for a stated period.

**Example.** Broker Q holds an omnibus account with Clearing Broker Y; the economic beneficiaries are Broker Q's own clients.

**Not to be confused with.** Ultimate beneficial owner in know-your-customer checks: that is a person who owns or controls a company, a separate compliance fact.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](#party-role)

**Words people use:** “beneficial owner” (ambiguous: also the know-your-customer meaning); “underlying client” (omnibus usage)

**Before using it, know:** Account concerned, source of the assertion, period; whether the holder discloses beneficiaries at all.

**Connected to:**

- *bears the result of positions in* [Account](#account)
- *is often an* [Indirect Client](#indirect-client)

<sub>Role · level 2 · Draft · reference LD-C0080 · approved by the Reference & Client Data</sub>

### Parent Entity

*The company that owns or controls another company, directly or at the top of the chain.*

**Definition.** Role of a legal entity that owns or controls another legal entity on a stated basis (accounting consolidation, regulatory consolidation or control), either directly or as the ultimate parent, as asserted by a stated source for a stated period.

**Example.** Holding H is the ultimate parent of Client A Ltd and Client A Inc, according to public LEI relationship data.

**Not to be confused with.** Commercial Group: ownership follows legal parents; the commercial group is the firm's own grouping and may differ.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](#party-role)

**Values (draft list):** direct parent; ultimate parent

**Words people use:** “ownership group” (any, for the set of entities under one ultimate parent); “corporate family” (sales usage); “affiliate” (CCP rulebook usage, ambiguous)

**Before using it, know:** Consolidation basis, direct or ultimate, source (public LEI relationship data or internal), effective period.

**Connected to:**

- *is held by and points to another* [Legal Entity](#legal-entity)
- *may inform but never defines* [Commercial Group](#commercial-group)
- *decides whether a client is* [Intra-group Client](#intra-group-client)

### Contracting Entity

*Which of a provider's legal entities has the contract with the client for a given service.*

**Definition.** Role of the provider's legal entity that is party to the agreement with a client for a service and is the provider in that client relationship. It is one candidate observing entity for revenue; the observing entity is stated by the Management Reporting Basis.

**Example.** Client A clears listed futures at a US CCP with Broker B's US entity and at a European CCP with Broker B's European entity: two contracting entities, two client relationships.

**Not to be confused with.** Branch: a branch may service the client, but the contract is with the legal entity.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](#party-role)

**Words people use:** “booking entity” (ambiguous: may mean the entity that records the trade); “provider entity” (any)

**Before using it, know:** Which service and which period; a client may contract with several of the firm's entities for different services.

**Connected to:**

- *is the provider toward* [Client](#client)
- *is party to* [Agreement](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#agreement)
- *provides* [Service Offering](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#service-offering)
- *is the observing entity for* [Revenue](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#revenue)
- [Client Regulatory Category](#client-regulatory-category) *is assigned by* Contracting Entity

<sub>Role · level 2 · Draft · reference LD-C0084 · approved by the Reference & Client Data</sub>

### Sponsored Clearing Participant

*A client that becomes a direct participant at a CCP with a clearing member as its sponsor.*

**Definition.** Role of a party admitted directly to a clearing service under a CCP's sponsored or direct-client model, with trades registered in its own name, sponsored by a Sponsoring Clearing Member under a Sponsored Clearing Arrangement. It normally also holds the Client role toward its sponsor. It holds the Clearing Member role as well only where that CCP's rules call it a member (to verify per rulebook).

**Example.** Pension Fund P is admitted to CCP Z's sponsored model, with Clearing Member Y as sponsor.

**Not to be confused with.** Sponsored access (execution): that is a way to send orders to a venue; this is a status at a CCP.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](#party-role)

**Words people use:** “sponsored member” (CCP usage); “direct client participant” (CCP usage)

**Before using it, know:** CCP, clearing service, sponsor, period; the split of obligations under that CCP's rules.

**Connected to:**

- *is sponsored by* [Sponsoring Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#sponsoring-clearing-member)
- *is admitted under* [Sponsored Clearing Arrangement](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#sponsored-clearing-arrangement)
- *normally also holds toward its sponsor* [Client](#client)

<sub>Role · level 2 · Draft · reference LD-C0087 · approved by the Reference & Client Data</sub>

### Market Operator

*The company that runs a trading venue.*

**Definition.** Role of a legal entity that operates one or more trading venues under their rules, for a stated period: it issues the venue's rulebook and fee schedule and is the creditor of exchange fees. The exchange group it belongs to is a Parent Entity relation, not this role.

**Example.** Operator O runs Venue V and its negotiated-trade segment; Operator O belongs to Exchange Group E.

**Not to be confused with.** Trading Venue: the venue is the trading system; the operator is the company that runs it. 'The exchange' can mean either, or the group.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](#party-role)

**Before using it, know:** Venue or venues operated, period.

**Connected to:**

- *operates* [Trading Venue](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#trading-venue)
- *issues* [Rulebook](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#rulebook)
- *issues* [Published Fee Schedule](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#published-fee-schedule)

<sub>Role · level 2 · Draft · reference LD-C0091 · approved by the Reference & Client Data</sub>

### Settlement or Custody Agent

*The bank, custodian or triparty agent a party uses to pay, deliver or hold collateral.*

**Definition.** Role of a party through which an account holder settles cash or holds, delivers or pledges assets, held toward that account holder for an identified account and a currency or asset: a settlement bank (including a concentration bank or payment agent), a custodian or a triparty agent.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y pays its sterling obligations to CCP Z through Settlement Bank S and holds pledged bonds with Triparty Agent T.

**Not to be confused with.** Clearing Broker: clears trades for a client; a settlement or custody agent only moves or holds cash and assets.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](#party-role)

**Values (draft list):** settlement bank; custodian; triparty agent

**Before using it, know:** Account holder, account, currency or asset, period.

**Connected to:**

- *maintains* [Settlement Account](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#settlement-account)
- *evidences* [Collateral Movement](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#collateral-movement)
- *evidences* [Cash Movement](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#cash-movement)

### Commercial Group

*The set of clients a provider treats as one relationship for sales, pricing and revenue.*

**Definition.** Grouping of parties that an identified provider manages as one commercial relationship over a stated period, for coverage, pricing and performance views; the provider sets its membership, which need not follow legal ownership. It carries no legal or regulatory status and is distinct from ownership groups and regulatory consolidation groups.

**Example.** Broker B groups Investment Manager M and the twenty funds it manages into one commercial group.

**Not to be confused with.** Ownership group: the commercial group is the provider's own choice and can sign nothing; the ownership group follows legal parents.

**Connected to:**

- [Party](#party) *member of* Commercial Group — Time-bound commercial grouping; never used for regulatory threshold or consolidation calculations (the open question “Group”).
- [Investment Manager](#investment-manager) *often anchors* Commercial Group
- [Parent Entity](#parent-entity) *may inform but never defines* Commercial Group
- [Client Pricing Terms](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#client-pricing-terms) *may be agreed at the level of* Commercial Group
- [Management Reporting Basis](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#management-reporting-basis) *rolls revenue up to* Commercial Group

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Client” (open, narrowed 2026-09-20, answer proposed); “Revenue by client” (open, narrowed 2026-09-20 and 2026-11-01, answer proposed); “Group” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Account

*A place in someone's books where a holder's trades, positions, cash or charges are recorded for a stated purpose.*

**Definition.** Record-keeping unit, opened and maintained by an identified party for an identified holder and a stated function, in which trades, positions, cash, collateral or charges are recorded and attributed; it carries identifiers issued by the maintaining party, a status and an effective period. The firm's record of an account maintained by another party (for example a settlement account at a settlement bank) refers to that real account; an identifier alone, such as a give-up reference, is not an account.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y opens a client account for Fund F and records its futures positions there.

**Not to be confused with.** Account identifier: the code (such as a give-up reference) points to an account; it is not the account.

**Breaks down into:** [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account) (kind of), [Client Account](#client-account) (kind of), [Settlement Account](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#settlement-account) (kind of), [Account Identifier](#account-identifier) (part of)

**Note.** Has identifiers per maintaining party, a lifecycle status and an effective period. A give-up reference is an identifier pointing to the receiving member's account, not an account. Regulatory qualifications (for example active-account status) are time-bounded classifications. [07, 06]

**Connected to:**

- [Allocation](../execution/concepts.md#allocation) *targets* Account — Target account does not by itself establish legal ownership.
- [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position) *recorded in* Account — Recording does not assert beneficial ownership.
- *mapped to* [Account](#account) — Explicit, time-bounded correspondence maintained by an identified party (for example internal client accounts to a CCP omnibus account); never by name equality; many-to-one is typical, not universal.
- [Collateral](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#collateral) *held for* Account — Account or segregation unit for which the collateral is held.
- [Economic Beneficiary](#economic-beneficiary) *bears the result of positions in* Account
- [Allocation Line](../execution/concepts.md#allocation-line) *targets* Account
- [Client Omnibus Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#client-omnibus-account) *is mapped from the clients internal accounts* Account
- [CCP Position Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-position-account) *is mapped from internal client accounts* Account

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Account” (open, answer proposed); “Position” (open, answer proposed); “Omnibus, house and client” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Client Account

*The account a broker keeps in its own books for one client.*

**Definition.** Account opened and maintained by a provider in its own books for one client and a stated function (for example execution, clearing, collateral or cash), linked by an explicit, dated mapping to the CCP account, or to the CCP position account where the CCP keeps one, in which the activity is held at each clearing service; for indirect clients, through the account of the client that clears for them.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y keeps client account 'F2-futures' for Sub-fund F2, mapped to its omnibus client account at CCP Z.

**Not to be confused with.** CCP Account: the CCP keeps that one in the clearing member's name; several client accounts often map to one CCP account.

**Kind of:** [Account](#account)

**Values (draft list):** clearing client account; execution client account (records executions and charges, no positions); sub-account (subdivision for allocation or reporting)

**Words people use:** “internal client account” (clearing usage); “customer account” (US usage, ambiguous with the US account class)

**Before using it, know:** Maintaining party, holder, function, status and period; for clearing, the mapped CCP account per clearing service.

**Connected to:**

- *is held for* [Client](#client)
- *is mapped to* [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account)
- *is targeted by* [Allocation](../execution/concepts.md#allocation)
- *records* [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position)

### Account Identifier

*A code that points to an account, unique only in the books of whoever issued it.*

**Definition.** Code issued by an identified party, under its own scheme, that designates one account for a stated use and period; it is unique only within that scheme, an account can carry several, and a code used to designate another firm's account (such as a give-up reference) identifies that account without making it an account of the sender.

**Example.** Executing Broker X tags a trade with give-up reference 'GU-77', which designates Fund F's account at Clearing Broker Y.

**Not to be confused with.** Account: the code is a label pointing to the account; the account is where the activity is recorded.

**Part of:** [Account](#account)

**Values (draft list):** CCP account number; provider's internal account number; give-up reference or mnemonic; venue trading account code; client's own account reference

**Words people use:** “give-up reference” (listed give-up usage); “account code” (any); “mnemonic” (listed markets usage)

**Before using it, know:** The identifier scheme it belongs to (the issuer's scheme), the issuing party, use and period.

**Connected to:**

- *is used to route* [Give-up / Take-up Process](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#give-up--take-up-process)
- *is issued by* [Party](#party)
- [Identifier Scheme](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#identifier-scheme) *is the scheme of* Account Identifier

### Client Regulatory Category

*How a provider has categorised a client under conduct rules, for example professional client or eligible counterparty.*

**Definition.** Regulatory classification of a client by a provider for an investment or clearing service under a named regime, attached to the client role toward that provider (not to the legal entity in general), with its basis (per se, on request, by agreement), evidence and period; the same entity can be categorised differently by different providers or for different services.

**Example.** Client A is categorised as a professional client by Broker B's European entity and as an eligible counterparty for a different service.

**Not to be confused with.** Counterparty Regulatory Status: that belongs to the legal entity and drives obligations such as mandatory clearing; this belongs to the relationship and drives client protection.

**Kind of:** [Regulatory Classification](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#regulatory-classification)

**Values (draft list):** retail client; professional client (per se); professional client (elective, on request); eligible counterparty; unknown; other regimes' customer categories (to verify per regime)

**Words people use:** “MiFID client category” (EU, UK); “client categorisation” (any)

**Before using it, know:** Regime, contracting entity, service, basis and period.

**Connected to:**

- *classifies* [Client](#client)
- *is assigned by* [Contracting Entity](#contracting-entity)
- *is defined under* [Regulatory Regime](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#regulatory-regime)

### Counterparty Regulatory Status

*The regulatory status a company has in its own right, which decides rules such as whether it must clear.*

**Definition.** Regulatory classification of a legal entity under a named regime that determines its own obligations as a counterparty (for example whether it is subject to a clearing obligation or margin rules), possibly per asset class, with basis (self-declaration, calculation, authority decision), evidence and period; it attaches to the legal entity, never to a client role or a commercial group.

**Example.** Client A Ltd declares itself a non-financial counterparty above the clearing threshold for interest rate derivatives.

**Not to be confused with.** Regulatory Authorisation: licences and registrations (for example swap dealer, futures commission merchant) are authorisations, not counterparty statuses.

**Kind of:** [Regulatory Classification](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#regulatory-classification)

**Values (draft list):** financial counterparty above the clearing threshold (per asset class and calculation period); financial counterparty below the clearing threshold; non-financial counterparty above the clearing threshold; non-financial counterparty below the clearing threshold; third-country entity; eligible contract participant (US); US person (CFTC cross-border meaning, to verify); unknown

**Words people use:** “EMIR classification” (EU, UK); “counterparty classification” (any)

**Before using it, know:** Regime, asset class where relevant, basis, source, as-of date and period.

**Connected to:**

- *classifies* [Legal Entity](#legal-entity)
- *is defined under* [Regulatory Regime](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#regulatory-regime)
