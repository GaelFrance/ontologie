# Clearing: services, accounts and client clearing

> Generated by `tools/build_domain_views.py`; edit `overview.md`, `company-questions.md` or the registers instead.

Once a derivative trade is executed, a central counterparty (CCP) steps in between buyer and seller and guarantees it. This domain describes where that cleared trade lives and on what terms: which clearing service clears it, which firm answers to the CCP, which firm clears for the client, which CCP account holds the position, how the client's assets are kept apart from others, and what happens when a client moves or a member fails. Getting this right answers two pilot questions: "can we clear for client X at CCP Z, and how?" and "where does this trade land?"

## Concepts at a glance

- **[Clearing Service](concepts.md#clearing-service)** — One line of business of a CCP, such as its listed equity derivatives clearing or its interest rate swap clearing, with its own members, accounts, margin and rules.
- **[Clearing Member](concepts.md#clearing-member)** — A firm admitted by a CCP to one of its clearing services, in whose name trades are registered and which answers to the CCP for them.
- **[Clearing Broker](concepts.md#clearing-broker)** — The firm that clears a client's trades for it and is answerable to that client, whether or not it is itself a member of the CCP.
- **[Central Counterparty](concepts.md#central-counterparty)** — The role of the clearing house that steps in between buyer and seller and becomes the counterparty to each.
- **[CCP Account](concepts.md#ccp-account)** — An account that a CCP opens in one clearing service, for a member's own business or for its clients, where the CCP records positions and attributes margin and collateral.
  - **[House Account](concepts.md#house-account)** — The CCP account for a clearing member's own trading.
  - **[Client Omnibus Account](concepts.md#client-omnibus-account)** — One CCP account shared by several clients of a clearing member, kept apart from the member's own business.
  - **[Individually Segregated Client Account](concepts.md#individually-segregated-client-account)** — A CCP account used for one client alone, so its positions and collateral are visible separately at the CCP.
  - **[Indirect Client Account](concepts.md#indirect-client-account)** — A CCP account for the clients of a clearing member's client.
  - **[CCP Position Account](concepts.md#ccp-position-account)** — A sub-account at the CCP where positions are kept apart inside a larger account.
- **[Segregation Model](concepts.md#segregation-model)** — The rules that say how a client's positions and assets are kept apart from the clearing member's and from other clients'.
- **[Agreement](concepts.md#agreement)** — Any arrangement that creates rights and duties between parties, whether signed or accepted by joining published rules.
  - **[Client Clearing Agreement](concepts.md#client-clearing-agreement)** — The contract between a client and its clearing broker that sets how the client is cleared: legal model, segregation, margin, collateral, limits, fees and what happens on default.
    - **[Segregation Election](concepts.md#segregation-election)** — The client's recorded choice of how its positions and collateral are segregated at a given CCP.
- **[Position](concepts.md#position)** — What is open in an account on a given day: how many contracts are held long and short, or which cleared swaps are still running.
  - **[Listed Position](concepts.md#listed-position)** — How many contracts of a listed future or option series are open, long and short, in one account on one day.
  - **[Cleared OTC Position](concepts.md#cleared-otc-position)** — The cleared swaps still running in one account on one day.
- **[CCP-facing Trade](concepts.md#ccp-facing-trade)** — The cleared trade that a member holds with the CCP after clearing, in its house account or in a client account.
- **[Client Back-to-Back Trade](concepts.md#client-back-to-back-trade)** — The matching trade between the clearing broker and its client that exists when the broker clears as principal.
- **[Position Transfer](concepts.md#position-transfer)** — Moving open positions from one CCP account to another on request, for example when a client changes clearing broker.
- **[Clearing Member Default](concepts.md#clearing-member-default)** — The CCP formally declares that a member has failed, which starts the default procedures.
  - **[Porting](concepts.md#porting)** — Moving clients' positions and collateral from a failed clearing member to a back-up member, or closing them out if that fails.
- **[Back-up Clearing Member](concepts.md#back-up-clearing-member)** — A clearing member a client names in advance, or that agrees at the time of a default, to receive the client's positions if its usual clearing member fails; being named does not by itself commit it.
- **[Sponsoring Clearing Member](concepts.md#sponsoring-clearing-member)** — A clearing member that stands behind a client admitted directly to a CCP under a sponsored model.
- **[Position Adjustment](concepts.md#position-adjustment)** — A permitted correction or close-out of a position at the CCP that does not involve a new trade.
- **[Client Default](concepts.md#client-default)** — When a clearing broker declares its own client in default and takes action on that client's positions.
