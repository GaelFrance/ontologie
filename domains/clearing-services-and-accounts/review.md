## 1. Concepts you approve

| Concept | Definition | Your verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **[Clearing Service](concepts.md#clearing-service)** | Distinct line of clearing operated by one central counterparty under its rules for a defined product scope (its eligible contracts or OTC products), with its own membership categories, CCP account types, margin methodology, fee schedule and default-management arrangements; default resources may be shared with other services of the same CCP. Not the firm's Service Offering. | |
| **[Clearing Member](concepts.md#clearing-member)** | Role of a party admitted to a Clearing Service under a membership category defined in that service's rules, which entitles it to have trades registered in its own name in CCP accounts and makes it directly answerable to the central counterparty for them, within the clearing scope its category allows (own business, affiliates, clients, non-clearing members). Held per clearing service and for an effective period. A participant admitted under a sponsored or direct-client model holds the Sponsored Clearing Participant role; it also holds Clearing Member only where that CCP's rules call it a member (to verify per rulebook). | |
| **[Clearing Broker](concepts.md#clearing-broker)** | Role of a party that provides clearing of an identified client's trades in a clearing service under a Client Clearing Agreement and is responsible to that client for them, either as a clearing member of that service or through another clearing member, including a client of a clearing member that clears for its own indirect clients; distinct from admission at the CCP (Clearing Member) and from regulatory registrations such as futures commission merchant. | |
| **[Central Counterparty](concepts.md#central-counterparty)** | Role of a legal entity that interposes itself between the counterparties to trades accepted in one or more of its clearing services, becoming the buyer to every seller and the seller to every buyer, by the legal mechanism (novation, open offer or other) and at the moment its rules define. Its authorisation, recognition or registration in each regime is a separate Regulatory Classification. | |
| **[CCP Account](concepts.md#ccp-account)** | Account opened by a central counterparty within one clearing service, in the name of a clearing member or, under a sponsored or direct-client model, of the participant admitted in its own name, of a type defined by that service's rules (house; client omnibus, net or gross; individually segregated client; indirect client, basic or gross omnibus; regime-specific customer account classes such as the US futures, foreign futures and cleared swaps customer accounts), to which the CCP attributes positions and, directly or through a linked margin or collateral unit defined by its rules, margin requirements and collateral. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[Segregation Model](concepts.md#segregation-model)** | Set of rules, set by a regulation, a CCP's rules or an agreement, for keeping the positions and assets of one party or group of parties apart from those of others (for example clients from the clearing member, or one client from all other clients), at a stated level (CCP books or clearing member books) and under a stated regime. It describes how records and assets are separated; the legal protection that results is a separate conclusion of Legal. | |
| **[Agreement](concepts.md#agreement)** | Arrangement creating rights and obligations between identified parties, for example for a service, documented by one or more signed documents or by adherence to published rules (such as a CCP rulebook or a venue's rules), with an effective period. | |
| **[Client Clearing Agreement](concepts.md#client-clearing-agreement)** | Agreement, made of one or more documents, under which a clearing broker clears an identified client's trades in one or more clearing services, setting the client clearing legal model (principal-to-principal or agency), the segregation election per clearing service, margin and collateral terms, the basis on which the clearing broker may set clearing limits, charges, whether the client may itself clear for indirect clients, and what happens on the default of either party, including porting and close-out. It also documents the non-clearing member model, where a general clearing member clears for a venue member. | |
| **[Position](concepts.md#position)** | Open quantity of a listed contract series, or set of open cleared OTC trades, recorded for an identified account (CCP account, CCP position account or internal account) within a clearing service, on a stated basis (long and short kept apart, or net) as of a stated business date and processing cycle, as asserted by an identified source. Exposure, notional totals and valuations are measures derived from a position, not the position itself. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[CCP-facing Trade](concepts.md#ccp-facing-trade)** *(level 2)* | Kind of Trade between a central counterparty and the clearing member, or the participant admitted in its own name under a sponsored or direct-client model, that holds the CCP account in which it is registered (house or client). It is created by CCP Interposition under a clearing service's rules and succeeds the executed trade on that side. A change of holding member or account (give-up registration, position transfer, porting), a change of economic terms, compression or close-out ends or reduces it and creates a linked successor trade; it is never changed in place. Only a non-economic correction (for example a client reference) is recorded as a Trade Amendment against the same trade. | |
| **[Client Back-to-Back Trade](concepts.md#client-back-to-back-trade)** *(level 2)* | Under the principal-to-principal client clearing model, the trade between a clearing broker and its client that mirrors, on opposite sides, the CCP-facing trade held in the client's account; it arises from the client clearing agreement, not from the CCP's rules. The same pattern repeats between a client and its indirect client. Under the agency model no such trade exists: the clearing broker carries the CCP-facing trade for the client and guarantees its performance. | |
| **[House Account](concepts.md#house-account)** *(level 2)* | CCP account in which a clearing member's own trades and positions are recorded and, where the member chooses and the service's rules allow, those of its affiliates; it is kept apart from client accounts, and its margining basis (net or gross) is stated per clearing service. | |
| **[Client Omnibus Account](concepts.md#client-omnibus-account)** *(level 2)* | CCP account, held in a clearing member's name, in which the positions and collateral of several clients are recorded together, apart from the member's own; it is net or gross, and its margining basis is stated per clearing service. The US customer account classes at a derivatives clearing organisation are margined gross, and cleared swaps customer collateral is attributed per customer (to verify); they must not be equated with EU or UK net omnibus accounts. | |
| **[Individually Segregated Client Account](concepts.md#individually-segregated-client-account)** *(level 2)* | CCP account, opened in a clearing member's name for one client only, in which that client's positions and the collateral provided for it are recorded apart from all other clients. Where collateral above the client's requirement must sit is set by the regime (for example posted to the CCP for individually segregated clients under EU and UK EMIR, to verify) and otherwise by the CCP's rules and the client clearing agreement. | |
| **[Indirect Client Account](concepts.md#indirect-client-account)** *(level 2)* | CCP account, held in a clearing member's name for a client that itself clears for indirect clients, in which the indirect clients' positions and collateral are recorded apart from that client's own business and from the member's. EU rules provide a basic omnibus form (all indirect clients of that client together) and a gross omnibus form (each indirect client's positions kept apart and margined separately). | |
| **[CCP Position Account](concepts.md#ccp-position-account)** *(level 2)* | Subdivision of a CCP account, opened under a clearing service's rules, in which the CCP keeps open positions separately, for example one per client inside a gross omnibus account. Margin is calculated on a Margin Portfolio that can group several position accounts, or split one. | |
| **[Segregation Election](concepts.md#segregation-election)** *(level 2)* | Choice made by a client, for each clearing service and clearing broker, among the segregation options it is offered (for example omnibus or individual segregation), where the regime or the CCP provides for a choice; dated, and recorded with the disclosure it was based on. Where the regime sets segregation by account class (for example US customer accounts), the value is 'not applicable (set by regime)'. The duty to offer the choice and to disclose levels of protection and costs is a Regulatory Obligation. | |
| **[Listed Position](concepts.md#listed-position)** *(level 2)* | Position in a series of a clearing contract, stated as the number of contracts held long and short, or net, in an identified account (CCP account, CCP position account or internal account), as of a clearing date and processing window, as asserted by a named source. For fungible or cross-listed contracts one position can hold series traded on several venues. | |
| **[Cleared OTC Position](concepts.md#cleared-otc-position)** *(level 2)* | Position made of the set of open cleared OTC trades (CCP-facing trades, or client back-to-back trades at the member-to-client tier) recorded for an identified account within a clearing service, as of a clearing date. It is described by trade count, notional or risk sensitivities, each a measure derived from the trades, never by a contract quantity. | |
| **[Position Transfer](concepts.md#position-transfer)** *(level 2)* | Movement, at the request of a clearing member or its client and under a clearing service's transfer rules, of existing open positions (or open cleared trades) from one CCP account to another, within one clearing member or between two clearing members, without a new execution; the client behind the positions normally stays the same, and the price basis, consent, deadlines and fees are set by the CCP's rules. Distinct from a give-up, which attributes a new trade, and from porting, which follows a member default. | |
| **[Clearing Member Default](concepts.md#clearing-member-default)** *(level 2)* | Declaration by a central counterparty, under a clearing service's default rules, that a clearing member is in default, which starts the CCP's default management of that member's house and client accounts in each service concerned (for example porting, close-out, auctions and use of default resources). Distinct from a client defaulting toward its clearing broker, which the client clearing agreement governs. | |
| **[Porting](concepts.md#porting)** *(level 2)* | Process, run under a CCP's default rules after a clearing member default, by which the positions recorded in a client account of the defaulting member, and the collateral attributable to them, are transferred to a back-up clearing member that accepts them, or, if transfer is not achieved within the time the rules allow, closed out with any balance returned to the clients or for their account. Positions and collateral are separate legs with separate outcomes. | |
| **[Back-up Clearing Member](concepts.md#back-up-clearing-member)** *(level 2)* | Role held toward a client of another clearing member, within one clearing service, by a party that holds the Clearing Member role there and that the client has named in advance, or that agrees at the time of a default, to receive the client's positions and collateral by porting. It is a role toward a client, not a membership category. | |
| **[Sponsoring Clearing Member](concepts.md#sponsoring-clearing-member)** *(level 2)* | Role held toward one Sponsored Clearing Participant, within one clearing service, by the party that supports the participant under the CCP's sponsored or direct-client model (for example by guaranteeing its obligations, paying its default fund contribution or acting as its clearing agent), under a Sponsored Clearing Arrangement. It is normally held by a clearing member of that service; whether a non-member clearing agent can hold it is a rulebook fact (to verify per CCP). | |
| **[Position Adjustment](concepts.md#position-adjustment)** *(level 2)* | Lifecycle event, permitted by the clearing service's rules and requested by the clearing member, that changes an open position at the CCP without a new trade: closing out long against short quantities held in a gross account, correcting an open or close indicator, or other position maintenance the rules allow. It references the positions affected and is distinct from a position transfer (a move between accounts) and from a trade amendment. | |
| **[Client Default](concepts.md#client-default)** *(level 2)* | Declaration by a clearing broker, under a client clearing agreement, that its client is in default, which allows the broker to close out, transfer or hedge the client's positions and use its collateral. It sits at the member-to-client tier and, under the principal-to-principal model, ends the client back-to-back trades. It is distinct from a Clearing Member Default declared by a CCP, and from a settlement fail or an unmet margin call, which may lead to it but are not it. | |

## 3. Open questions on meaning

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### Brokerage paid (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Identify debtor, actual payer, beneficiary and recharge treatment.

**Proposed answer:** Under a give-up the client usually owes executing brokerage and the clearing broker often pays it and debits the client: two charges linked by a “recharges” link, with debtor, payer and observing entity explicit. "We paid" cannot be answered without the observing entity. Confidence high. Firm decision: which model applies per client or market. [05, 02, 01]

<sub>reference LD-A0003</sub>

### Account (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify function, maintaining party, holder, beneficiary and account-to-account relationships.

**Proposed answer:** Three levels: the CCP Account of each clearing service; the member's internal client account; execution-side and give-up references, which are identifiers rather than accounts. Levels are related only by an explicit, time-bounded mapping. Segregation regime and regulatory account statuses are separate, time-bounded dimensions. Confidence high. [02, 07, 06]

<sub>reference LD-A0007</sub>

### Margin required (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Distinguish IM/VM, member–CCP/client–member relationship, risk scope, time and collateral coverage.

**Proposed answer:** Undefined until five qualifiers are fixed: tier (from margin provider and taker role assignments: CCP–member house, CCP–member client account, member–client, client–indirect client); component (IM, VM, add-ons, total); account and gross or net basis; as-of time and run; currency basis. Under net omnibus accounts a client-level figure exists only in the member's own calculation. Member–client IM is a CCP replication, a replication with multiplier or add-on, or an own model (Margin Methodology). Confidence high. Firm decision: default tier, if any. [04]

<sub>reference LD-A0011</sub>

### Position (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify gross/net, quantity/notional/exposure, account, instrument, CCP and as-of basis.

**Proposed answer:** Key: account (CCP or internal) × instrument × clearing service × side or net basis, as of a business date and cycle, with the asserting source (CCP statement or internal). House accounts are generally net; client omnibus accounts are net or gross by account type. CCP-reported and internal positions are two records of one position; a difference is a break. OTC positions are sets of open cleared trades measured by count, notional or risk sensitivity. Confidence high. Firm decision: default basis and source, or return both labelled. [02, 03, 07]

<sub>reference LD-A0013</sub>

### Clearing broker, clearing member, FCM (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Service role toward the client versus admission at a CCP versus a regulatory registration (FCM in the US).

**Proposed answer:** Clearing Broker is the service role toward the client and may exist without membership of the clearing service; Clearing Member is admission at the CCP; FCM is a US regulatory registration held by a legal entity, recorded as a Regulatory Classification, and only a contextual alias. Confidence high. [02, 03, 05, 06, 08]

<sub>reference LD-A0019</sub>

### Service (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** A CCP's clearing service versus the firm's service offering versus a MiFID investment service; also the service mix (execution-only, clearing-only, execute-and-clear).

**Proposed answer:** Two concepts, no common parent for now: a CCP's Clearing Service and a provider's Service Offering, linked by a “relies on” link. A MiFID "investment service" is a regulatory category, not either concept. Confidence high. [02, 03, 05, 07, 08]

<sub>reference LD-A0020</sub>

### Current, as of, as known at (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Valid time versus record time: valid now and latest non-superseded record, valid at a business date, or known by a timestamp.

**Proposed answer:** Current = valid now and latest non-superseded record from the stated source; as of D = valid at business date D; as known at T = only records received by T. Every answer states source, as-of date and freshness. See the time convention in [modelling guidelines](../docs/modelling-guidelines.md). Confidence high. Firm decision: whether "as known at" history is needed. [07, 08, 06]

<sub>reference LD-A0023</sub>

### Omnibus, house and client (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** EMIR omnibus (net or gross) versus US customer omnibus versus indirect omnibus versus internal pooled account; CCP account type versus economic relationship of affiliates.

**Proposed answer:** EMIR omnibus and individual client segregation (Art. 39(2) and (3), to verify), US futures-customer omnibus, indirect-client omnibus account types (Delegated Regulations (EU) 2017/2154 and 2017/2155, to verify) and internal pooled accounts are different things; the CCP account type does not determine the economic relationship (affiliates may be cleared as clients or in the house account). Confidence high. [02, 04, 03]

<sub>reference LD-A0028</sub>

### Eligible, mandatory, offered, covered (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** CCP clearing eligibility versus a regulatory clearing obligation for a counterparty pair versus the firm's offering versus "we cover market X" (membership, third-party access or actual offering).

**Proposed answer:** Eligibility is a CCP fact; a clearing obligation applies to a counterparty pair, a product class including trade-term ranges, and a date; offering and coverage are firm facts in the internal overlay, through a Market Access Arrangement. Never infer one from another. Confidence high. [03, 05, 06]

<sub>reference LD-A0029</sub>

### Client clearing model (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Combination of access model, legal model, segregation model and margining basis; model offered versus legal protection or portability actually achieved.

**Proposed answer:** Never resolve to a single concept: decompose into access model (Market Access Arrangement), legal model (Client Clearing Agreement), Segregation Model and margining basis (CCP Account). The model offered says nothing by itself about legal protection or portability achieved, which only Legal concludes. Confidence high. [05, 06]

<sub>reference LD-A0030</sub>

### Executing broker, firm, entity (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Client-facing executing broker versus trading venue member entering the order versus regulatory executing entity in transaction reports versus giving clearing member.

**Proposed answer:** Executing Broker is the client-facing service role; the Trading Venue Member enters the order in its own name; the executing entity of a transaction report is a regulatory reporting role; the giving clearing member is the clearing member that holds the trade before the give-up. One party may hold several of these roles; never infer one from another. Confidence high. [01]
