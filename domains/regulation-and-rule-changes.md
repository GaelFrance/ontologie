## 3. Ambiguities and proposed answers

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### LD-A0019 — Clearing broker, clearing member, FCM (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Service role toward the client versus admission at a CCP versus a regulatory registration (FCM in the US).

**Proposed answer:** Clearing Broker (LD-C0057) is the service role toward the client and may exist without membership of the clearing service; Clearing Member (LD-C0007) is admission at the CCP; FCM is a US regulatory registration held by a legal entity, recorded as a Regulatory Classification (LD-C0068), and only a contextual alias. Confidence high. [02, 03, 05, 06, 08]

### LD-A0022 — Dates of a rule change (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Publication, entry into force, application, phase-in and end of transition versus the date the firm recorded the change.

**Proposed answer:** Each Rule Change (LD-C0064) carries role-qualified dates: publication, entry into force, application, phase-in and end of transition, plus the date the firm recorded it. "Effective date" without a role is ambiguous. Confidence high. [02, 05, 06, 08]

### LD-A0023 — Current, as of, as known at (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Valid time versus record time: valid now and latest non-superseded record, valid at a business date, or known by a timestamp.

**Proposed answer:** Current = valid now and latest non-superseded record from the stated source; as of D = valid at business date D; as known at T = only records received by T. Every answer states source, as-of date and freshness. See the time convention in [modelling guidelines](../docs/modelling-guidelines.md). Confidence high. Firm decision: whether "as known at" history is needed. [07, 08, 06]

### LD-A0031 — Group (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Regulatory consolidation group versus commercial group versus CCP affiliate versus legal parent in public entity-relationship data.

**Proposed answer:** A regulatory consolidation group (used in some threshold calculations), a Commercial Group (LD-C0073), a CCP's "affiliate" and a legal parent in public entity-relationship data are different; regulatory statuses never attach to a commercial group. Confidence high. [06, 05, 07]

### LD-A0032 — OTC and listed (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Regulatory "OTC" (derivatives not executed on a regulated market or equivalent) versus off-venue versus not exchange-listed; cleared is a separate dimension.

**Proposed answer:** Under EMIR Art. 2(7) a derivative not executed on a regulated market or an equivalent third-country market is OTC, so MTF and OTF trades count as OTC; venue type is a Regulatory Classification of LD-C0009. Scope modules by listing and clearing structure, not by that label. Confidence high. [06, 02, 03]
