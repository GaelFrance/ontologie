## Usage notes and required context

- **LD-C0011 Segregation Model**
  - Note: Public instances, not subclasses: EMIR omnibus and individual client segregation (Art. 39); indirect-clearing account structures (Delegated Regulations (EU) 2017/2154 for listed and 2017/2155 for OTC); US futures-customer segregation, cleared-swaps LSOC (17 CFR Part 22) and foreign-futures secured amount (Part 30); paragraphs to verify. State the level (CCP or member books). Legal protection is a Legal conclusion. [02, 03, 04, 06]
- **LD-C0024 Novation**
  - Note: Generic legal concept: one possible mechanism of CCP interposition, and also used for bilateral step-in and step-out outside clearing. Never infer novation from "cleared" or "ported". [02, 03, 06]
- **LD-C0068 Regulatory Classification**
  - An agent must know: Regime, asset class where relevant, basis, effective period, evidence, as-of and record time

## Ambiguities and proposed answers

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### LD-A0007 — Account (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify function, maintaining party, holder, beneficiary and account-to-account relationships.

**Proposed answer:** Three levels: the CCP Account (LD-C0069) of each clearing service; the member's internal client account; execution-side and give-up references, which are identifiers rather than accounts. Levels are related only by explicit, time-bounded LD-R0065. Segregation regime and regulatory account statuses are separate, time-bounded dimensions. Confidence high. [02, 07, 06]

### LD-A0009 — Cleared (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate acceptance status from legal effect and record effective time.

**Proposed answer:** "Cleared" is a derived status meaning that CCP Interposition (LD-C0054) has occurred in a named clearing service. Three timestamps stay distinct: clearing member acceptance (LD-C0053), CCP acceptance or registration (LD-C0023), interposition. Under open offer interposition can occur at matching, before any registration message; under novation it occurs at registration. A completed give-up changes which member holds the trade, not whether it is cleared. Confidence high on the principle, medium on each CCP's moment. Legal decision: the interposition moment per clearing service, from the rulebook. [02, 03, 06, 08]

### LD-A0017 — Novation and acceptance (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Do not assume all clearing uses the same legal mechanism or timestamp.

**Proposed answer:** Four distinct steps: clearing member acceptance, CCP acceptance or registration, CCP interposition with its mechanism (novation, open offer, other), and settlement finality (outside current scope). Under the EU principal-to-principal model the client's contract is with the clearing member and arises from the client clearing agreement, not from the CCP's novation; in US models the FCM guarantees performance, and who is principal to the CCP contract varies by product and rulebook. OTC "novation" also names bilateral step-in and step-out, which is not clearing. Confidence high. [02, 03, 06]

### LD-A0019 — Clearing broker, clearing member, FCM (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Service role toward the client versus admission at a CCP versus a regulatory registration (FCM in the US).

**Proposed answer:** Clearing Broker (LD-C0057) is the service role toward the client and may exist without membership of the clearing service; Clearing Member (LD-C0007) is admission at the CCP; FCM is a US regulatory registration held by a legal entity, recorded as a Regulatory Classification (LD-C0068), and only a contextual alias. Confidence high. [02, 03, 05, 06, 08]

### LD-A0022 — Dates of a rule change (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Publication, entry into force, application, phase-in and end of transition versus the date the firm recorded the change.

**Proposed answer:** Each Rule Change (LD-C0064) carries role-qualified dates: publication, entry into force, application, phase-in and end of transition, plus the date the firm recorded it. "Effective date" without a role is ambiguous. Confidence high. [02, 05, 06, 08]

### LD-A0023 — Current, as of, as known at (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Valid time versus record time: valid now and latest non-superseded record, valid at a business date, or known by a timestamp.

**Proposed answer:** Current = valid now and latest non-superseded record from the stated source; as of D = valid at business date D; as known at T = only records received by T. Every answer states source, as-of date and freshness. See the time convention in [modelling guidelines](../docs/modelling-guidelines.md). Confidence high. Firm decision: whether "as known at" history is needed. [07, 08, 06]

### LD-A0025 — The trade (clearing tier) (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Pre-clearing (executed) trade versus CCP-facing trade versus member-client trade; which tier, identifier and counterparty view a question uses.

**Proposed answer:** State the tier: pre-clearing trade, CCP-facing trade, member–client trade (principal model). Successor trades are linked by LD-R0034 and may carry new identifiers. Confidence high. [03]

### LD-A0026 — Give-up, transfer, porting (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Listed give-up versus voluntary position transfer versus default porting versus OTC or FX prime-brokerage "give-up"; trigger, consent, rule, fees and whether collateral moves.

**Proposed answer:** Give-up (LD-C0022) attributes a listed trade to a receiving clearing member; a position transfer moves open positions between CCP accounts under transfer rules, usually voluntarily; porting is the default-triggered transfer of a defaulting member's client positions and collateral to a back-up member, or their close-out (EMIR Art. 48(5) to (7), to verify). Positions and collateral port as separate legs. OTC or FX "give-up" usually means prime-brokerage give-up. Position Transfer and Porting are backlog concepts. Confidence high. [02, 03, 04, 06]

### LD-A0028 — Omnibus, house and client (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** EMIR omnibus (net or gross) versus US customer omnibus versus indirect omnibus versus internal pooled account; CCP account type versus economic relationship of affiliates.

**Proposed answer:** EMIR omnibus and individual client segregation (Art. 39(2) and (3), to verify), US futures-customer omnibus, indirect-client omnibus account types (Delegated Regulations (EU) 2017/2154 and 2017/2155, to verify) and internal pooled accounts are different things; the CCP account type does not determine the economic relationship (affiliates may be cleared as clients or in the house account). Confidence high. [02, 04, 03]

### LD-A0030 — Client clearing model (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Combination of access model, legal model, segregation model and margining basis; model offered versus legal protection or portability actually achieved.

**Proposed answer:** Never resolve to a single concept: decompose into access model (LD-C0059), legal model (LD-C0056), segregation model (LD-C0011) and margining basis (LD-C0069). The model offered says nothing by itself about legal protection or portability achieved, which only Legal concludes. Confidence high. [05, 06]

### LD-A0031 — Group (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Regulatory consolidation group versus commercial group versus CCP affiliate versus legal parent in public entity-relationship data.

**Proposed answer:** A regulatory consolidation group (used in some threshold calculations), a Commercial Group (LD-C0073), a CCP's "affiliate" and a legal parent in public entity-relationship data are different; regulatory statuses never attach to a commercial group. Confidence high. [06, 05, 07]

### LD-A0032 — OTC and listed (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Regulatory "OTC" (derivatives not executed on a regulated market or equivalent) versus off-venue versus not exchange-listed; cleared is a separate dimension.

**Proposed answer:** Under EMIR Art. 2(7) a derivative not executed on a regulated market or an equivalent third-country market is OTC, so MTF and OTF trades count as OTC; venue type is a Regulatory Classification of LD-C0009. Scope modules by listing and clearing structure, not by that label. Confidence high. [06, 02, 03]
