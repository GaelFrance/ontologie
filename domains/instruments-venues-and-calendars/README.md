# Instruments, venues and calendars

> Generated by `tools/build_domain_views.py`; edit `overview.md`, `company-questions.md` or the registers instead.

This domain describes **what is traded, where it is traded, and on which day it counts**. Every other part of the business leans on it: an order names a contract, a trade happens on a venue, a give-up has a deadline on a given day, a fee is charged per contract. Misread the contract, the venue or the date, and every answer built on it is wrong.

## Concepts at a glance

- **[Product Family](concepts.md#product-family)** — A family of similar traded products, such as 'equity index futures' or 'fixed-to-floating interest rate swaps', described without reference to any exchange.
- **[Contract Specification](concepts.md#contract-specification)** — The rulebook page of one listed contract: underlying, size, tick, expiry months, settlement method and trading hours, from which all its series are created.
  - **[Underlying](concepts.md#underlying)** — The thing a derivative is 'on': a share, an index, an interest rate, a commodity, or another derivative.
  - **[Key-Date Rule](concepts.md#key-date-rule)** — The rule that says, for each series, when it stops trading, when it expires and when it settles, for example 'third Friday of the month, or the day before if that is a holiday'.
- **[Instrument](concepts.md#instrument)** — One specific thing you can buy or sell that is the same for everybody: for listed derivatives, a series such as the March future or the June 100 call.
  - **[Listed Strategy](concepts.md#listed-strategy)** — A ready-made combination, such as a calendar spread or an option straddle, that a venue lets you trade in one go.
  - **[Flexible Series](concepts.md#flexible-series)** — A series with custom terms, such as an unusual expiry date or strike, that a venue creates on demand for a participant.
  - **[Settlement Price](concepts.md#settlement-price)** — The official end-of-day (or final) price that the exchange or CCP publishes for a series and that positions are valued at.
- **[Trading Venue](concepts.md#trading-venue)** — An organised marketplace, such as an exchange or a multilateral trading facility, where many participants' buying and selling interests meet under the venue's rules.
  - **[Trading Venue Segment](concepts.md#trading-venue-segment)** — A section of an exchange, such as its equity derivatives market or its commodity market, that can have its own rules, hours and fees.
- **[Business Date](concepts.md#business-date)** — A day that a business event counts towards, always stated with its role (trade date, clearing date, value date) and the calendar it is read against.
- **[Business Calendar](concepts.md#business-calendar)** — The published list of open and closed days, early closes and sessions for one venue, contract, CCP, currency or office.
  - **[Trading Session](concepts.md#trading-session)** — A part of the trading day, such as the opening auction, continuous trading or an evening session, with its hours and the trade date it counts towards.
  - **[Processing Window](concepts.md#processing-window)** — A named stretch of a clearing day when a CCP or payment system accepts a given step.
- **[Identifier Scheme](concepts.md#identifier-scheme)** — A numbering system, such as ISIN for instruments or MIC for venues, that says who issues the numbers, what they look like and where they are unique.
- **[Contract Adjustment](concepts.md#contract-adjustment)** — A change a venue makes to existing contracts because something happened to the underlying, such as a share split or special dividend.
- **[Clearing Contract](concepts.md#clearing-contract)** — The CCP's own version of a contract, under which it records positions, even when the contract is traded on more than one venue.
- **[Clearing Eligibility Schedule](concepts.md#clearing-eligibility-schedule)** — The CCP's published list of what it can clear, and within which limits.
