## 1. Concepts you approve

| Concept | Definition | Your verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **[Fee Charge](concepts.md#fee-charge)** | Obligation of exactly one debtor toward exactly one creditor to pay an amount for a service, arising from a chargeable event or a period under an applicable fee schedule or agreed terms; it carries its amount certainty (estimated or final) and billing stage (accrued, invoiced, disputed, adjusted, settled, written off), and may be paid by a party other than the debtor or collected by a party other than the creditor. Includes late and penalty charges arising from a missed cut-off or a settlement fail. Distinct from the revenue it may contribute to and from the cash movement that settles it. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[Fee Schedule](concepts.md#fee-schedule)** | Versioned set of rates, charge bases, conditions, discounts, caps, minimums and incentive programmes, issued by an identified issuer (a trading venue operator, a CCP, a regulator or self-regulatory body, or a broker in its client pricing terms) for a defined scope (service, clearing service, venue or segment, contract specification, client or client segment) and effective period, under which fee charges are determined. | |
| **[Client Execution Fee](concepts.md#client-execution-fee)** | Fee charge owed by a client to the provider of an execution service for executing its orders, stated on a charge basis (per contract side, round turn, ticket, allocation or notional) and under a pricing form (all-in, including venue charges, or fees-plus, with venue charges passed on separately). When the client's trades are given up to another clearing broker, the same charge is also executing brokerage. Distinct from the venue's own fee. | |
| **[Client Clearing Fee](concepts.md#client-clearing-fee)** | Fee charge owed by a client to its clearing broker for clearing its trades in one or more clearing services, arising from clearing events (registration of a listed lot or an OTC trade, give-in, exercise, assignment or delivery, transfer) or from a period (OTC maintenance on open trades or notional, account fees), stated on a charge basis and pricing form. Distinct from CCP fee charges passed on to the client and from income earned on the client's collateral. | |
| **[Executing Broker Brokerage Charge](concepts.md#executing-broker-brokerage-charge)** | Fee charge owed to an executing broker for executing orders, most often for executions given up to a clearing broker, whose debtor (the client, or the broker that engaged the executing broker) and actual payer (often the clearing broker, which then recharges the client) are stated. From the firm's view it is brokerage received when the firm is the executing broker and brokerage paid when the firm owes or pays another executing broker. | |
| **[Exchange Fee Charge](concepts.md#exchange-fee-charge)** | Fee charge whose creditor is the operator of a trading venue, for a stated service nature (trading, registration of negotiated trades, market data, connectivity, membership or other), owed by the venue member, clearing member or other debtor that the venue's fee schedule names, and possibly collected by another organisation such as the CCP. Distinct from the CCP's own fees, from regulatory transaction fees and from any charge passing it on to a client. | |
| **[CCP Fee Charge](concepts.md#ccp-fee-charge)** | Fee charge whose creditor is a central counterparty, for a service of one of its clearing services (clearing or registration of trades, OTC maintenance, exercise, assignment or delivery, account and position services, holding of non-cash collateral, membership), owed by a clearing member or other participant under the CCP's fee schedule. Distinct from margin, default fund contributions, interest on cash collateral and any client charge passing it on. | |
| **[Commission](concepts.md#commission)** | Commercial label for the remuneration a broker charges a client per contract or per trade, quoted per side or per round turn, for execution, clearing or both; not an economic category of its own, so every use must be resolved to the client execution fee, the client clearing fee or a bundled execution and clearing fee. Deferred by the sponsor; resolution stays blocked. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[Revenue](concepts.md#revenue)** | Management measure of the income an observing entity earns from its business over a period, in a stated currency and under a stated management reporting basis (including whether pass-through charges are shown gross or net), attributable to a target such as a client, commercial group, service offering or order handling channel; built from fee charges earned and other income streams the basis includes. Distinct from cash received, from contribution after costs and from accounting revenue. | |
| **[Cost](concepts.md#cost)** | Management measure of expense that an observing entity incurs for its business over a period, in a stated currency and under a stated management reporting basis, classified by cost category (venue and CCP fees not recharged, third-party brokerage, third-party clearing, connectivity and market data, funding and collateral, default fund funding, attributed shared cost) and attributable to a target only under a stated attribution rule; which costs a contribution measure deducts is set by an approved cost perimeter. | |
| **[Execution Revenue](concepts.md#execution-revenue)** | Revenue an observing entity earns from execution services, attributed to the Service Offering (and its service kind) under which the client is served and to the contractual client of the execution relationship, and split by order handling channel; it includes client execution fees and executing brokerage received, with venue charges passed on shown gross or net as the management reporting basis states. | |
| **[Clearing Revenue](concepts.md#clearing-revenue)** | Revenue an observing entity earns from clearing services, attributed to the Service Offering (and its service kind) under which the client is served, to the contractual client of the clearing relationship, and also per Clearing Service of the CCP; it includes client clearing fees, with CCP charges passed on shown gross or net as the management reporting basis states. Collateral income is a separate stream whose inclusion is decided by the Collateral Manager and is never implicit. | |
| **[Contribution](concepts.md#contribution)** | Management revenue attributable to a target (client relationship, commercial group, service offering, venue or clearing service) less the costs within an explicitly approved cost perimeter, for a stated observing entity, period and currency; no formula or default cost perimeter is approved. | |
| **[Published Fee Schedule](concepts.md#published-fee-schedule)** *(level 2)* | Fee schedule issued by a trading venue operator, a CCP, or a regulator or self-regulatory organisation, made public to its members or participants, with versions and effective dates; it may be published with or annexed to a rulebook, but its meaning stays here. Its changes are Rule Changes announced by Change Notices, including any approval, filing or self-certification step. | |
| **[Client Pricing Terms](concepts.md#client-pricing-terms)** *(level 2)* | Fee schedule agreed between the broker's contracting entity and a client, possibly negotiated once for all clients of a commercial group, applying to a stated scope (clients, service offerings, service components, period); it sets the pricing form (all-in or fees-plus), rates, discounts, caps, minimums and the treatment of passed-on charges. A Commercial Group is the scope of the terms, never a party to them. A rate for the firm's own service points to the Service Component it prices. | |
| **[Fee Rate](concepts.md#fee-rate)** *(level 2)* | Single line of a fee schedule that sets an amount or percentage for a stated scope (product or contract, account type, execution method, service nature, client segment, membership status or programme), on a stated charge basis and currency, possibly tiered by activity volume, valid for the schedule's effective period. | |
| **[Fee Incentive Programme](concepts.md#fee-incentive-programme)** *(level 2)* | Set of conditions, published by a venue or CCP in its fee schedule or agreed with a client in its pricing terms, under which participants who register and meet stated obligations or volume thresholds over a period receive reduced rates or rebates; for example a liquidity provider, market-making or volume-tier programme. | |
| **[Pass-through Charge](concepts.md#pass-through-charge)** *(level 2)* | Fee charge owed by a client to its broker that passes on, at cost or with a stated mark-up, an identified charge the broker owes a third party (a venue, a CCP, a regulator or self-regulatory body, another executing broker or a third-party clearing member) for the client's activity; it keeps a link to the charge it passes on. | |
| **[Bundled Execution and Clearing Fee](concepts.md#bundled-execution-and-clearing-fee)** *(level 2)* | Single fee charge owed by a client to a broker that provides both execution and clearing of the same trades under an execute-and-clear service, quoted as one rate; for revenue per service it must be split between execution and clearing by a rule stated in the management reporting basis. | |
| **[Regulatory Transaction Fee](concepts.md#regulatory-transaction-fee)** *(level 2)* | Fee charge whose creditor is a regulator or self-regulatory organisation, levied per trade, per contract or on traded value under a published rule of that body and owed by a member, broker or clearing member, often collected through a venue, CCP or clearing broker and passed on to the client. Neither a venue fee nor a CCP fee. The name is the industry's; 'transaction' here does not resolve to the blocked concept Transaction. | |
| **[Fee Rebate](concepts.md#fee-rebate)** *(level 2)* | Amount owed back by the creditor of earlier fee charges to their debtor because the conditions of a fee incentive programme or of client pricing terms were met over a period; it refers to the charges or activity it rewards and is kept apart from the reduced rate (discount) and from a decision not to charge (waiver). | |
| **[Fee Adjustment](concepts.md#fee-adjustment)** *(level 2)* | Event that changes a fee charge after it was accrued or invoiced, by correction, cancellation, waiver, credit, dispute outcome or write-off, referring to the original charge and stating its own effect, reason and approver; the original is never deleted. | |
| **[Fee Invoice](concepts.md#fee-invoice)** *(level 2)* | Demand for payment issued by a creditor (or its collector) to a debtor for a set of fee charges and adjustments over a billing period, with a total per currency and a due date; one invoice can carry charges of several service natures and of several creditors when one organisation collects for others, so its lines must be decomposed before aggregation. | |
| **[Fee Collector](concepts.md#fee-collector)** *(level 2)* | Role of a party that bills and collects a fee charge on behalf of its creditor without being the creditor, such as a CCP collecting a venue's trading fees from clearing members, or a clearing broker collecting executing brokerage or regulatory fees; the collector passes the amount on and does not earn it. | |
| **[Activity Volume](concepts.md#activity-volume)** | Measure of business activity for a stated scope and period, counted in a stated unit (contract sides, round turns, tickets or trades, notional), on a stated counting basis (single or double), at a stated stage (executed, cleared, open) and from a stated source (published by a venue or CCP, or the firm's own records). | |
| **[Collateral Revenue](concepts.md#collateral-revenue)** *(level 2)* | Revenue an observing entity earns from client collateral over a period: income from client cash collateral net of interest paid to clients, where the firm earns any; fees for holding non-cash collateral; and other streams the Management Reporting Basis includes (for example price alignment amounts received and not passed on, if the Collateral Manager so decides). Price alignment amounts are otherwise neither fees nor collateral revenue. It is never included in clearing revenue implicitly. | |
| **[Management Reporting Basis](concepts.md#management-reporting-basis)** *(level 2)* | Approved, versioned set of rules under which management revenue, cost and contribution are reported: observing entity, attribution rule (per service to the contractual client, roll-up to commercial group, intra-group elimination), period and date role, reporting currency and conversion rule, gross or net presentation of pass-through charges and rebates, split rule for bundled fees, and the income streams included. | |
| **[Cost Perimeter](concepts.md#cost-perimeter)** *(level 2)* | Approved, versioned list of cost categories, with their attribution rules, that a given contribution measure deducts from revenue; any cost outside the list is excluded from that measure. No default perimeter is approved. | |

## 3. Open questions on meaning

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### Execution fee (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate client execution charges, executing-broker brokerage and exchange charges.

**Proposed answer:** Three charges, separated by creditor and service: the client execution charge (Client Execution Fee); executing brokerage on given-up executions (Executing Broker Brokerage Charge); the venue transaction fee (Exchange Fee Charge), whose debtor is set by venue or CCP rules. "Execution fee" is an ambiguous alias of all three. Confidence high. Firm decision: all-in versus fees-plus client pricing. [01, 05]

<sub>reference LD-A0001</sub>

### Fee, revenue and cash received (Open, narrowed 2026-09-20, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate charges, management revenue and cash settlement; define timing, gross/net, pass-through treatment and the boundary with contribution after costs.

**Proposed answer:** A charge accrues at its chargeable event, is then invoiced or debited and finally settled (Cash Movement); amount certainty (estimated or final) and billing stage are facets of Fee Charge. Collateral income is a separate stream whose inclusion in clearing revenue is undecided. Gross or net treatment of pass-through charges is a Finance decision; accounting principal-versus-agent guidance is only an analogue. Confidence high on stages, medium on treatment. [05, 04, 02]

<sub>reference LD-A0002</sub>

### Brokerage paid (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Identify debtor, actual payer, beneficiary and recharge treatment.

**Proposed answer:** Under a give-up the client usually owes executing brokerage and the clearing broker often pays it and debits the client: two charges linked by a “recharges” link, with debtor, payer and observing entity explicit. "We paid" cannot be answered without the observing entity. Confidence high. Firm decision: which model applies per client or market. [05, 02, 01]

<sub>reference LD-A0003</sub>

### Commission (Open, deferred by sponsor, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Validate whether this is a remuneration category, contractual charge type or local label.

**Proposed answer:** In listed derivatives usually the client-facing per-contract remuneration (per side or round turn) for execution, clearing or both: a commercial label, not an economic category. Keep Commission Draft with no fee parent and treat "commission" as a blocked alias that returns candidates (Client Execution Fee, Client Clearing Fee, a bundled execute-and-clear charge). Deferred by the sponsor. Confidence medium. [05, 01]

<sub>reference LD-A0004</sub>

### Exchange fees (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate service nature, economic beneficiary and collecting organisation; bundled bills need explicit decomposition.

**Proposed answer:** Decompose bundled bills by service nature (trading, post-trade, market data, connectivity, membership) before aggregating; the creditor is the venue operator and the collecting organisation (often the CCP) is recorded separately. CCPs and clearing members publicly disclose prices and fees per service, including discounts and rebates (EMIR Art. 38(1), to verify). Confidence high. Firm decision: treatment of market data, connectivity and rebate netting. [05, 02]

<sub>reference LD-A0005</sub>

### Revenue by client (Open, narrowed 2026-09-20 and 2026-11-01, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Use the approved management perspective; specify client attribution, consolidation, internal eliminations, measurement period, reporting currency and any separately defined cost deductions.

**Proposed answer:** Sponsor decision 2026-11-01: execution-only, clearing-only and execute-and-clear are all offered; give-up and give-in are both supported; revenue is attributed per service. Proposed: attribute to the contractual client of each service relationship, show the service mix, roll up to Commercial Group, flag intra-group clients for elimination; mandatory context is observing entity, management basis, period and currency. Open: cost perimeter for Contribution, pass-through and collateral income treatment. [05, 01, 04]

<sub>reference LD-A0014</sub>

### Fee Charge and Commission types (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Validate estimated versus enforceable charges and avoid additive counting of overlapping fee categories.

**Proposed answer:** An estimated charge is a Fee Charge whose amount certainty is "estimated"; billing stage is a facet. Proposed additive key: creditor × service nature × chargeable event; "commission", "execution fee" and bundled labels are non-additive views over it. Confidence high. Firm decision: Finance confirms the additive key. [05, 08]

<sub>reference LD-A0018</sub>

### Volume (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Per side versus round turn versus trades versus notional; single- or double-counted; executed versus cleared; published versus firm data.

**Proposed answer:** State unit (contract side, round turn, trade, notional), counting basis (single or double), stage (executed or cleared) and source (published or firm). Confidence high. [05, 01]
