### Lifecycle Event

*Anything that happens to a trade, position or obligation after it exists and changes it: a take-up, a transfer, an exercise, an expiry, a correction.*

**Definition.** Dated occurrence that creates, changes or ends a trade, position, give-up, obligation or other business object of the trading and clearing lifecycle, or its legal effect, recorded with when it happened, when it takes effect and when it was recorded, plus business dates by role. A correction or cancellation is a new event that references the original; nothing is deleted. Changes to rules, calendars or fee schedules are Rule Changes, not lifecycle events.

**Example.** Client A's long option position in a series at CCP Z is exercised at expiry; the exercise is one lifecycle event, the resulting futures position another.

**Not to be confused with.** Rule Change: a rule change alters the rules or schedules; a lifecycle event alters a trade, position or obligation.

**Breaks down into:** [Execution](../execution/concepts.md#execution) (kind of), [Clearing Acceptance](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#clearing-acceptance) (kind of), [Novation](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#novation) (kind of), [Clearing Member Acceptance](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#clearing-member-acceptance) (kind of), [CCP Interposition](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#ccp-interposition) (kind of), [Contract Adjustment](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#contract-adjustment) (kind of), [Execution Correction](../execution/concepts.md#execution-correction) (kind of), [Position Transfer](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position-transfer) (kind of), [Clearing Member Default](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-member-default) (kind of), [Position Adjustment](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position-adjustment) (kind of), [Client Default](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#client-default) (kind of), [Expiry](#expiry) (kind of), [Exercise](#exercise) (kind of), [Assignment](#assignment) (kind of), [Final Settlement](#final-settlement) (kind of), [Trade Amendment](#trade-amendment) (kind of)

**Note.** Carries occurrence time, effective time and recording time, plus business dates by role. Corrections and cancellations reference the original; nothing is deleted. Event kinds are a taxonomy facet. [07, 03]

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Trade, transaction and execution” (open, answer proposed); “Current, as of, as known at” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Expiry

*The day a contract series ends and every open position in it is closed out.*

**Definition.** Lifecycle event at which a listed series, or an OTC contract, reaches the end of its life on the expiry date set by its contract specification or terms: trading has stopped, and open positions are closed by final settlement (futures), or exercised or abandoned (options), so that no position remains afterwards.

**Example.** The March series of a futures contract at Venue V expires; Client A's remaining positions go to final settlement.

**Not to be confused with.** Last trading day: a date of the contract specification; expiry is the event, which may fall on a later day.

**Kind of:** [Lifecycle Event](#lifecycle-event)

**Values (draft list):** outcome for the position: final settlement (cash or delivery); exercised; assigned; abandoned

**Words people use:** “expiration”; “maturity” (OTC, contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Series, clearing service, expiry date by role and calendar.

**Connected to:**

- *ends* [Instrument](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#instrument)
- *closes* [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position)
- *leads to* [Final Settlement](#final-settlement)
- *may lead to* [Exercise](#exercise)

### Exercise

*The option holder using its right to buy or sell, on instruction or automatically at expiry.*

**Definition.** Lifecycle event by which the holder of a long option position uses its right for a stated quantity under the clearing service's rules: on the holder's instruction (early exercise of an American-style option, or at expiry), or automatically at expiry when the option is in the money by the CCP's threshold and no contrary instruction was given. It creates obligations settled in cash or by delivery (or, for an option on a future, a futures position) and leads to assignment on the short side.

**Example.** Client A's in-the-money calls at CCP Z are exercised automatically at expiry because no contrary instruction was received.

**Not to be confused with.** Assignment: exercise is the long side's act; assignment is its allocation to the short side.

**Kind of:** [Lifecycle Event](#lifecycle-event)

**Breaks down into:** [Exercise Instruction](#exercise-instruction) (step in)

**Values (draft list):** exercise at expiry (automatic or instructed); early exercise (American style)

**Words people use:** “exercise notice” (the instruction, contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Series, style (American, European), CCP rule on automatic exercise and threshold, tier.

**Connected to:**

- [Expiry](#expiry) *may lead to* Exercise
- *reduces the long* [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position)
- *leads to* [Assignment](#assignment)
- *is requested by* [Exercise Instruction](#exercise-instruction)
- *creates* [Settlement Obligation](#settlement-obligation)

### Exercise Instruction

*The holder's order to exercise an option, or to override what would happen automatically at expiry.*

**Definition.** Instruction from the holder of a long option position (a client to its clearing broker, a clearing member to the CCP) to exercise a stated quantity, or at expiry not to exercise or to exercise against the automatic rule (a contrary instruction), before the applicable cut-off. It can be amended or cancelled until that cut-off.

**Example.** Client A tells Clearing Broker Y not to exercise 10 calls that are just in the money, before Y's firm cut-off on expiry day.

**Not to be confused with.** Exercise: the instruction is the request; the exercise is the event registered by the CCP.

**Step in:** [Exercise](#exercise)

**Values (draft list):** exercise; do not exercise (abandon); contrary instruction against automatic exercise

**Words people use:** “exercise notice”; “abandonment instruction”; “contrary instruction”

**Before using it, know:** Holder and tier, series, quantity, applicable market and firm cut-offs.

**Connected to:**

- [Exercise](#exercise) *is requested by* Exercise Instruction
- *must arrive before* [Cut-off](#cut-off)
- *may be subject to* [Firm Cut-off](#firm-cut-off)

### Assignment

*The CCP and then the clearing broker deciding which short option holders must honour an exercise.*

**Definition.** Lifecycle event by which exercised option quantities are allocated to short positions in the same series: first by the CCP to clearing members' accounts under its published method, then by each clearing member to its clients' short positions under its own method. The assigned short must pay or deliver, or takes the resulting futures position.

**Example.** CCP Z assigns 50 exercised puts to Clearing Broker Y's client account; Y assigns 20 of them to Client B under its allocation method.

**Not to be confused with.** Exercise: assignment is the short side's obligation that follows an exercise by someone else.

**Kind of:** [Lifecycle Event](#lifecycle-event)

**Values (draft list):** CCP to member; member to client; random allocation; pro rata allocation; other disclosed method

**Words people use:** “assignment notice”

**Before using it, know:** Tier, series, the CCP method and the member's method.

**Connected to:**

- *follows* [Exercise](#exercise)
- *reduces the short* [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position)
- *creates* [Settlement Obligation](#settlement-obligation)

### Final Settlement

*Closing an expiring position for good, either by paying the difference in cash or by delivering the underlying.*

**Definition.** Lifecycle event that closes an expiring futures position, or an exercised or assigned option position, by cash settlement against a final settlement price or by physical delivery of the underlying, creating the settlement obligations concerned. Cash or physical settlement is a term of the contract specification.

**Example.** Client A's remaining long futures in the expiring series are cash settled against the final settlement price published by Venue V.

**Not to be confused with.** Variation margin: daily settlement while the position is open; final settlement closes it at the end.

**Kind of:** [Lifecycle Event](#lifecycle-event)

**Breaks down into:** [Delivery Notice](#delivery-notice) (step in)

**Values (draft list):** cash settlement; physical delivery

**Words people use:** “delivery” (physical, contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Series, settlement method, final settlement price source, tier.

**Connected to:**

- [Expiry](#expiry) *leads to* Final Settlement
- *uses the final kind of* [Settlement Price](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#settlement-price)

### Delivery Notice

*The notice that starts the physical delivery of a futures contract.*

**Definition.** Notice, for a physically delivered futures contract, by which a short position holder declares its intention to deliver (and, where the rules require, a long holder states its readiness or preferences), within the notice period set by the contract specification, after which the CCP allocates the delivery to long positions.

**Example.** Client B, short a physically delivered commodity future at CCP Z, gives notice of intention to deliver through Clearing Broker Y on a notice day.

**Not to be confused with.** Exercise Instruction: exercise is an option holder's right; a delivery notice starts the delivery of a future.

**Step in:** [Final Settlement](#final-settlement)

**Values (draft list):** notice of intention to deliver; delivery allocation to longs

**Words people use:** “notice of intention”; “tender” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Contract, notice period dates by role (first notice day), tier.

**Connected to:**

- *must arrive before* [Cut-off](#cut-off)
- *is governed by* [Contract Specification](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#contract-specification)

### Trade Amendment

*A change to, or early ending of, a trade that already exists, recorded as a new event that points to the original.*

**Definition.** Lifecycle event that changes or ends a trade registered for clearing, made under the clearing service's rules or by agreement of the parties, referring to the original, which is kept: change of economic terms where allowed (mainly OTC), partial termination, full termination (unwind), or correction of a non-economic attribute that does not move the trade at the CCP (for example internal client account or client reference). For a CCP-facing trade, an economic change or termination ends or reduces the trade and creates a linked successor; only a non-economic correction is recorded against the same trade. A move of positions to another CCP account or clearing member is a Position Transfer or a give-up step, not an amendment; a correction from the venue's error-trade rules or the broker's client-facing correction is an Execution Correction.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y corrects the client reference on a registered trade at CCP Z; a cleared swap of Client A is partially terminated, reducing its notional.

**Not to be confused with.** Execution Correction: corrects a venue execution before clearing; a trade amendment acts on the registered trade.

**Kind of:** [Lifecycle Event](#lifecycle-event)

**Values (draft list):** non-economic correction; economic change; partial termination; full termination (unwind)

**Words people use:** “amendment”; “unwind” (OTC); “termination” (OTC); “trade correction” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing tier of the trade, whether a successor trade is created, which source records the change.

**Connected to:**

- *changes or ends* [Trade](../execution/concepts.md#trade)
- *may change* [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position)
- *may raise* [Operational Exception](#operational-exception)

### Cut-off

*The latest time a step can be done, with the calendar it is read against, what happens if it is missed, and who set it.*

**Definition.** Rule fixing the latest time by which a stated process step must be completed, set by a rule provision (regulation, CCP or venue rulebook, payment system rule, firm procedure) or by a term of an agreement, the source kind being recorded on each cut-off; expressed either as a local time on a business date of a named business calendar, read against the relevant processing window, or as a period after a stated triggering event; it states the consequence of missing it (rejection, lapse, late handling, charge, default procedure) and the authority that sets it. Only cut-offs set by a rule provision follow the Rule Change lifecycle. Versioned; on a given day it resolves to one exact time, which can move with early closes or extended trading hours. — *meaning changed in 2026-11, accepted as Draft*

**Example.** CCP Z's deadline for taking up give-ups is a stated local time on the clearing date; a line not taken up by then lapses and stays with the giving member.

**Not to be confused with.** Business Calendar: the calendar says which days count; the cut-off says the latest time on such a day and the consequence of missing it.

**Breaks down into:** [Take-up Deadline](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#take-up-deadline) (kind of), [Settlement Deadline](#settlement-deadline) (kind of), [Firm Cut-off](#firm-cut-off) (kind of)

**Words people use:** “deadline” (Process steps)

**Connected to:**

- *applies to* [Allocation](../execution/concepts.md#allocation), [Give-up / Take-up Process](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#give-up--take-up-process), [Clearing Acceptance](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#clearing-acceptance), [Margin Call](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#margin-call), [Settlement Obligation](#settlement-obligation) — A step of the process or obligation concerned; versioned by supersession.
- *relative to* [Business Calendar](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#business-calendar) — Deadline in local time against the named calendar's business date.
- [Trading Session](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#trading-session) *positions cut offs relative to* Cut-off
- [Processing Window](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#processing-window) *frames* Cut-off
- [Allocation Line](../execution/concepts.md#allocation-line) *must be completed by* Cut-off
- [Position Adjustment](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position-adjustment) *must be done before* Cut-off
- [Margin Call Terms](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#margin-call-terms) *names the deadline as* Cut-off
- [Exercise Instruction](#exercise-instruction) *must arrive before* Cut-off
- [Delivery Notice](#delivery-notice) *must arrive before* Cut-off
- *set by* [Rule Provision](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#rule-provision), [Agreement](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#agreement) — A cut-off is set by a rule provision or by a term of an agreement (sponsor decision 2026-11-01); only rule-set cut-offs follow the rule change lifecycle.

### Settlement Deadline

*The latest time a payment or delivery must reach the other side.*

**Definition.** Cut-off by which a settlement obligation must be settled, set by the creditor's rules (CCP rulebook, payment system rules) or by a term of an agreement (for example a client clearing agreement); a margin call deadline is one. Its place under Cut-off depends on the sponsor's decision on the Cut-off meaning change.

**Example.** CCP Z requires its morning cash call in euro to be settled by a stated local time on the clearing date.

**Not to be confused with.** Margin Call: the call is the demand; the settlement deadline is the time by which the resulting obligation must be met.

**Kind of:** [Cut-off](#cut-off)

**Values (draft list):** CCP payment deadline (per cycle); payment system closing time; client payment deadline; delivery deadline

**Words people use:** “payment deadline”; “settlement cut-off”

**Before using it, know:** Calendar, time zone, currency, cycle, the authority that sets it, the consequence.

**Connected to:**

- [Settlement Fail](#settlement-fail) *is assessed against* Settlement Deadline
- *applies to* [Settlement Obligation](#settlement-obligation)
- *is set for the obligation created by* [Margin Call](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#margin-call)
- *is read against* [Business Calendar](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#business-calendar)

### Firm Cut-off

*An earlier deadline the firm gives clients or its own teams so that the market deadline can be met.*

**Definition.** Cut-off set by the observing firm, in its own procedure or in a client agreement, for a client or an internal team ahead of the market cut-off it protects (for example an allocation, give-up, take-up, exercise-instruction, delivery or payment deadline), so that the firm can complete its own step in time. Its place under Cut-off depends on the sponsor's decision on the Cut-off meaning change.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y asks clients to send contrary exercise instructions well before CCP Z's exercise deadline on expiry day.

**Not to be confused with.** Market cut-off (set by a CCP, venue or payment system): binds the member; the firm cut-off binds the client or team.

**Kind of:** [Cut-off](#cut-off)

**Words people use:** “client cut-off”; “internal deadline”

**Before using it, know:** Which market cut-off it protects, who it binds, calendar and time zone, consequence.

**Connected to:**

- *may be set in* [Client Clearing Agreement](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#client-clearing-agreement)
- *may apply to* [Allocation Instruction](../execution/concepts.md#allocation-instruction)
- *may apply to* [Exercise Instruction](#exercise-instruction)

### Settlement Obligation

*Something that must be paid or delivered, by whom, to whom, by when and why.*

**Definition.** Duty of an identified debtor toward an identified creditor to transfer a stated amount of cash in a currency, or a stated quantity of an asset, by a due date and settlement deadline through a stated settlement route, arising from an identified source: variation margin settled to market, price alignment, option premium, final cash settlement, physical delivery, a margin call to be met in cash or assets, a fee invoice or interest. It can be settled in part and netted with other obligations into one payment. Distinct from the requirement or charge it comes from and from the cash movement that settles it.

**Example.** After the end-of-day run, Clearing Broker Y owes CCP Z variation margin in euro, due in the next settlement cycle.

**Not to be confused with.** Cash Movement: the obligation is what is owed; the cash movement is what was actually paid.

**Breaks down into:** [Netted Settlement Obligation](#netted-settlement-obligation) (kind of), [Settlement Fail](#settlement-fail) (state of), [Payment Instruction](#payment-instruction) (step in)

**Note.** Purpose facet includes price alignment (never part of VM) and cash or physical settlement. [04, 02]

**Connected to:**

- [Cash Movement](#cash-movement) *settles* Settlement Obligation — May be partial; net payment may cover several obligations.
- [Variation Margin Requirement](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#variation-margin-requirement) *gives rise to* Settlement Obligation — Under settled-to-market treatment; under collateralised-to-market treatment VM is demanded by a margin call and met with collateral. Treatment is a versioned fact per clearing service and account type (the open question “Variation margin”).
- [Average Price Residual](../execution/concepts.md#average-price-residual) *may give rise to a settlement obligation settled by a cash movement* Settlement Obligation
- [Price Alignment Amount](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#price-alignment-amount) *gives rise to* Settlement Obligation
- [Cash Collateral Interest](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#cash-collateral-interest) *gives rise to* Settlement Obligation
- [Fee Invoice](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#fee-invoice) *gives rise to* Settlement Obligation
- [Settlement Deadline](#settlement-deadline) *applies to* Settlement Obligation

- [Assignment](#assignment) *creates* Settlement Obligation
- [Option Premium](#option-premium) *gives rise to* Settlement Obligation — Premium-style options; futures-style options carry no upfront premium.

### Netted Settlement Obligation

*One payment that covers many amounts due between the same two parties on the same day in the same currency.*

**Definition.** Settlement obligation formed by netting several settlement obligations between the same two parties, in either direction (for example between a CCP and a clearing member, or a clearing member and a client), in the same currency, with the same due date and settlement route, into one net amount payable in one direction, under the agreement or clearing service rule that allows that netting. Settling it settles each underlying obligation, which keep their own purpose and amount.

**Example.** CCP Z nets Clearing Broker Y's variation margin, option premium and fees in euro for the cycle into one amount Y must pay.

**Not to be confused with.** Settlement Obligation in general: a single obligation has one purpose; the netted one combines several and must not hide them.

**Kind of:** [Settlement Obligation](#settlement-obligation)

**Words people use:** “net cash call”; “net settlement amount”; “daily settlement” (CCP, contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Parties and tier, currency, cycle, settlement route, list of underlying obligations.

**Connected to:**

- *is settled by* [Cash Movement](#cash-movement)
- *is stated in* [CCP Report](#ccp-report)

### Settlement Fail

*An amount or asset that should have been paid or delivered by the deadline and was not.*

**Definition.** The record of one settlement obligation not settled in full by its settlement deadline: the unsettled amount or quantity, the deadline missed, its age, and how it ended (settled late, cancelled, replaced by a close-out or default procedure). A partial settlement leaves the unsettled part in fail. The general settlement status of an obligation (pending, settled, partially settled, failed, cancelled, unknown) is a value list on Settlement Obligation, not this concept.

**Example.** Client A's variation margin payment to Clearing Broker Y due by the client deadline on date D is still unpaid at the deadline: settlement fail.

**Not to be confused with.** An overdue Margin Call: for a call met in cash, 'overdue' on the call and 'failed' on the resulting obligation are two views of one situation, counted once. Also not a rejected or lapsed give-up or a reconciliation break.

**State of:** [Settlement Obligation](#settlement-obligation)

**Values (draft list):** end: settled late; cancelled; replaced by close-out or default procedure; still open

**Words people use:** “fail”; “failed payment”; “failed delivery”; “late payment” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Obligation, its settlement deadline and calendar, the source asserting non-settlement, tier.

**Connected to:**

- *is assessed against* [Settlement Deadline](#settlement-deadline)
- *may raise* [Operational Exception](#operational-exception)

### Payment Instruction

*The order given to a bank or system to make a payment.*

**Definition.** Instruction given by or for the payer (or, under a direct debit arrangement, by the payee) to a settlement bank, payment system or the firm's own ledger, to transfer a stated amount in a currency on a value date in order to settle one or more settlement obligations. It can be released, rejected, amended or cancelled; it is not the cash movement it asks for.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y's settlement bank receives CCP Z's direct debit for the net euro amount of the morning cycle.

**Not to be confused with.** Cash Movement: the instruction asks for the payment; the cash movement is the payment that happened.

**Step in:** [Settlement Obligation](#settlement-obligation)

**Values (draft list):** created; released; rejected; cancelled; executed

**Words people use:** “payment order”; “SSI” (standing settlement instructions, contextual: the standing details, not the instruction); “direct debit” (CCP)

**Before using it, know:** Payer, payee, settlement account, currency, value date, settlement route.

**Connected to:**

- *carries out for cash collateral* [Collateral Instruction](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#collateral-instruction)
- [Settlement Account](#settlement-account) *is named in* Payment Instruction

### Cash Movement

*Money that actually moved from one account to another, with the evidence that says so.*

**Definition.** Transfer of funds in one currency, of a stated amount and value date, from a payer to a payee, evidenced by an identified source: a payment system, settlement bank or CCP for an external payment, or the firm's books for a transfer between accounts the firm maintains. Distinct from the settlement obligation it settles (it may settle several netted obligations, or part of one) and from the payment instruction that asked for it. A cash collateral transfer is both a cash movement and a collateral movement and is counted once.

**Example.** The settlement bank's statement shows Clearing Broker Y's payment of the net euro amount to CCP Z on the value date.

**Not to be confused with.** Payment Instruction: the instruction asks for the payment; the cash movement is the payment that happened.

**Connected to:**

- *settles* [Settlement Obligation](#settlement-obligation) — May be partial; net payment may cover several obligations.
- [Settlement or Custody Agent](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#settlement-or-custody-agent) *evidences* Cash Movement
- [Fee Rebate](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#fee-rebate) *is settled by* Cash Movement
- [Fee Invoice](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#fee-invoice) *is settled by* Cash Movement
- [Netted Settlement Obligation](#netted-settlement-obligation) *is settled by* Cash Movement
- [Settlement Account](#settlement-account) *is debited or credited by* Cash Movement

### Operational Exception

*A problem someone must act on, because something expected did not happen or a rule was not met.*

**Definition.** Situation, raised against one or more identified business objects (trade, give-up line, position, settlement obligation, cash movement, reconciliation break), in which an expected outcome or rule has not been met and which needs action by an identified owner, usually before a stated deadline; it holds from when it is raised until it is resolved or closed. A reconciliation break, a rejected or lapsed give-up, a missed cut-off or a settlement fail can raise one but is not one. Its handling is recorded in an exception case. Technology incidents are out of scope.

**Example.** A give-up line to Clearing Broker Y lapsed at the cut-off; operations raises an exception to agree with Client A how to rebook the 40 lots.

**Not to be confused with.** Reconciliation Break: a break says two records differ; it becomes an exception only if someone must act on it.

**Note.** Type State kept. Exceptions are usually handled as cases with owner, priority and lifecycle; the case is a record of handling the exception. A break or a rejected give-up may raise one but is not one (the open question “Break, exception, reject, fail”). [07]

**Connected to:**

- [Reconciliation](#reconciliation) *identifies* Operational Exception — A reconciliation identifies discrepancies (breaks); only some are handled as operational exceptions, and a break does not prove underlying trade failure.
- [Execution Correction](../execution/concepts.md#execution-correction) *may raise* Operational Exception
- [Fee Adjustment](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#fee-adjustment) *may resolve* Operational Exception
- [Reconciliation Break](#reconciliation-break) *may raise* Operational Exception

- [Settlement Fail](#settlement-fail) *may raise* Operational Exception
- [Trade Amendment](#trade-amendment) *may raise* Operational Exception

### Reconciliation

*Checking, for a given date, that two sources tell the same story about the same trades, positions or amounts, and listing where they do not.*

**Definition.** Process of comparing two or more records of the same business objects held by different sources (for example the firm's books and a CCP report), of a stated type (trade, give-up, position, cash, margin, collateral, fee), scope, business date by role and cycle, using stated matching keys and tolerances, to establish which items match and which differ (reconciliation breaks). It does not by itself decide which record is right; where a rule names an authoritative record, the difference is still reported as a break, never silently resolved.

**Example.** Each morning operations compares its end-of-day positions per CCP account with CCP Z's position report for the previous clearing date.

**Not to be confused with.** Operational Exception: reconciliation finds differences; an exception is the situation someone must then handle.

**Breaks down into:** [Position Reconciliation](#position-reconciliation) (kind of), [Reconciliation Break](#reconciliation-break) (part of)

**Connected to:**

- *identifies* [Operational Exception](#operational-exception) — A reconciliation identifies discrepancies (breaks); only some are handled as operational exceptions, and a break does not prove underlying trade failure.
- [Listed Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#listed-position) *is compared in position reconciliation* Reconciliation
- [Fee Invoice](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#fee-invoice) *is checked by a fee reconciliation* Reconciliation
- [Source Record](#source-record) *is compared in* Reconciliation
- [CCP Report](#ccp-report) *is the counterpart record in* Reconciliation

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Break, exception, reject, fail” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Position Reconciliation

*Checking that the positions in our books match the CCP's (or the client's) for each account and series on a clearing date.*

**Definition.** Reconciliation of open positions between the observing firm's books and a CCP report (at the member tier) or a client's or another broker's records (at the client tier), per CCP account, CCP position account or internal account, series of the clearing contract, position basis (gross long and short, or net), clearing date and processing window.

**Example.** Operations compares Clearing Broker Y's long and short positions per series in its client omnibus account at CCP Z with CCP Z's position report for date D.

**Not to be confused with.** Trade reconciliation: compares individual trades; positions can match while trades differ, and the reverse.

**Kind of:** [Reconciliation](#reconciliation)

**Words people use:** “position rec”; “open position reconciliation”

**Before using it, know:** CCP account or internal account, series, gross or net basis, clearing date and cycle, tier.

- *uses* [CCP Report](#ccp-report)
- *compares* [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position)
- *is keyed by* [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account)
- *produces* [Reconciliation Break](#reconciliation-break)

### Reconciliation Break

*A mismatch between two records that should agree.*

**Definition.** A difference, found by a reconciliation, between records of the same business object held by different sources, or a record present in one source and missing in the other, that exceeds the stated tolerance. It says the records disagree, not which one is right. It holds from detection until it is explained and cleared, and keeps its age, the records concerned and, once known, its cause. A break may raise an operational exception; it is not one, and it does not prove that the underlying trade or payment failed.

**Example.** The firm's books show 100 lots long for Client A at CCP Z; the CCP position report shows 60: a 40-lot quantity break on that account and clearing date.

**Not to be confused with.** Operational Exception: the break is the difference; the exception is the situation someone must act on, if any.

**Part of:** [Reconciliation](#reconciliation)

**Values (draft list):** missing in one source; quantity difference; price or amount difference; account or attribution difference; status difference; date difference; open; under investigation; explained, awaiting correction; resolved; written off where allowed

**Words people use:** “break”; “mismatch”; “unmatched item” (contextual); “difference”

**Before using it, know:** Reconciliation type, business date by role and cycle, the sources compared, tolerance applied.

**Connected to:**

- *concerns two or more* [Source Record](#source-record)
- *may raise* [Operational Exception](#operational-exception)
- *may be handled in* [Exception Case](#exception-case)
- [Position Reconciliation](#position-reconciliation) *produces* Reconciliation Break

### Source Record

*One source's version of a trade, position or amount, with its own references and status.*

**Definition.** A record of a business object (trade, give-up line, position, margin requirement, margin call, settlement obligation, cash movement, access arrangement status) as held or reported by one identified source (a function of the observing firm's own books, a CCP, a venue, an executing or clearing broker, a settlement bank or a client), with that source's identifiers, native status and record time. Every harmonised status in the ontology is asserted in a source record, with the native status kept beside it; other pages refer to this rule instead of restating it. New level-1 concept promoting the backlog item.

**Example.** Client A's 100-lot trade appears in the firm's clearing books and in CCP Z's trade register: two source records of one trade.

**Not to be confused with.** Business object (Trade, Position): the object exists once; each source holds its own record of it.

**Breaks down into:** [CCP Report](#ccp-report) (kind of), [Client Statement](#client-statement) (kind of)

**Values (draft list):** firm books (by function: front office, middle office, clearing back office); CCP report; venue report; executing broker statement; clearing broker statement; settlement bank or payment system statement; client record or instruction

**Words people use:** “book of record” (when a rule makes the record authoritative); “statement” (broker, bank or CCP); “report” (CCP or venue)

**Before using it, know:** The source, its record time and version, and whether a rule makes it authoritative for the question asked.

### CCP Report

*The daily (or intraday) statement a CCP sends a clearing member about its trades, positions, margin and payments.*

**Definition.** Source record issued by a CCP to a clearing member for one clearing service, a set of CCP accounts and a clearing date (and cycle, where the CCP runs several), stating the trades, give-up and take-up results, positions, exercises and assignments, margin requirements, collateral and cash settlement amounts as the CCP has recorded them. It is the authoritative record for give-up completion (sponsor decision); for other questions its authority is a stated rule per question.

**Example.** CCP Z's end-of-day position report for Clearing Broker Y's client omnibus account, for clearing date D.

**Not to be confused with.** Source Record in general: the firm's own books and broker statements are records too, but not CCP reports.

**Kind of:** [Source Record](#source-record)

**Values (draft list):** trade register; give-up and take-up report; position report; exercise and assignment report; delivery report; margin report; collateral report; cash settlement statement

**Words people use:** “CCP statement”; “clearing report”; “end-of-day report” (CCP)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service, CCP accounts covered, clearing date and cycle, report version (CCPs reissue corrected reports).

### Client Statement

*The daily or monthly report a broker sends a client on its accounts.*

**Definition.** Source record issued by a clearing broker or executing broker to a client for one or more of the client's accounts and a business date or period (daily, monthly), stating as the broker has recorded them the trades and executions, positions, cash balance, open trade equity and account equity, margin requirement and excess or shortfall, fees, commissions and cash movements. Its minimum content and frequency are set partly by regulation (to verify per regime) and by the client agreement. It is the broker's record toward the client, not the CCP's record (CCP Report), and not a demand for payment (Fee Invoice).

**Example.** Each morning Client A receives from Clearing Broker Y a statement of yesterday's trades, positions, cash, equity and margin excess.

**Not to be confused with.** CCP Report: what the CCP reports to its member; the client statement is what the broker reports to its client.

**Kind of:** [Source Record](#source-record)

**Values (draft list):** daily; monthly; other periodic

**Words people use:** “monthly statement” (client usage); “daily statement” (broker to client)

**Before using it, know:** Issuer, client, accounts covered, business date or period.

**Connected to:**

- [Source Record](#source-record) *has as a kind* Client Statement
- *is issued by* [Broker](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#broker) — Clearing or executing broker, to its client.
- *states* [Account Equity](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#account-equity) — Also states the cash balance, open trade equity, margin requirement and excess or shortfall.

### Exception Case

*The ticket that tracks who is fixing an exception, what was done and when it was closed.*

**Definition.** The record of handling one or more operational exceptions: owner, priority, the business objects and counterparties concerned, actions taken, handling state, deadline and outcome. One case can group several exceptions with the same cause. Closing a case does not change any source record; corrections are made by new lifecycle events or source corrections.

**Example.** One case groups the three give-up lines to Clearing Broker Y that lapsed on the same clearing date because the give-up reference was wrong.

**Not to be confused with.** Operational Exception: the exception is the situation; the case is the record of handling it.

**Values (draft list):** open; assigned; in progress; waiting on counterparty; resolved; closed

**Words people use:** “case”; “ticket”; “query” (with a counterparty, contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Owner role, priority scheme, deadline, handling state source.

**Connected to:**

- *handles* [Operational Exception](#operational-exception) — One case can handle several exceptions. Corrected 2026-11-09 (was part_of).

### Settlement Account

*The bank or delivery account a party uses to pay or receive what it owes.*

**Definition.** Account, maintained for a party by a settlement bank, payment system, CCP, custodian or triparty agent, through which the party settles cash obligations in a currency (cash settlement account), delivers and receives securities or commodities (delivery account), or holds or pledges collateral (collateral account: custody, pledge or triparty). The account is real; the observing firm holds its identifier. Not a client's cash account kept in the broker's own books, which is a Client Account, and not the CCP account in which positions are recorded.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y settles its sterling obligations to CCP Z through its cash account at an approved settlement bank.

**Not to be confused with.** CCP Account: positions and margin are recorded in the CCP account; cash moves through the settlement account.

**Kind of:** [Account](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#account)

**Values (draft list):** cash settlement account; delivery account; collateral account (custody, pledge or triparty)

**Words people use:** “settlement bank account”; “nostro” (contextual, firm's own cash account); “SSI account”

**Before using it, know:** Holder, maintaining institution, currency or asset, service it settles.

**Connected to:**

### Option Premium

*The price paid for an option, by the buyer to the seller.*

**Definition.** Amount the buyer of an option pays the seller for it: the trade price times quantity and contract multiplier. For premium-style listed options it is paid in full on the settlement of the trade, through the CCP and down each clearing tier, giving rise to settlement obligations; for futures-style options no premium is paid upfront and the option's value moves through variation margin instead. For OTC options the premium amount and payment date are terms of the trade. Premium paid or received is posted to the account's cash balance.

**Example.** Client A buys options on Exchange X; the premium is paid the next day through CCP Z and debited from its cash balance.

**Not to be confused with.** Option value: the premium is paid once at the trade price; the option's value then changes every day.

**Values (draft list):** premium-style (paid upfront); futures-style (no upfront premium)

**Words people use:** “premium” (options)

**Before using it, know:** Premium-style or futures-style option; payer and payee view; clearing tier.

**Connected to:**

- *gives rise to* [Settlement Obligation](#settlement-obligation) — Premium-style options; futures-style options carry no upfront premium.
- *is owed on* [Trade](../execution/concepts.md#trade) — The option trade.
