# Post-trade operations

> Generated by `tools/build_domain_views.py`; edit `overview.md`, `company-questions.md` or the registers instead.

Post-trade operations is everything that happens to a trade after it is done and handed to clearing: the events that change it and the positions it creates, the deadlines each step must meet, the money and assets that must move, the checks that records agree, and the problems someone has to fix. It matters because this is where a missed deadline becomes a charge, a complaint or a risk, and where every status an operations agent reports comes from.

## Concepts at a glance

- **[Lifecycle Event](concepts.md#lifecycle-event)** — Anything that happens to a trade, position or obligation after it exists and changes it: a take-up, a transfer, an exercise, an expiry, a correction.
  - **[Expiry](concepts.md#expiry)** — The day a contract series ends and every open position in it is closed out.
  - **[Exercise](concepts.md#exercise)** — The option holder using its right to buy or sell, on instruction or automatically at expiry.
    - **[Exercise Instruction](concepts.md#exercise-instruction)** — The holder's order to exercise an option, or to override what would happen automatically at expiry.
  - **[Assignment](concepts.md#assignment)** — The CCP and then the clearing broker deciding which short option holders must honour an exercise.
  - **[Final Settlement](concepts.md#final-settlement)** — Closing an expiring position for good, either by paying the difference in cash or by delivering the underlying.
    - **[Delivery Notice](concepts.md#delivery-notice)** — The notice that starts the physical delivery of a futures contract.
  - **[Trade Amendment](concepts.md#trade-amendment)** — A change to, or early ending of, a trade that already exists, recorded as a new event that points to the original.
- **[Cut-off](concepts.md#cut-off)** — The latest time a step can be done, with the calendar it is read against, what happens if it is missed, and who set it.
  - **[Settlement Deadline](concepts.md#settlement-deadline)** — The latest time a payment or delivery must reach the other side.
  - **[Firm Cut-off](concepts.md#firm-cut-off)** — An earlier deadline the firm gives clients or its own teams so that the market deadline can be met.
- **[Settlement Obligation](concepts.md#settlement-obligation)** — Something that must be paid or delivered, by whom, to whom, by when and why.
  - **[Netted Settlement Obligation](concepts.md#netted-settlement-obligation)** — One payment that covers many amounts due between the same two parties on the same day in the same currency.
  - **[Settlement Fail](concepts.md#settlement-fail)** — An amount or asset that should have been paid or delivered by the deadline and was not.
  - **[Payment Instruction](concepts.md#payment-instruction)** — The order given to a bank or system to make a payment.
- **[Cash Movement](concepts.md#cash-movement)** — Money that actually moved from one account to another, with the evidence that says so.
- **[Operational Exception](concepts.md#operational-exception)** — A problem someone must act on, because something expected did not happen or a rule was not met.
  - **[Exception Case](concepts.md#exception-case)** — The ticket that tracks who is fixing an exception, what was done and when it was closed.
- **[Reconciliation](concepts.md#reconciliation)** — Checking, for a given date, that two sources tell the same story about the same trades, positions or amounts, and listing where they do not.
  - **[Position Reconciliation](concepts.md#position-reconciliation)** — Checking that the positions in our books match the CCP's (or the client's) for each account and series on a clearing date.
  - **[Reconciliation Break](concepts.md#reconciliation-break)** — A mismatch between two records that should agree.
- **[Source Record](concepts.md#source-record)** — One source's version of a trade, position or amount, with its own references and status.
  - **[CCP Report](concepts.md#ccp-report)** — The daily (or intraday) statement a CCP sends a clearing member about its trades, positions, margin and payments.
  - **[Client Statement](concepts.md#client-statement)** — The daily or monthly report a broker sends a client on its accounts.
- **[Settlement Account](concepts.md#settlement-account)** — The bank or delivery account a party uses to pay or receive what it owes.
- **[Option Premium](concepts.md#option-premium)** — The price paid for an option, by the buyer to the seller.
