## Usage notes and required context

- **LD-C0011 Segregation Model**
  - Note: Public instances, not subclasses: EMIR omnibus and individual client segregation (Art. 39); indirect-clearing account structures (Delegated Regulations (EU) 2017/2154 for listed and 2017/2155 for OTC); US futures-customer segregation, cleared-swaps LSOC (17 CFR Part 22) and foreign-futures secured amount (Part 30); paragraphs to verify. State the level (CCP or member books). Legal protection is a Legal conclusion. [02, 03, 04, 06]
- **LD-C0028 Settlement Obligation**
  - Note: Purpose facet includes price alignment (never part of VM) and cash or physical settlement. [04, 02]
- **LD-C0043 Margin Requirement**
  - An agent must know: Margin provider and taker (tier), account, component, methodology version, run type, as-of time, currency basis
- **LD-C0044 Initial Margin Requirement**
  - Note: A member–client IM derived from the CCP figure is still IM at the member–client tier. US futures "initial" and "maintenance" are two call-trigger levels of one requirement. [04]
- **LD-C0045 Variation Margin Requirement**
  - Note: Price alignment is a separate flow. Premium-style options carry no VM; futures-style options do. [04, 03]
- **LD-C0046 Margin Call**
  - Note: A return of excess on request is a Collateral Movement, not a negative call; VM paid out by a CCP is a payment, not a call. [04]
- **LD-C0048 Collateral Asset**
  - Note: Cash in a given currency is a collateral asset. Eligibility holds only against a version of a Collateral Eligibility Schedule (LD-R0072). [04]

## Ambiguities and proposed answers

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### LD-A0002 — Fee, revenue and cash received (Open, narrowed 2026-09-20, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate charges, management revenue and cash settlement; define timing, gross/net, pass-through treatment and the boundary with contribution after costs.

**Proposed answer:** A charge accrues at its chargeable event (LD-R0078), is then invoiced or debited and finally settled (LD-C0029); amount certainty (estimated or final) and billing stage are facets of LD-C0031. Collateral income is a separate stream whose inclusion in clearing revenue is undecided. Gross or net treatment of pass-through charges is a Finance decision; accounting principal-versus-agent guidance is only an analogue. Confidence high on stages, medium on treatment. [05, 04, 02]

### LD-A0007 — Account (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify function, maintaining party, holder, beneficiary and account-to-account relationships.

**Proposed answer:** Three levels: the CCP Account (LD-C0069) of each clearing service; the member's internal client account; execution-side and give-up references, which are identifiers rather than accounts. Levels are related only by explicit, time-bounded LD-R0065. Segregation regime and regulatory account statuses are separate, time-bounded dimensions. Confidence high. [02, 07, 06]

### LD-A0011 — Margin required (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Distinguish IM/VM, member–CCP/client–member relationship, risk scope, time and collateral coverage.

**Proposed answer:** Undefined until five qualifiers are fixed: tier (from margin provider and taker role assignments: CCP–member house, CCP–member client account, member–client, client–indirect client); component (IM, VM, add-ons, total); account and gross or net basis; as-of time and run; currency basis. Under net omnibus accounts a client-level figure exists only in the member's own calculation. Member–client IM is a CCP replication, a replication with multiplier or add-on, or an own model (LD-C0070). Confidence high. Firm decision: default tier, if any. [04]

### LD-A0012 — Variation margin (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify collateral versus settlement treatment under the governing arrangement.

**Proposed answer:** Settled-to-market: the daily payment settles exposure (LD-R0067) and is accompanied by a price alignment amount. Collateralised-to-market: VM is collateral to be returned, with price alignment interest. The treatment is a versioned fact per clearing service and account type; some OTC CCPs converted some or all accounts to settled-to-market (dates per service to verify). Price alignment is never part of VM. Confidence high on concepts, medium on per-CCP facts. Legal/Finance decision per service for internal reporting. [04, 03, 06]

### LD-A0027 — Margin and initial margin (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** "Margin" as requirement versus collateral held or posted; CCP initial margin versus US futures initial and maintenance call levels versus uncleared bilateral initial margin.

**Proposed answer:** "Client margin held" usually means collateral (LD-C0047); "margin required" means LD-C0043; "margin posted" means delivered collateral. US futures initial and maintenance levels are two call-trigger levels of one requirement, not two requirement types. Initial margin under uncleared bilateral margin rules is out of scope and must not resolve to LD-C0044. Confidence high. [04]

### LD-A0028 — Omnibus, house and client (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** EMIR omnibus (net or gross) versus US customer omnibus versus indirect omnibus versus internal pooled account; CCP account type versus economic relationship of affiliates.

**Proposed answer:** EMIR omnibus and individual client segregation (Art. 39(2) and (3), to verify), US futures-customer omnibus, indirect-client omnibus account types (Delegated Regulations (EU) 2017/2154 and 2017/2155, to verify) and internal pooled accounts are different things; the CCP account type does not determine the economic relationship (affiliates may be cleared as clients or in the house account). Confidence high. [02, 04, 03]

### LD-A0030 — Client clearing model (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Combination of access model, legal model, segregation model and margining basis; model offered versus legal protection or portability actually achieved.

**Proposed answer:** Never resolve to a single concept: decompose into access model (LD-C0059), legal model (LD-C0056), segregation model (LD-C0011) and margining basis (LD-C0069). The model offered says nothing by itself about legal protection or portability achieved, which only Legal concludes. Confidence high. [05, 06]

### LD-A0034 — Break, exception, reject, fail (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Reconciliation break versus operational exception versus rejected or lapsed give-up versus settlement fail versus technology incident.

**Proposed answer:** A reconciliation break says records disagree, not which is wrong; an operational exception (LD-C0030) is a situation requiring handling and may be raised by a break; a rejected or lapsed give-up is an event and may raise an exception but is not a trade failure; a settlement fail concerns LD-C0028; a technology incident is out of scope. Confidence high. [07, 02]
