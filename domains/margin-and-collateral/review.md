## 1. Concepts you approve

| Concept | Definition | Your verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **[Margin Methodology](concepts.md#margin-methodology)** | Versioned rules by which a margin taker determines margin requirements for a scope: the model family, confidence level, close-out period, look-back, offsets recognised, add-ons and anti-procyclicality measures, together with the parameter set the model runs on, which is versioned separately because it changes more often. At the member-to-client tier the methodology is a replication of the CCP's, a replication with a multiplier or add-on, or the margin taker's own model. | |
| **[Margin Requirement](concepts.md#margin-requirement)** | Amount of margin that a margin provider must provide to a margin taker for an identified account or risk scope, stated by tier, component, run and as-of time, and currency basis, and determined under an identified margin methodology or contractual rule. For variation margin the amount is paid by whichever party the value has moved against, which can be the margin taker (for example a CCP paying a clearing member); the provider and taker roles stay those set by the clearing tier. It is an amount owed in principle, distinct from the margin call that demands it and the collateral that covers it. | |
| **[Initial Margin Requirement](concepts.md#initial-margin-requirement)** | Margin requirement intended to cover the loss the margin taker could suffer in closing out or hedging the provider's positions after its default, over an assumed close-out period at a stated confidence level under the applicable margin methodology, including the add-ons that methodology applies. Where a CCP reports the current liquidation value of premium-style options inside its initial margin total, that amount is recorded as a separate component. | |
| **[Variation Margin Requirement](concepts.md#variation-margin-requirement)** | Margin requirement reflecting the current market value of positions, owed by the party the value has moved against: under settled-to-market treatment, the change in value since the previous margin cycle, paid outright as a settlement of the exposure rather than posted as collateral; under collateralised-to-market treatment, the cumulative mark-to-market exposure, covered by collateral that is returned as the exposure falls. For listed contracts positions are valued at the daily settlement price. Direction is stated from the observing party. Price alignment is a separate amount and never part of variation margin. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[Margin Call](concepts.md#margin-call)** | Demand issued by a margin taker to a margin provider, from a scheduled end-of-day, scheduled intraday or ad hoc margin run, for an amount due by a stated deadline in a stated calendar and time zone, to cover the part of one or more margin requirements not covered by collateral already held after haircuts, subject to thresholds, minimum transfer amount and rounding. One call may combine several requirements and currencies, and a CCP may collect it by direct debit. Which collateral can cover a requirement depends on its type and on the agreement or CCP rules: variation margin is usually settled in cash, so securities eligible only for initial margin do not cover it. | |
| **[Collateral](concepts.md#collateral)** | Quantity of a collateral asset provided by a collateral provider to a collateral taker at a stated tier, under an identified collateral arrangement, by title transfer or security interest, for an account as of a time, with its market value and its value after haircuts and concentration limits; distinct from assets that are merely eligible. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[Collateral Asset](concepts.md#collateral-asset)** | Asset that can be delivered or pledged as collateral: cash in a given currency, a security identified by its issue, or another form a collateral taker accepts under its rules (for example gold or a bank guarantee, to verify). Whether it is eligible depends on a version of a collateral eligibility schedule; whether it is committed depends on collateral actually held. | |
| **[Collateral Eligibility Schedule](concepts.md#collateral-eligibility-schedule)** | Versioned schedule, published by a collateral taker for a clearing service or arrangement, account type and tier, of the assets or asset criteria acceptable as collateral, with their haircuts (including currency mismatch haircuts), concentration limits, wrong-way restrictions and accepted holding locations. | |
| **[Collateral Arrangement](concepts.md#collateral-arrangement)** | Terms under which collateral is provided, valued, substituted, remunerated and returned between a collateral provider and a collateral taker: legal form (title transfer or security interest) and any right of use, secured obligations, applicable eligibility schedule, haircut, valuation, margin call and interest terms; standalone or part of a CCP rulebook or client clearing agreement. | |
| **[Collateral Movement](concepts.md#collateral-movement)** | Observed transfer of collateral assets between a collateral provider and a collateral taker, or between their accounts, under a specified arrangement: delivery, return, substitution or reallocation, including a book-entry pledge or release; distinct from the collateral instruction that requests it. A cash collateral transfer is also a Cash Movement and is counted once. | |
| **[Portfolio](concepts.md#portfolio)** | Collection of positions or other business objects, delimited for a stated purpose (for example margining, compression or reporting) by an identified party, as of a time; membership has no legal effect by itself. | |
| **[Margin Parameter Set](concepts.md#margin-parameter-set)** *(level 2)* | Versioned set of values that a margin methodology applies at a given time, published by the margin taker for a clearing service, clearing contract or product family: margin rates per clearing contract, scenarios, risk parameter files, offsets and add-on parameters. A routine scheduled publication under an unchanged method creates a new version, not a Rule Change; a new parameter set announced by notice or circular, or one that needs supervisory validation, is a Rule Change. | |
| **[Margin Run](concepts.md#margin-run)** *(level 2)* | Dated calculation by a margin taker that applies a methodology version and parameter set to the positions of one or more accounts at an as-of time, producing margin requirements by account, component and currency; of a run type (scheduled end-of-day, scheduled intraday or ad hoc). A run may or may not lead to a margin call. | |
| **[Margin Requirement Component](concepts.md#margin-requirement-component)** *(level 2)* | Separately determined and reported part of a margin requirement, identified by the component type the margin taker uses, at the same tier, account, run and currency basis as the requirement it belongs to. Components are not always additive across CCPs; native component names are kept beside the harmonised type. | |
| **[Client Margin Uplift](concepts.md#client-margin-uplift)** *(level 2)* | Rule in a member-to-client margin methodology by which a clearing broker requires from a client more margin than the CCP-derived figure, set per client, per group of clients defined by the clearing broker for risk purposes (not necessarily the commercial group), per product or per clearing service. Its basis is either a mandated minimum set by a regime or CCP rule (for example customer initial margin above the clearing house level for non-hedge accounts in the US, to verify) or a discretionary choice under broker policy or client terms. Part of the member-to-client methodology, as the expert-review arbitration intended, not a separate policy object. | |
| **[Margin Portfolio](concepts.md#margin-portfolio)** *(level 2)* | Set of positions that a margin taker margins together under one methodology version, within which offsets between positions are recognised; it may be narrower than an account (a liquidation group or a gross client sub-account) or wider (cross-margining across products, clearing services or, under an agreement, CCPs). | |
| **[Margin Call Terms](concepts.md#margin-call-terms)** *(level 2)* | Part of a collateral arrangement, CCP rulebook or client clearing agreement that governs when and how margin is called: call frequency and runs, call and maintenance trigger levels, thresholds, minimum transfer amount, rounding, call currency, deadline with its calendar and time zone, acceptable means of payment, and the consequence of a missed deadline. | |
| **[Margin Excess or Shortfall](concepts.md#margin-excess-or-shortfall)** *(level 2)* | Difference, for the same margin provider and taker, tier, account, as-of time and currency basis, between collateral held at its value after haircuts and concentration limits and the margin requirement; an excess where collateral exceeds the requirement, a shortfall where it falls short. At the member-to-client tier the excess may be held at the CCP or retained by the clearing broker. | |
| **[Collateral Haircut](concepts.md#collateral-haircut)** *(level 2)* | Reduction, set in a version of a collateral eligibility schedule for an asset or asset criterion, applied to the market value of collateral to give its value as cover, including any currency mismatch haircut. | |
| **[Concentration Limit](concepts.md#concentration-limit)** *(level 2)* | Cap, set in a version of a collateral eligibility schedule, on how much of a given asset, issuer, asset class or currency counts as collateral for a provider or account; value above the cap counts for nothing. | |
| **[Collateral Instruction](concepts.md#collateral-instruction)** *(level 2)* | Request by a collateral provider or taker to deliver, return, substitute or reallocate collateral under an arrangement, which the counterparty or an agent can accept or reject. For cash collateral, it is carried out through a Payment Instruction; the two are linked and form one pending transfer, not two. | |
| **[Price Alignment Amount](concepts.md#price-alignment-amount)** *(level 2)* | Amount owed under a clearing service's rules between a CCP and a clearing member, and passed on or not to clients, that offsets the interest effect of variation margin so that cleared prices match uncleared ones: under collateralised-to-market treatment it is interest on cumulative variation margin collateral; under settled-to-market treatment it accompanies the daily settlement. It gives rise to a Settlement Obligation with purpose 'price alignment' and is never part of the variation margin amount itself. | |
| **[Cash Collateral Interest](concepts.md#cash-collateral-interest)** *(level 2)* | Interest, positive or negative, owed for a period on cash held as collateral, at the rate and terms set by the collateral arrangement or the CCP's rules, by the collateral taker to the provider or, under a negative rate, the reverse; stated at each tier (CCP to clearing member, clearing member to client). When it falls due it gives rise to a Settlement Obligation with purpose 'interest'. Distinct from price alignment amounts. Promotion of the backlog item is conditional on open question 9 (collateral income) or a sponsor waiver. | |
| **[Default Fund Contribution](concepts.md#default-fund-contribution)** | Contribution that the holder of a Clearing Member role must make, under the rules of a clearing service, to the mutualised default fund that covers losses beyond a defaulter's own margin and contribution; sized and recalibrated by the CCP and covered by eligible collateral. It may be paid by a sponsoring clearing member for a sponsored participant. Distinct from margin, from CCP fees and from assessments a CCP may call after a default. | |
| **[Margin Provider](concepts.md#margin-provider)** *(level 2)* | Role of a party that must provide margin to a margin taker for an account at a tier, determined by the clearing roles of the two parties at that tier, never set independently: a clearing member toward a CCP, a participant admitted in its own name toward a CCP (directly or through its sponsor), a client toward its clearing broker, or an indirect client toward its client. Whether the account is house or client comes from the CCP account kind, not from this role. Usually the same party as the collateral provider under the collateral arrangement, except where a third party pledges assets. The direction of each variation margin payment follows the movement of value and does not change the role. | |
| **[Margin Taker](concepts.md#margin-taker)** *(level 2)* | Role of a party that requires and calls margin from a margin provider for an account at a tier, applying its margin methodology and collateral eligibility schedule, determined by the clearing roles of the two parties at that tier: a CCP toward its clearing members or admitted participants, a clearing broker toward its clients, or a client toward its indirect clients. Usually the same party as the collateral taker under the collateral arrangement. | |
| **[Open Trade Equity](concepts.md#open-trade-equity)** *(level 2)* | Unrealised gain or loss on the open futures and futures-style option positions of an identified account, measured from the trade price to the current settlement price (or another stated valuation price) times quantity and contract multiplier, as stated by the account's maintaining party for an as-of time and currency. It is a component of Account Equity on a clearing broker's statement and becomes realised, moving to the cash balance, when the position is closed. Whether variation margin already settled each day is presented in open trade equity or posted to the cash balance, and whether open trade equity can be withdrawn or used to cover a requirement, are conventions of the statement issuer and the client agreement (to verify per firm and regime). Premium-style options are not in open trade equity: their value is reported as option market value. | |
| **[Account Cash Balance](concepts.md#account-cash-balance)** *(level 2)* | Cash recorded in an identified account, by currency, as of a stated time, on the books of the account's maintaining party (clearing broker, CCP or the observing firm): deposits and withdrawals, realised gains and losses, option premiums paid or received, fees, commissions, interest and other posted cash amounts. Also called ledger balance. It excludes the unrealised value of open positions (Open Trade Equity, option market value). The cash itself, where it secures a requirement, is Collateral; the balance is the account's record of it. | |
| **[Account Equity](concepts.md#account-equity)** *(level 2)* | Value of an identified account at an as-of time and in a currency basis, as stated by its maintaining party: the cash balance plus open trade equity (total equity) and, where the account holds premium-style options, plus the market value of long options less that of short options (net liquidating value). Which components are included, whether securities collateral is counted (at its value after haircuts) and the names used follow the statement issuer and the client agreement. Where the agreement margins on account equity (mainly US futures), it is the held side compared with the margin requirement to state the Margin Excess or Shortfall. | |
| **[Position Valuation](concepts.md#position-valuation)** *(level 2)* | Value of a position of an identified account at an as-of time and in a currency, with its valuation party, price source and method: for listed contracts, the settlement price applied to quantity and contract multiplier; for cleared OTC trades, the net present value from the CCP's valuation, or from another stated party where the client agreement or the firm's own books use one. Its change between valuation times, and its difference from the value at the trade price, underlie variation margin requirements, open trade equity and account equity; for listed contracts the valuation itself is a market value of the position, never by itself a profit, loss or margin amount. A valuation is a measure derived from a position, not the position itself. | |

## 3. Open questions on meaning

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### Margin required (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Distinguish IM/VM, member–CCP/client–member relationship, risk scope, time and collateral coverage.

**Proposed answer:** Undefined until five qualifiers are fixed: tier (from margin provider and taker role assignments: CCP–member house, CCP–member client account, member–client, client–indirect client); component (IM, VM, add-ons, total); account and gross or net basis; as-of time and run; currency basis. Under net omnibus accounts a client-level figure exists only in the member's own calculation. Member–client IM is a CCP replication, a replication with multiplier or add-on, or an own model (Margin Methodology). Confidence high. Firm decision: default tier, if any. [04]

<sub>reference LD-A0011</sub>

### Variation margin (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify collateral versus settlement treatment under the governing arrangement.

**Proposed answer:** Settled-to-market: the daily payment settles exposure and is accompanied by a price alignment amount. Collateralised-to-market: VM is collateral to be returned, with price alignment interest. The treatment is a versioned fact per clearing service and account type; some OTC CCPs converted some or all accounts to settled-to-market (dates per service to verify). Price alignment is never part of VM. Confidence high on concepts, medium on per-CCP facts. Legal/Finance decision per service for internal reporting. [04, 03, 06]

<sub>reference LD-A0012</sub>

### Margin and initial margin (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** "Margin" as requirement versus collateral held or posted; CCP initial margin versus US futures initial and maintenance call levels versus uncleared bilateral initial margin.

**Proposed answer:** "Client margin held" usually means Collateral; "margin required" means Margin Requirement; "margin posted" means delivered collateral. US futures initial and maintenance levels are two call-trigger levels of one requirement, not two requirement types. Initial margin under uncleared bilateral margin rules is out of scope and must not resolve to Initial Margin Requirement. Confidence high. [04]
