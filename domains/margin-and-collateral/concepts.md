### Margin Methodology

*The rules and numbers a CCP or broker uses to work out how much margin is needed.*

**Definition.** Versioned rules by which a margin taker determines margin requirements for a scope: the model family, confidence level, close-out period, look-back, offsets recognised, add-ons and anti-procyclicality measures, together with the parameter set the model runs on, which is versioned separately because it changes more often. At the member-to-client tier the methodology is a replication of the CCP's, a replication with a multiplier or add-on, or the margin taker's own model.

**Example.** CCP Z margins equity index futures with a portfolio VaR-type model; Clearing Broker Y applies CCP Z's model to Client A with a multiplier.

**Not to be confused with.** Margin Requirement: the methodology is the recipe, the requirement is the amount it produces.

**Breaks down into:** [Margin Parameter Set](#margin-parameter-set) (part of), [Client Margin Uplift](#client-margin-uplift) (part of)

**Connected to:**

- [Margin Requirement](#margin-requirement) *determined under* Margin Methodology — One methodology version per calculation run; a member-client methodology may replicate the CCP's.
- [Clearing Contract](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#clearing-contract) *is margined under* Margin Methodology
- [Margin Run](#margin-run) *applies* Margin Methodology
- [Margin Taker](#margin-taker) *applies* Margin Methodology

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Margin required” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Margin Parameter Set

*The current numbers a margin model runs on, updated more often than the model itself.*

**Definition.** Versioned set of values that a margin methodology applies at a given time, published by the margin taker for a clearing service, clearing contract or product family: margin rates per clearing contract, scenarios, risk parameter files, offsets and add-on parameters. A routine scheduled publication under an unchanged method creates a new version, not a Rule Change; a new parameter set announced by notice or circular, or one that needs supervisory validation, is a Rule Change.

**Example.** CCP Z publishes new margin rates for its equity index futures, effective next business day, without changing its model.

**Not to be confused with.** Margin Methodology: a methodology change alters the model; a parameter change only updates its inputs.

**Part of:** [Margin Methodology](#margin-methodology)

**Values (draft list):** margin rates per contract; risk parameter file (SPAN-type); scenario set (VaR-type); liquidity or concentration factors; intraday call thresholds

**Words people use:** “margin rates” (contextual, listed derivatives); “margin levels” (contextual, circulars); “risk parameters” (contextual, CCP)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service, product scope, effective date and version; which methodology version it belongs to.

**Connected to:**

- *is changed by a rule change* [Rule Change](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#rule-change)
- *is published for a clearing service* [Clearing Service](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-service)
- *is applied in a margin run* [Margin Run](#margin-run)

### Client Margin Uplift

*The extra margin a clearing broker asks from a client on top of what the CCP requires.*

**Definition.** Rule in a member-to-client margin methodology by which a clearing broker requires from a client more margin than the CCP-derived figure, set per client, per group of clients defined by the clearing broker for risk purposes (not necessarily the commercial group), per product or per clearing service. Its basis is either a mandated minimum set by a regime or CCP rule (for example customer initial margin above the clearing house level for non-hedge accounts in the US, to verify) or a discretionary choice under broker policy or client terms. Part of the member-to-client methodology, as the expert-review arbitration intended, not a separate policy object.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y requires 1.2 times CCP Z's initial margin from Client A on its energy futures.

**Not to be confused with.** Margin Add-on: set by the CCP on the clearing member; the uplift is set by the broker on the client.

**Part of:** [Margin Methodology](#margin-methodology)

**Values (draft list):** basis: mandated minimum (regime or CCP rule); discretionary (broker policy or client terms); form: multiplier; fixed add-on; own model; none (pure replication, only where no mandated minimum applies)

**Words people use:** “client margin multiplier” (contextual); “house margin” (ambiguous with margin on the house account; avoid)

**Before using it, know:** Client, product scope, clearing service, effective date and the CCP figure it is applied to.

**Connected to:**

- *is agreed or notified under* [Client Clearing Agreement](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#client-clearing-agreement)
- *applies to* [Client](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#client)

### Margin Requirement

*How much margin one party must provide to another, for an account, at a given time.*

**Definition.** Amount of margin that a margin provider must provide to a margin taker for an identified account or risk scope, stated by tier, component, run and as-of time, and currency basis, and determined under an identified margin methodology or contractual rule. For variation margin the provider is whichever party the value has moved against. It is an amount owed in principle, distinct from the margin call that demands it and the collateral that covers it.

**Example.** At end of day CCP Z requires Clearing Broker Y to hold an amount of initial margin on its client omnibus account.

**Not to be confused with.** Collateral: the requirement is what is owed; collateral is what is actually held.

**Breaks down into:** [Initial Margin Requirement](#initial-margin-requirement) (kind of), [Margin Run](#margin-run) (step in), [Margin Requirement Component](#margin-requirement-component) (part of), [Margin Excess or Shortfall](#margin-excess-or-shortfall) (state of)

**Words people use:** “margin held, margin posted” (Any)

**Before using it, know:** Margin provider and taker (tier), account, component, methodology version, run type, as-of time, currency basis

**Connected to:**

- *assessed for* [Portfolio](#portfolio), [Account](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#account) — Include margin provider and taker role assignments (which give the tier), component, methodology version, run type and as-of time; not individual-position causality. At CCP tier the account is a CCP Account.
- [Margin Call](#margin-call) *requests satisfaction of* Margin Requirement — Requirement, demand and remaining shortfall are different objects; the called amount derives from the requirement less collateral already held after haircut, adjusted for minimum transfer amount and rounding.
- *determined under* [Margin Methodology](#margin-methodology) — One methodology version per calculation run; a member-client methodology may replicate the CCP's.
- [Collateral](#collateral) *covers* Margin Requirement — Same provider-taker pair, account, as-of time and currency basis; the difference is the excess or shortfall.
- [Margin Portfolio](#margin-portfolio) *is the scope of* Margin Requirement
- [Margin Provider](#margin-provider) *owes* Margin Requirement

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Margin required” (open, answer proposed); “Margin and initial margin” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Initial Margin Requirement

*Margin held to cover the loss of closing out a defaulter's positions over a few days.*

**Definition.** Margin requirement intended to cover the loss the margin taker could suffer in closing out or hedging the provider's positions after its default, over an assumed close-out period at a stated confidence level under the applicable margin methodology, including the add-ons that methodology applies. Where a CCP reports the current liquidation value of premium-style options inside its initial margin total, that amount is recorded as a separate component.

**Example.** Client A's futures portfolio at Clearing Broker Y needs initial margin for a two-day close-out under CCP Z's model.

**Not to be confused with.** US maintenance margin: a call-trigger level of the same requirement, not a separate one.

**Kind of:** [Margin Requirement](#margin-requirement)

**Note.** A member–client IM derived from the CCP figure is still IM at the member–client tier. US futures "initial" and "maintenance" are two call-trigger levels of one requirement. [04]

**Connected to:**

- [Margin Requirement Component](#margin-requirement-component) *breaks down* Initial Margin Requirement

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Margin required” (open, answer proposed); “Margin and initial margin” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Margin Run

*One calculation of margin at a point in time, which produces the requirement figures.*

**Definition.** Dated calculation by a margin taker that applies a methodology version and parameter set to the positions of one or more accounts at an as-of time, producing margin requirements by account, component and currency; of a run type (scheduled end-of-day, scheduled intraday or ad hoc). A run may or may not lead to a margin call.

**Example.** CCP Z's midday run recalculates Clearing Broker Y's requirement on current prices; no call follows because the increase is below the threshold.

**Not to be confused with.** Intraday Margin Call: an intraday run is a calculation; a call is a demand that only some runs trigger.

**Step in:** [Margin Requirement](#margin-requirement)

**Values (draft list):** scheduled end-of-day; scheduled intraday; ad hoc

**Words people use:** “margin cycle” (contextual); “margin calculation” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Margin taker, run type, as-of time, time zone, methodology and parameter versions.

**Connected to:**

- [Processing Window](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#processing-window) *dates* Margin Run
- *applies* [Margin Methodology](#margin-methodology)
- *uses* [Margin Parameter Set](#margin-parameter-set)
- *may lead to* [Margin Call](#margin-call)
- *is calculated over* [Margin Portfolio](#margin-portfolio)

### Margin Requirement Component

*One of the pieces a margin requirement is broken into on a CCP or client statement.*

**Definition.** Separately determined and reported part of a margin requirement, identified by the component type the margin taker uses, at the same tier, account, run and currency basis as the requirement it belongs to. Components are not always additive across CCPs; native component names are kept beside the harmonised type.

**Example.** CCP Z's statement to Clearing Broker Y shows base initial margin, a concentration add-on and premium margin as separate lines.

**Not to be confused with.** Initial Margin Requirement: initial margin is a kind of requirement; a component is a line inside one.

**Part of:** [Margin Requirement](#margin-requirement)

**Values (draft list):** component type: base initial margin; add-on (for example concentration, liquidity, wrong-way risk); premium or net liquidation value margin; delivery margin; variation margin is a separate kind of requirement, not a component, even when CCP statements show it on a line next to the components

**Words people use:** “margin component” (any); “margin breakdown” (contextual, statements)

**Before using it, know:** Tier, account, run, currency basis, and the CCP's native component name.

**Connected to:**

- *breaks down* [Initial Margin Requirement](#initial-margin-requirement)

### Margin Excess or Shortfall

*How much more, or less, collateral is held than is required.*

**Definition.** Difference, for the same margin provider and taker, tier, account, as-of time and currency basis, between collateral held at its value after haircuts and concentration limits and the margin requirement; an excess where collateral exceeds the requirement, a shortfall where it falls short. At the member-to-client tier the excess may be held at the CCP or retained by the clearing broker.

**Example.** After the overnight run Client A has an excess at Clearing Broker Y, which Client A asks to be returned.

**Not to be confused with.** Margin Call: a shortfall becomes a call only after thresholds and minimum transfer amount; an excess is not a negative call.

**State of:** [Margin Requirement](#margin-requirement)

**Values (draft list):** excess; shortfall; fully covered

**Words people use:** “excess” (contextual); “deficit” (contextual, US futures); “margin surplus” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Tier, account, as-of time, currency basis, where the excess is held.

**Connected to:**

- *compares* [Collateral](#collateral)
- *may give rise to* [Margin Call](#margin-call)
- *may be held at* [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account)

### Variation Margin Requirement

*Margin that reflects the change in value of positions at current prices, settled daily or held as collateral.*

**Definition.** Amount payable or receivable reflecting the change in value of positions since the previous margin cycle, with direction stated from the observing party; under settled-to-market treatment it gives rise to a Settlement Obligation, under collateralised-to-market treatment to collateral that must be returned as exposure changes.

**Example.** Client A's long futures lost value today, so Client A owes variation margin, which Clearing Broker Y pays on to CCP Z.

**Not to be confused with.** Price Alignment Amount: a small interest adjustment paid alongside variation margin, never part of it.

**Breaks down into:** [Price Alignment Amount](#price-alignment-amount) (part of)

**Note.** Price alignment is a separate flow. Premium-style options carry no VM; futures-style options do. [04, 03]

**Connected to:**

- *gives rise to* [Settlement Obligation](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#settlement-obligation) — Under settled-to-market treatment; under collateralised-to-market treatment VM is demanded by a margin call and met with collateral. Treatment is a versioned fact per clearing service and account type (the open question “Variation margin”).
- [Settlement Price](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#settlement-price) *values listed positions for* Variation Margin Requirement

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Margin required” (open, answer proposed); “Variation margin” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Price Alignment Amount

*A small interest-like payment that goes with variation margin on cleared swaps but is not part of it.*

**Definition.** Amount owed under a clearing service's rules between a CCP and a clearing member, and passed on or not to clients, that offsets the interest effect of variation margin so that cleared prices match uncleared ones: under collateralised-to-market treatment it is interest on cumulative variation margin collateral; under settled-to-market treatment it accompanies the daily settlement. It gives rise to a Settlement Obligation with purpose 'price alignment' and is never part of the variation margin amount itself.

**Example.** CCP Z pays Clearing Broker Y a price alignment amount on its swap account with the daily settlement; Clearing Broker Y passes it to Client A.

**Not to be confused with.** Cash Collateral Interest: price alignment relates to variation margin; cash collateral interest remunerates cash held as initial margin.

**Part of:** [Variation Margin Requirement](#variation-margin-requirement)

**Values (draft list):** price alignment interest (collateralised-to-market); price alignment amount (settled-to-market)

**Words people use:** “PAI” (OTC clearing); “PAA” (OTC clearing)

**Before using it, know:** Tier, clearing service, account, variation margin treatment, currency and period.

**Connected to:**

- *gives rise to* [Settlement Obligation](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#settlement-obligation)
- *counts in only if the management reporting basis says so* [Collateral Revenue](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#collateral-revenue)

### Margin Call

*A demand to deliver margin by a deadline, for the part of the requirement not already covered.*

**Definition.** Demand issued by a margin taker to a margin provider, from a scheduled end-of-day, scheduled intraday or ad hoc margin run, for an amount due by a stated deadline in a stated calendar and time zone, to cover the part of one or more margin requirements not covered by collateral already held after haircuts, subject to thresholds, minimum transfer amount and rounding. One call may combine several requirements and currencies, and a CCP may collect it by direct debit.

**Example.** CCP Z calls Clearing Broker Y for cash by the morning deadline after the overnight run.

**Not to be confused with.** Margin Requirement: the call is only the uncovered part of the requirement, after collateral held.

**Note.** A return of excess on request is a Collateral Movement, not a negative call; VM paid out by a CCP is a payment, not a call. [04]

**Connected to:**

- *requests satisfaction of* [Margin Requirement](#margin-requirement) — Requirement, demand and remaining shortfall are different objects; the called amount derives from the requirement less collateral already held after haircut, adjusted for minimum transfer amount and rounding.
- [Margin Run](#margin-run) *may lead to* Margin Call
- [Margin Call Terms](#margin-call-terms) *governs* Margin Call
- [Margin Excess or Shortfall](#margin-excess-or-shortfall) *may give rise to* Margin Call
- [Collateral Instruction](#collateral-instruction) *may respond to* Margin Call
- [Margin Taker](#margin-taker) *issues* Margin Call
- [Settlement Deadline](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#settlement-deadline) *is set for the obligation created by* Margin Call

### Collateral

*Cash or securities actually held by one party from another to cover margin.*

**Definition.** Quantity of a collateral asset provided by a collateral provider to a collateral taker at a stated tier, under an identified collateral arrangement, by title transfer or security interest, for an account as of a time, with its market value and its value after haircuts and concentration limits; distinct from assets that are merely eligible. — *meaning changed in 2026-11, accepted as Draft*

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y holds government bonds from Client A under a security interest, valued after haircut.

**Not to be confused with.** Collateral Asset: an asset that could be used is not collateral until it is delivered.

**Words people use:** “margin held, margin posted” (Any); “collateral balance” (Any)

### Collateral Asset

*Something that could be used as collateral, such as cash in a currency or a bond.*

**Definition.** Asset that can be delivered or pledged as collateral: cash in a given currency, a security identified by its issue, or another form a collateral taker accepts under its rules (for example gold or a bank guarantee, to verify). Whether it is eligible depends on a version of a collateral eligibility schedule; whether it is committed depends on collateral actually held.

**Example.** A euro government bond is a collateral asset; it is eligible at CCP Z with a haircut.

**Not to be confused with.** Collateral: the asset in general, versus a quantity of it actually held.

**Note.** Cash in a given currency is a collateral asset. Eligibility holds only against a version of a Collateral Eligibility Schedule. [04]

**Connected to:**

- [Collateral](#collateral) *of asset* Collateral Asset — Quantity of the asset, with market value and value after haircut as of a time.
- [Collateral Eligibility Schedule](#collateral-eligibility-schedule) *admits* Collateral Asset — Per schedule version, with haircut, limits and validity; eligibility is not commitment.
- [Collateral Movement](#collateral-movement) *transfers* Collateral Asset — Asset quantities moved; records the resulting change in collateral held.
- [Collateral Haircut](#collateral-haircut) *applies to* Collateral Asset
- [Concentration Limit](#concentration-limit) *applies to* Collateral Asset

### Collateral Eligibility Schedule

*The list of what a collateral taker accepts, with haircuts and limits.*

**Definition.** Versioned schedule, published by a collateral taker for a clearing service or arrangement, account type and tier, of the assets or asset criteria acceptable as collateral, with their haircuts (including currency mismatch haircuts), concentration limits, wrong-way restrictions and accepted holding locations.

**Example.** CCP Z's schedule accepts cash in three currencies and certain government bonds, each with a haircut and a limit.

**Not to be confused with.** Collateral Arrangement: the schedule says what is acceptable; the arrangement sets the legal terms.

**Breaks down into:** [Collateral Haircut](#collateral-haircut) (part of), [Concentration Limit](#concentration-limit) (part of)

**Connected to:**

- *admits* [Collateral Asset](#collateral-asset) — Per schedule version, with haircut, limits and validity; eligibility is not commitment.
- [Collateral Arrangement](#collateral-arrangement) *references* Collateral Eligibility Schedule — The eligibility schedule version applicable under the arrangement.

### Collateral Haircut

*The percentage knocked off an asset's market value when it is counted as collateral.*

**Definition.** Reduction, set in a version of a collateral eligibility schedule for an asset or asset criterion, applied to the market value of collateral to give its value as cover, including any currency mismatch haircut.

**Example.** CCP Z values a ten-year government bond at its market value less a haircut, plus a currency haircut because the account's requirement is in another currency.

**Not to be confused with.** Concentration Limit: a haircut reduces the value of every unit; a limit caps how much of an asset counts at all.

**Part of:** [Collateral Eligibility Schedule](#collateral-eligibility-schedule)

**Values (draft list):** asset haircut; currency mismatch haircut; additional wrong-way or concentration haircut; client-tier haircut set by the clearing broker

**Words people use:** “haircut” (any); “valuation percentage” (contextual, bilateral documentation)

**Before using it, know:** Schedule version, asset, tier, currency of the requirement.

**Connected to:**

- *applies to* [Collateral Asset](#collateral-asset)
- *gives the value after haircut of* [Collateral](#collateral)
- *is changed by* [Rule Change](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#rule-change)

### Concentration Limit

*A limit on how much of one kind of asset can count towards margin.*

**Definition.** Cap, set in a version of a collateral eligibility schedule, on how much of a given asset, issuer, asset class or currency counts as collateral for a provider or account; value above the cap counts for nothing.

**Example.** CCP Z counts one issuer's bonds only up to a share of Clearing Broker Y's requirement; the rest is not valued.

**Not to be confused with.** Collateral Haircut: the haircut reduces each unit's value; the limit caps the quantity that counts.

**Part of:** [Collateral Eligibility Schedule](#collateral-eligibility-schedule)

**Values (draft list):** per issuer; per asset class; per currency; per security issue; absolute cap; share of requirement

**Words people use:** “concentration cap” (any)

**Before using it, know:** Schedule version, tier, account, limit basis.

**Connected to:**

- *applies to* [Collateral Asset](#collateral-asset)
- *limits the value of* [Collateral](#collateral)

### Collateral Arrangement

*The legal terms under which collateral is given, valued, paid interest and returned.*

**Definition.** Terms under which collateral is provided, valued, substituted, remunerated and returned between a collateral provider and a collateral taker: legal form (title transfer or security interest) and any right of use, secured obligations, applicable eligibility schedule, haircut, valuation, margin call and interest terms; standalone or part of a CCP rulebook or client clearing agreement.

**Example.** Client A's clearing agreement with Clearing Broker Y includes collateral terms with title transfer of cash.

**Not to be confused with.** Client Clearing Agreement: the wider agreement that may contain the collateral terms.

**Breaks down into:** [Margin Call Terms](#margin-call-terms) (part of), [Cash Collateral Interest](#cash-collateral-interest) (part of)

### Margin Call Terms

*The rules on when a margin call is made, how big it must be and how fast it must be paid.*

**Definition.** Part of a collateral arrangement, CCP rulebook or client clearing agreement that governs when and how margin is called: call frequency and runs, call and maintenance trigger levels, thresholds, minimum transfer amount, rounding, call currency, deadline with its calendar and time zone, acceptable means of payment, and the consequence of a missed deadline.

**Example.** Client A's agreement with Clearing Broker Y sets one daily call, a minimum transfer amount and a deadline of noon local time.

**Not to be confused with.** Cut-off: a cut-off is a deadline in general; call terms say which deadline applies to margin calls and with which other rules.

**Part of:** [Collateral Arrangement](#collateral-arrangement)

**Values (draft list):** call frequency and run types; deadline: a Settlement Deadline (a kind of Cut-off) with calendar, time zone and consequence; threshold; minimum transfer amount; rounding; dispute process

**Words people use:** “call parameters” (contextual); “margin terms” (contextual, client agreements)

**Before using it, know:** Tier, arrangement or rulebook version, clearing service, client or client group.

**Connected to:**

- *may be set in* [Client Clearing Agreement](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#client-clearing-agreement)
- *names the deadline as* [Cut-off](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#cut-off)
- *governs* [Margin Call](#margin-call)

### Cash Collateral Interest

*Interest paid or charged on cash given as margin.*

**Definition.** Interest, positive or negative, owed for a period on cash held as collateral, at the rate and terms set by the collateral arrangement or the CCP's rules, by the collateral taker to the provider or, under a negative rate, the reverse; stated at each tier (CCP to clearing member, clearing member to client). When it falls due it gives rise to a Settlement Obligation with purpose 'interest'. Distinct from price alignment amounts. Promotion of the backlog item is conditional on open question 9 (collateral income) or a sponsor waiver.

**Example.** CCP Z pays Clearing Broker Y interest on euro cash posted as initial margin; Clearing Broker Y pays Client A interest on its cash under their agreement.

**Not to be confused with.** Price Alignment Amount: tied to variation margin, not to cash held as collateral.

**Part of:** [Collateral Arrangement](#collateral-arrangement)

**Values (draft list):** tier: CCP to clearing member; clearing member to client; direction from the observing party: received; paid; stage: accrued; due; paid

**Words people use:** “interest on margin” (contextual); “cash collateral remuneration” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Tier, arrangement, currency, accrual period, whether the amount is accrued or paid.

**Connected to:**

- *accrues on* [Collateral](#collateral)
- *gives rise to* [Settlement Obligation](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#settlement-obligation)
- *feeds* [Collateral Revenue](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#collateral-revenue)

### Collateral Movement

*An actual transfer of collateral: delivery, return, substitution or move between accounts.*

**Definition.** Observed transfer of collateral assets between a collateral provider and a collateral taker, or between their accounts, under a specified arrangement: delivery, return, substitution or reallocation, including a book-entry pledge or release; distinct from the collateral instruction that requests it. A cash collateral transfer is also a Cash Movement and is counted once.

**Example.** Client A delivers bonds to Clearing Broker Y's collateral account to meet a call.

**Not to be confused with.** Margin Call: the call is the demand; the movement is what actually happened.

**Breaks down into:** [Collateral Instruction](#collateral-instruction) (step in)

**Connected to:**

- *changes* [Collateral](#collateral) — A movement transfers asset quantities and thereby changes the collateral held; identify provider, taker, arrangement and quantity. Label changed from moves on 2026-11-01 because Collateral is now a State.
- *transfers* [Collateral Asset](#collateral-asset) — Asset quantities moved; records the resulting change in collateral held.
- [Settlement or Custody Agent](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#settlement-or-custody-agent) *evidences* Collateral Movement

### Collateral Instruction

*A request to deliver, return or swap collateral, before anything has moved.*

**Definition.** Request by a collateral provider or taker to deliver, return, substitute or reallocate collateral under an arrangement, which the counterparty or an agent can accept or reject. For cash collateral, it is carried out through a Payment Instruction; the two are linked and form one pending transfer, not two.

**Example.** Client A asks Clearing Broker Y to substitute bonds for cash; Clearing Broker Y accepts and the substitution settles the next day.

**Not to be confused with.** Collateral Movement: the instruction is the request; the movement is the observed transfer.

**Step in:** [Collateral Movement](#collateral-movement)

**Values (draft list):** delivery; return of excess; substitution; reallocation between accounts

**Words people use:** “collateral request” (contextual); “substitution request” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Arrangement, provider, taker, asset and quantity, requested date, status.

**Connected to:**

- *is made under* [Collateral Arrangement](#collateral-arrangement)
- *may respond to* [Margin Call](#margin-call)
- [Payment Instruction](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#payment-instruction) *carries out for cash collateral* Collateral Instruction

### Portfolio

*A named group of positions or other items gathered for a purpose.*

**Definition.** Collection of positions or other business objects, delimited for a stated purpose (for example margining, compression or reporting) by an identified party, as of a time; membership has no legal effect by itself.

**Example.** CCP Z margins all of Client A's index futures and options together as one portfolio.

**Not to be confused with.** Account: an account records ownership and booking; a portfolio groups items for a purpose such as margining.

**Breaks down into:** [Margin Portfolio](#margin-portfolio) (kind of)

**Connected to:**

- [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position) *included in* Portfolio — Membership is purpose-specific and time-bound.
- [Cleared OTC Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#cleared-otc-position) *may be included in a margin portfolio* Portfolio

### Margin Portfolio

*The group of positions whose risks are offset against each other when margin is calculated.*

**Definition.** Set of positions that a margin taker margins together under one methodology version, within which offsets between positions are recognised; it may be narrower than an account (a liquidation group or a gross client sub-account) or wider (cross-margining across products, clearing services or, under an agreement, CCPs).

**Example.** CCP Z margins Client A's index futures and index options in one portfolio, so the hedge reduces the requirement.

**Not to be confused with.** CCP Account: the account records positions and collateral; the portfolio is the scope over which offsets are allowed.

**Kind of:** [Portfolio](#portfolio)

**Values (draft list):** within an account; liquidation group; per client in a gross account; cross-product or cross-service; cross-CCP under an agreement

**Words people use:** “margin unit” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Margin taker, clearing services and accounts covered, methodology version, as-of time.

**Connected to:**

- [Margin Run](#margin-run) *is calculated over* Margin Portfolio
- *is defined within or across* [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account)
- *contains* [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position)
- *is the scope of* [Margin Requirement](#margin-requirement)

### Default Fund Contribution

*A clearing member's share of the CCP's shared pool for covering another member's default.*

**Definition.** Contribution that the holder of a Clearing Member role must make, under the rules of a clearing service, to the mutualised default fund that covers losses beyond a defaulter's own margin and contribution; sized and recalibrated by the CCP and covered by eligible collateral. It may be paid by a sponsoring clearing member for a sponsored participant. Distinct from margin, from CCP fees and from assessments a CCP may call after a default.

**Example.** CCP Z recalibrates Clearing Broker Y's contribution to its listed derivatives default fund each month.

**Not to be confused with.** Initial Margin Requirement: margin covers the member's own positions; the default fund is shared among members.

**Part of:** [Clearing Service](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-service)

**Values (draft list):** required contribution; contribution held; replenishment after use

**Words people use:** “guarantee fund contribution” (contextual, some CCPs); “clearing fund contribution” (contextual, US)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service, clearing member, recalibration date, currency.

**Connected to:**

- [Clearing Member Default](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-member-default) *may use* Default Fund Contribution
- *is owed by the holder of* [Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-member)
- *may be paid by* [Sponsoring Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#sponsoring-clearing-member)
- *may be required by* [Access Requirement](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#access-requirement)
- *is covered by* [Collateral](#collateral)
- *gives rise to a funding cost in* [Cost](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#cost)

### Margin Provider

*The party that has to give margin.*

**Definition.** Role of a party that must provide margin to a margin taker for an account at a tier, determined by the clearing roles of the two parties at that tier, never set independently: a clearing member toward a CCP, a participant admitted in its own name toward a CCP (directly or through its sponsor), a client toward its clearing broker, or an indirect client toward its client. Whether the account is house or client comes from the CCP account kind, not from this role. Usually the same party as the collateral provider under the collateral arrangement, except where a third party pledges assets.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y is the margin provider toward CCP Z and the margin taker toward Client A.

**Not to be confused with.** Collateral provider: usually the same party, but the collateral provider is defined by the arrangement and may be a third party pledging assets.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#party-role)

**Values (draft list):** tier: see the clearing tier vocabulary

**Words people use:** “collateral provider” (contextual, in margin)

**Before using it, know:** Account, clearing service, period.

**Connected to:**

- *owes* [Margin Requirement](#margin-requirement)
- *faces* [Margin Taker](#margin-taker)

### Margin Taker

*The party that requires margin and calls for it.*

**Definition.** Role of a party that requires and calls margin from a margin provider for an account at a tier, applying its margin methodology and collateral eligibility schedule, determined by the clearing roles of the two parties at that tier: a CCP toward its clearing members or admitted participants, a clearing broker toward its clients, or a client toward its indirect clients. Usually the same party as the collateral taker under the collateral arrangement.

**Example.** CCP Z is the margin taker toward Clearing Broker Y; Clearing Broker Y is the margin taker toward Client A.

**Not to be confused with.** Central Counterparty: a CCP is one kind of margin taker; a clearing broker is another, toward its clients.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#party-role)

**Values (draft list):** tier: see the clearing tier vocabulary

**Words people use:** “collateral taker” (contextual, in margin); “secured party” (contextual, legal)

**Before using it, know:** Account, clearing service, period.

**Connected to:**

- [Margin Provider](#margin-provider) *faces* Margin Taker
- *applies* [Margin Methodology](#margin-methodology)
- *issues* [Margin Call](#margin-call)
