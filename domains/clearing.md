## Usage notes and required context

- **LD-C0010 Account**
  - Note: Has identifiers per maintaining party, a lifecycle status and an effective period. A give-up reference is an identifier pointing to the receiving member's account, not an account. Regulatory qualifications (for example active-account status) are time-bounded classifications. [07, 06]
- **LD-C0011 Segregation Model**
  - Note: Public instances, not subclasses: EMIR omnibus and individual client segregation (Art. 39); indirect-clearing account structures (Delegated Regulations (EU) 2017/2154 for listed and 2017/2155 for OTC); US futures-customer segregation, cleared-swaps LSOC (17 CFR Part 22) and foreign-futures secured amount (Part 30); paragraphs to verify. State the level (CCP or member books). Legal protection is a Legal conclusion. [02, 03, 04, 06]
- **LD-C0012 Agreement**
  - Note: The client clearing legal model belongs to LD-C0056, not to the CCP or the segregation model. [06, 03]
- **LD-C0018 Trade**
  - Note: One pair of counterparties per trade; clearing, compression and porting create successor trades linked by LD-R0034, never mutating the predecessor. How many trades one execution yields depends on the clearing legal model, allocation and averaging: record lineage, not counts. A bespoke OTC trade carries its own economic terms. UTIs are per regime. [01, 03, 06]
  - An agent must know: Clearing tier and counterparty view (LD-A0025)
- **LD-C0022 Give-up / Take-up Process**
  - Note: Listed derivatives. Qualifier "attribution timing": at registration (member designated at trade time) or after registration to the executing member. Both directions (give-up out, give-in) are in scope (sponsor decision 2026-11-01). OTC trades reach a CCP by clearing submission (backlog). [01, 02, 03]
- **LD-C0023 Clearing Acceptance**
  - Note: Contextual alias "registration". Rejection is an outcome value, not a separate concept. [02, 08]
- **LD-C0024 Novation**
  - Note: Generic legal concept: one possible mechanism of CCP interposition, and also used for bilateral step-in and step-out outside clearing. Never infer novation from "cleared" or "ported". [02, 03, 06]
- **LD-C0025 Lifecycle Event**
  - Note: Carries occurrence time, effective time and recording time, plus business dates by role. Corrections and cancellations reference the original; nothing is deleted. Event kinds are a taxonomy facet. [07, 03]
- **LD-C0026 Position**
  - An agent must know: Account (CCP or internal), instrument, clearing service, gross or net basis, quantity kind, as-of business date and cycle, asserting source

## Ambiguities and proposed answers

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### LD-A0003 — Brokerage paid (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Identify debtor, actual payer, beneficiary and recharge treatment.

**Proposed answer:** Under a give-up the client usually owes executing brokerage and the clearing broker often pays it and debits the client: two charges linked by LD-R0080, with debtor, payer and observing entity explicit. "We paid" cannot be answered without the observing entity. Confidence high. Firm decision: which model applies per client or market. [05, 02, 01]

### LD-A0007 — Account (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify function, maintaining party, holder, beneficiary and account-to-account relationships.

**Proposed answer:** Three levels: the CCP Account (LD-C0069) of each clearing service; the member's internal client account; execution-side and give-up references, which are identifiers rather than accounts. Levels are related only by explicit, time-bounded LD-R0065. Segregation regime and regulatory account statuses are separate, time-bounded dimensions. Confidence high. [02, 07, 06]

### LD-A0008 — Trade, transaction and execution (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Establish identity and lineage; Transaction is provisional and must not collapse all lifecycle events.

**Proposed answer:** Three identity levels linked by directional lineage, never by a fixed cardinality: the execution event, the trade, and successor trades after clearing (LD-R0034). Each level carries its own identifiers (for example venue trade IDs, UTI ISO 23897). The MiFIR "transaction" (Art. 26) is a regulatory reporting unit and an ambiguous alias, never LD-C0019, which stays deferred. Confidence high. [01, 03, 06, 07, 08]

### LD-A0009 — Cleared (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate acceptance status from legal effect and record effective time.

**Proposed answer:** "Cleared" is a derived status meaning that CCP Interposition (LD-C0054) has occurred in a named clearing service. Three timestamps stay distinct: clearing member acceptance (LD-C0053), CCP acceptance or registration (LD-C0023), interposition. Under open offer interposition can occur at matching, before any registration message; under novation it occurs at registration. A completed give-up changes which member holds the trade, not whether it is cleared. Confidence high on the principle, medium on each CCP's moment. Legal decision: the interposition moment per clearing service, from the rulebook. [02, 03, 06, 08]

### LD-A0010 — Successful give-up (Decided 2026-11-01 (sponsor; see decision log))

**To decide:** Define completion, partial acceptance, rejection, correction and current versus historical status.

**Proposed answer:** States per give-up line: pending, accepted (in full or in part), rejected, lapsed (not taken up by the cut-off, so the trade stays with the giving member), cancelled, reversed where permitted, and unknown (no record), which is not "pending". Proposed definition of complete: the full allocated quantity is registered to the receiving clearing member's CCP account, as evidenced by the CCP record; partial take-ups are reported separately and both member acceptance time and registration time are kept. Where the take-up is made in the CCP's system the two coincide. Current versus historical follows LD-A0023. Confidence high on the pattern, medium on state names. Firm decision: definition of complete and source of record. **Decided 2026-11-01: the proposed definition is accepted and the CCP record is authoritative; discrepancies with internal records are reported as breaks** (see the [decision log](decision-log.md)). [01, 02, 07]

### LD-A0011 — Margin required (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Distinguish IM/VM, member–CCP/client–member relationship, risk scope, time and collateral coverage.

**Proposed answer:** Undefined until five qualifiers are fixed: tier (from margin provider and taker role assignments: CCP–member house, CCP–member client account, member–client, client–indirect client); component (IM, VM, add-ons, total); account and gross or net basis; as-of time and run; currency basis. Under net omnibus accounts a client-level figure exists only in the member's own calculation. Member–client IM is a CCP replication, a replication with multiplier or add-on, or an own model (LD-C0070). Confidence high. Firm decision: default tier, if any. [04]

### LD-A0013 — Position (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify gross/net, quantity/notional/exposure, account, instrument, CCP and as-of basis.

**Proposed answer:** Key: account (CCP or internal) × instrument × clearing service × side or net basis, as of a business date and cycle, with the asserting source (CCP statement or internal). House accounts are generally net; client omnibus accounts are net or gross by account type. CCP-reported and internal positions are two records of one position; a difference is a break. OTC positions are sets of open cleared trades measured by count, notional or risk sensitivity. Confidence high. Firm decision: default basis and source, or return both labelled. [02, 03, 07]

### LD-A0016 — Product, instrument and OTC contract (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Validate identity boundaries for listed series and bespoke OTC agreements.

**Proposed answer:** Proposed (implemented in the Draft definitions of LD-C0013, LD-C0014 and LD-C0062): Product Family is a classification (CFI ISO 10962 and the UPI ISO 4914 are classification and product identifiers); Contract Specification is a venue's versioned listing template; Instrument is a standardised, counterparty-independent specification at the level at which it is listed or assigned an instrument identifier (a listed series; an OTC ISIN where assigned, with the Trade still carrying full terms); a bespoke OTC contract is a Trade, not an Instrument. Confidence high. Reference Data and OTC Product to confirm the meaning change of LD-C0014. [01, 03, 07, 08]

### LD-A0017 — Novation and acceptance (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Do not assume all clearing uses the same legal mechanism or timestamp.

**Proposed answer:** Four distinct steps: clearing member acceptance, CCP acceptance or registration, CCP interposition with its mechanism (novation, open offer, other), and settlement finality (outside current scope). Under the EU principal-to-principal model the client's contract is with the clearing member and arises from the client clearing agreement, not from the CCP's novation; in US models the FCM guarantees performance, and who is principal to the CCP contract varies by product and rulebook. OTC "novation" also names bilateral step-in and step-out, which is not clearing. Confidence high. [02, 03, 06]

### LD-A0019 — Clearing broker, clearing member, FCM (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Service role toward the client versus admission at a CCP versus a regulatory registration (FCM in the US).

**Proposed answer:** Clearing Broker (LD-C0057) is the service role toward the client and may exist without membership of the clearing service; Clearing Member (LD-C0007) is admission at the CCP; FCM is a US regulatory registration held by a legal entity, recorded as a Regulatory Classification (LD-C0068), and only a contextual alias. Confidence high. [02, 03, 05, 06, 08]

### LD-A0020 — Service (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** A CCP's clearing service versus the firm's service offering versus a MiFID investment service; also the service mix (execution-only, clearing-only, execute-and-clear).

**Proposed answer:** Two concepts, no common parent for now: a CCP's Clearing Service (LD-C0051) and a provider's Service Offering (LD-C0052), linked by LD-R0082. A MiFID "investment service" is a regulatory category, not either concept. Confidence high. [02, 03, 05, 07, 08]

### LD-A0023 — Current, as of, as known at (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Valid time versus record time: valid now and latest non-superseded record, valid at a business date, or known by a timestamp.

**Proposed answer:** Current = valid now and latest non-superseded record from the stated source; as of D = valid at business date D; as known at T = only records received by T. Every answer states source, as-of date and freshness. See the time convention in [modelling guidelines](../docs/modelling-guidelines.md). Confidence high. Firm decision: whether "as known at" history is needed. [07, 08, 06]

### LD-A0025 — The trade (clearing tier) (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Pre-clearing (executed) trade versus CCP-facing trade versus member-client trade; which tier, identifier and counterparty view a question uses.

**Proposed answer:** State the tier: pre-clearing trade, CCP-facing trade, member–client trade (principal model). Successor trades are linked by LD-R0034 and may carry new identifiers. Confidence high. [03]

### LD-A0026 — Give-up, transfer, porting (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Listed give-up versus voluntary position transfer versus default porting versus OTC or FX prime-brokerage "give-up"; trigger, consent, rule, fees and whether collateral moves.

**Proposed answer:** Give-up (LD-C0022) attributes a listed trade to a receiving clearing member; a position transfer moves open positions between CCP accounts under transfer rules, usually voluntarily; porting is the default-triggered transfer of a defaulting member's client positions and collateral to a back-up member, or their close-out (EMIR Art. 48(5) to (7), to verify). Positions and collateral port as separate legs. OTC or FX "give-up" usually means prime-brokerage give-up. Position Transfer and Porting are backlog concepts. Confidence high. [02, 03, 04, 06]

### LD-A0028 — Omnibus, house and client (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** EMIR omnibus (net or gross) versus US customer omnibus versus indirect omnibus versus internal pooled account; CCP account type versus economic relationship of affiliates.

**Proposed answer:** EMIR omnibus and individual client segregation (Art. 39(2) and (3), to verify), US futures-customer omnibus, indirect-client omnibus account types (Delegated Regulations (EU) 2017/2154 and 2017/2155, to verify) and internal pooled accounts are different things; the CCP account type does not determine the economic relationship (affiliates may be cleared as clients or in the house account). Confidence high. [02, 04, 03]

### LD-A0029 — Eligible, mandatory, offered, covered (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** CCP clearing eligibility versus a regulatory clearing obligation for a counterparty pair versus the firm's offering versus "we cover market X" (membership, third-party access or actual offering).

**Proposed answer:** Eligibility is a CCP fact (LD-R0050); a clearing obligation applies to a counterparty pair, a product class including trade-term ranges, and a date; offering and coverage are firm facts in the internal overlay, through a Market Access Arrangement. Never infer one from another. Confidence high. [03, 05, 06]

### LD-A0030 — Client clearing model (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Combination of access model, legal model, segregation model and margining basis; model offered versus legal protection or portability actually achieved.

**Proposed answer:** Never resolve to a single concept: decompose into access model (LD-C0059), legal model (LD-C0056), segregation model (LD-C0011) and margining basis (LD-C0069). The model offered says nothing by itself about legal protection or portability achieved, which only Legal concludes. Confidence high. [05, 06]

### LD-A0032 — OTC and listed (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Regulatory "OTC" (derivatives not executed on a regulated market or equivalent) versus off-venue versus not exchange-listed; cleared is a separate dimension.

**Proposed answer:** Under EMIR Art. 2(7) a derivative not executed on a regulated market or an equivalent third-country market is OTC, so MTF and OTF trades count as OTC; venue type is a Regulatory Classification of LD-C0009. Scope modules by listing and clearing structure, not by that label. Confidence high. [06, 02, 03]

### LD-A0034 — Break, exception, reject, fail (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Reconciliation break versus operational exception versus rejected or lapsed give-up versus settlement fail versus technology incident.

**Proposed answer:** A reconciliation break says records disagree, not which is wrong; an operational exception (LD-C0030) is a situation requiring handling and may be raised by a break; a rejected or lapsed give-up is an event and may raise an exception but is not a trade failure; a settlement fail concerns LD-C0028; a technology incident is out of scope. Confidence high. [07, 02]
