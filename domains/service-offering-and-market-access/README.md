## Concepts at a glance

- **[Service Offering](concepts.md#service-offering)** — A service the firm sells to clients, such as execute-and-clear on listed equity index futures for institutional clients, described by what it covers, where and through which channels.
  - **[Service Component](concepts.md#service-component)** — One building block of a service, for example average pricing inside an execute-and-clear service.
  - **[Offering Coverage Line](concepts.md#offering-coverage-line)** — One line of 'where and for what' a service is available, and which access route makes it possible.
- **[Market Access Arrangement](concepts.md#market-access-arrangement)** — The route by which someone gets into one venue or one clearing house: as a member, through another member, as a client, or under a sponsored model, with its status and dates.
  - **[Direct Electronic Access Arrangement](concepts.md#direct-electronic-access-arrangement)** — Permission given by a venue member for a client to send its own orders to the venue under the member's identity.
  - **[Third-Party Execution Arrangement](concepts.md#third-party-execution-arrangement)** — A broker reaches a venue where it is not a member by passing orders to a broker that is.
  - **[Non-Clearing Member Arrangement](concepts.md#non-clearing-member-arrangement)** — A venue member that is not a clearing member gets its trades cleared by a clearing member that is.
  - **[Sponsored Clearing Arrangement](concepts.md#sponsored-clearing-arrangement)** — A client is admitted directly to a clearing house, with a clearing member standing behind it under the clearing house's rules.
  - **[Indirect Clearing Arrangement](concepts.md#indirect-clearing-arrangement)** — Clearing for someone who is a client of a client of the clearing member.
  - **[Access Onboarding](concepts.md#access-onboarding)** — The steps the firm, or a client, goes through to get a new route into a venue or clearing house.
  - **[Connectivity Route](concepts.md#connectivity-route)** — The technical line used to reach a venue or clearing house.
- **[Offering Eligibility Criterion](concepts.md#offering-eligibility-criterion)** — A rule the firm applies to decide which clients can get a service.
- **[Access Agreement](concepts.md#access-agreement)** — A signed document that sets up or governs a route into a venue or clearing house.
- **[Access Requirement](concepts.md#access-requirement)** — A condition a venue, clearing house or regulator sets before someone can use a given route in.
