## 1. Concepts you approve

| Concept | Definition | Your verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **[Service Offering](concepts.md#service-offering)** | Defined execution, clearing or related post-trade service that a contracting entity makes available to clients under stated terms, identified by its service kind (execution-only, clearing-only, execute-and-clear, or a related post-trade service such as average pricing or handling of trades given in), its product scope stated by product family, contract specification or clearing contract, the trading venues, venue segments or clearing services it relies on, its order handling channels and the client segments it is offered to, with a status and an effective period. The service kind indicates the usual give-up direction (execution-only usually means give-up out, clearing-only usually means give-in, by clearing submission for OTC); the actual direction of each trade is recorded on the give-up line and can differ. Distinct from a central counterparty's clearing service, from the Client role a client holds toward the contracting entity for this offering, and from the prices agreed with that client. | |
| **[Market Access Arrangement](concepts.md#market-access-arrangement)** | Access relationship by which one party can execute on one trading venue or venue segment, or clear in one clearing service, under one access model (execution: own venue membership by category, through a third-party executing member, or direct electronic access as direct market access or sponsored access; clearing: own clearing membership by category, as a non-clearing member through a general clearing member, client clearing, indirect clearing, or sponsored clearing or a direct-client model), naming the intermediary through which the party reaches the venue or clearing service; where clearing passes through a chain, each link is a separate arrangement and the chain is recorded in order. It records the role it confers, a status (only a live arrangement can be relied on) and an effective period, and is documented by one or more agreements, which are separate objects and may serve several arrangements. Execution access and clearing access are always separate arrangements. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[Service Component](concepts.md#service-component)** *(level 2)* | Separately describable element of a service offering that the provider performs and may document, price or withdraw on its own, such as order execution, give-up processing, take-up of trades given in, average pricing, allocation, clearing and position maintenance, margin and collateral handling, exercise and delivery handling, or delegated regulatory reporting. A component belongs to one or more offerings; it is not a service offering unless sold on its own. | |
| **[Offering Coverage Line](concepts.md#offering-coverage-line)** *(level 2)* | One entry in a service offering's coverage: the offering is available for a stated product scope (product family, contract specification or clearing contract) on one trading venue or venue segment, or in one clearing service, relying on a stated market access arrangement, with a status and an effective period. | |
| **[Offering Eligibility Criterion](concepts.md#offering-eligibility-criterion)** *(level 2)* | Condition set by the provider that a client must meet to receive a service offering or one of its coverage lines: party type, client segment, Client Regulatory Category or Counterparty Regulatory Status, jurisdiction, credit approval or documents signed, each as a separate criterion. A criterion that applies a regulatory condition refers to the Regulatory Classification or Regulatory Obligation it applies. Instances are recorded only in the internal layer. | |
| **[Access Agreement](concepts.md#access-agreement)** *(level 2)* | Agreement that documents a market access arrangement and is not already a give-up agreement or a client clearing agreement: admission to a venue or clearing service under its rules, a direct electronic access agreement between a venue member and its client, an order-routing agreement with a third-party venue member, a non-clearing member clearing agreement, or a sponsorship or clearing agent agreement under a CCP sponsored model. One agreement may document several arrangements, and one arrangement may need several agreements. | |
| **[Access Onboarding](concepts.md#access-onboarding)** *(level 2)* | Process by which a party obtains a market access arrangement: choice of access model and category, application, the venue's, CCP's or intermediary's due diligence, legal documentation, meeting access requirements, connectivity set-up and conformance testing, static data and account set-up, and go-live; it ends when the arrangement is live or the application is withdrawn. Its steps are reported as events with dates. | |
| **[Access Requirement](concepts.md#access-requirement)** *(level 2)* | Rule provision of a trading venue, CCP, regulator or intermediary that a party must meet to obtain or keep a given access model or membership category, such as minimum capital, default fund contribution, regulatory authorisation or registration, operational and risk-management capability, technical conformance, qualified staff, settlement and payment arrangements, or pre-trade controls for direct electronic access; versioned, with issuer and effective period. | |
| **[Connectivity Route](concepts.md#connectivity-route)** *(level 2)* | Technical path by which a holder of a market access arrangement sends and receives orders, trade registrations or clearing messages with a venue or CCP: a direct connection (co-located or remote), a connection through an independent software vendor or service bureau, through another member's infrastructure, or an order-routing network; with its sessions or user identifiers, status and effective period. | |
| **[Direct Electronic Access Arrangement](concepts.md#direct-electronic-access-arrangement)** *(level 2)* | Market access arrangement in which a venue member (the provider) permits a client (the user) to send orders electronically to a trading venue under the member's trading identity, as direct market access through the member's infrastructure or as sponsored access; the provider and the user are the two named parties of the arrangement, not new roles. Orders on the direct market access or sponsored access channel require a live arrangement of the same form. | |
| **[Third-Party Execution Arrangement](concepts.md#third-party-execution-arrangement)** *(level 2)* | Market access arrangement by which a party that is not a member of a trading venue, or is a member but chooses not to use its membership for that business, has orders executed there by a Third-party Executing Member acting under an agreement with it; brokerage owed to that member is executing brokerage paid. | |
| **[Non-Clearing Member Arrangement](concepts.md#non-clearing-member-arrangement)** *(level 2)* | Market access arrangement by which a trading venue member that is not a clearing member of the linked clearing service has its trades cleared by a general clearing member; it is documented by a Client Clearing Agreement (a CCP standard form where one exists, to verify), and the non-clearing member is a client of that clearing member. | |
| **[Sponsored Clearing Arrangement](concepts.md#sponsored-clearing-arrangement)** *(level 2)* | Market access arrangement under a CCP's sponsored or direct-client model by which a party is admitted to a clearing service with trades registered in its own name, with a Sponsoring Clearing Member as intermediary; 'clearing agent' is recorded as a value where the rulebook uses that term (to verify). | |
| **[Indirect Clearing Arrangement](concepts.md#indirect-clearing-arrangement)** *(level 2)* | Market access arrangement by which a party clears in a clearing service as a client of a client of a clearing member (or further down a chain); each link is a separate arrangement and the chain is recorded in order. It results in an Indirect Client Account at the CCP, whose forms are defined on the clearing services page. | |

## 3. Open questions on meaning

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### Service (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** A CCP's clearing service versus the firm's service offering versus a MiFID investment service; also the service mix (execution-only, clearing-only, execute-and-clear).

**Proposed answer:** Two concepts, no common parent for now: a CCP's Clearing Service and a provider's Service Offering, linked by a “relies on” link. A MiFID "investment service" is a regulatory category, not either concept. Confidence high. [02, 03, 05, 07, 08]

<sub>reference LD-A0020</sub>

### Product (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Product family classification versus a venue's contract specification versus a listed series versus a service offering ("our clearing product").

**Proposed answer:** "Product" returns candidates Product Family, Contract Specification, Instrument and Service Offering unless context selects one; a venue's "product code" usually designates a Contract Specification. Confidence high. [01, 05, 07]

<sub>reference LD-A0021</sub>

### Eligible, mandatory, offered, covered (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** CCP clearing eligibility versus a regulatory clearing obligation for a counterparty pair versus the firm's offering versus "we cover market X" (membership, third-party access or actual offering).

**Proposed answer:** Eligibility is a CCP fact; a clearing obligation applies to a counterparty pair, a product class including trade-term ranges, and a date; offering and coverage are firm facts in the internal overlay, through a Market Access Arrangement. Never infer one from another. Confidence high. [03, 05, 06]

<sub>reference LD-A0029</sub>

### Client clearing model (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Combination of access model, legal model, segregation model and margining basis; model offered versus legal protection or portability actually achieved.

**Proposed answer:** Never resolve to a single concept: decompose into access model (Market Access Arrangement), legal model (Client Clearing Agreement), Segregation Model and margining basis (CCP Account). The model offered says nothing by itself about legal protection or portability achieved, which only Legal concludes. Confidence high. [05, 06]

<sub>reference LD-A0030</sub>
