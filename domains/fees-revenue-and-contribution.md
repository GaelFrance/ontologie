### Usage notes and required context

- **LD-C0031 Fee Charge**
  - Note: Facets: amount certainty (estimated, final) and billing stage (accrued, invoiced, disputed, adjusted, settled, written off). Proposed definitional cardinality: exactly one debtor and one creditor. [05, 08]
- **LD-C0032 Fee Schedule**
  - Note: Generic parent of public venue or CCP tariffs and negotiated client terms, distinguished by issuer and scope; versioned; a Rule Change can change it. [05]
- **LD-C0033 Client Execution Fee**
  - Note: State the charge basis (per contract side, round turn, allocation, ticket, notional) and grain. An all-in rate must be decomposed before comparison with LD-C0036 or LD-C0037. [01]
- **LD-C0035 Executing Broker Brokerage Charge**
  - Note: Viewpoint matters: received as executing broker, paid and recharged as clearing broker; debtor, payer and recharge explicit. Label unchanged. [01, 05]
- **LD-C0036 Exchange Fee Charge**
  - Note: Creditor is the venue operator; record the collector separately; service-nature facet mandatory. [05, 02]
- **LD-C0037 CCP Fee Charge**
  - Note: Distinct from default-fund contributions and from interest on cash collateral; charges for holding non-cash collateral are CCP fee charges with service nature "collateral". [04, 05]
- **LD-C0040 Cost**
  - Note: Cost-category facet: non-recharged venue and CCP fees, third-party brokerage, third-party clearing, connectivity and market data, funding and collateral, allocated shared cost. [05]
- **LD-C0042 Clearing Revenue**
  - Note: Inclusion of collateral income (interest on client cash, price alignment pass-through) is undecided and never implicit. [04, 05]

## 3. Ambiguities and proposed answers

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### LD-A0001 — Execution fee (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate client execution charges, executing-broker brokerage and exchange charges.

**Proposed answer:** Three charges, separated by creditor and service: the client execution charge (LD-C0033); executing brokerage on given-up executions (LD-C0035); the venue transaction fee (LD-C0036), whose debtor is set by venue or CCP rules. "Execution fee" is an ambiguous alias of all three. Confidence high. Firm decision: all-in versus fees-plus client pricing. [01, 05]

### LD-A0002 — Fee, revenue and cash received (Open, narrowed 2026-09-20, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate charges, management revenue and cash settlement; define timing, gross/net, pass-through treatment and the boundary with contribution after costs.

**Proposed answer:** A charge accrues at its chargeable event (LD-R0078), is then invoiced or debited and finally settled (LD-C0029); amount certainty (estimated or final) and billing stage are facets of LD-C0031. Collateral income is a separate stream whose inclusion in clearing revenue is undecided. Gross or net treatment of pass-through charges is a Finance decision; accounting principal-versus-agent guidance is only an analogue. Confidence high on stages, medium on treatment. [05, 04, 02]

### LD-A0003 — Brokerage paid (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Identify debtor, actual payer, beneficiary and recharge treatment.

**Proposed answer:** Under a give-up the client usually owes executing brokerage and the clearing broker often pays it and debits the client: two charges linked by LD-R0080, with debtor, payer and observing entity explicit. "We paid" cannot be answered without the observing entity. Confidence high. Firm decision: which model applies per client or market. [05, 02, 01]

### LD-A0004 — Commission (Open, deferred by sponsor, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Validate whether this is a remuneration category, contractual charge type or local label.

**Proposed answer:** In listed derivatives usually the client-facing per-contract remuneration (per side or round turn) for execution, clearing or both: a commercial label, not an economic category. Keep LD-C0038 Draft with no fee parent and treat "commission" as a blocked alias that returns candidates (LD-C0033, LD-C0034, a bundled execute-and-clear charge). Deferred by the sponsor. Confidence medium. [05, 01]

### LD-A0005 — Exchange fees (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate service nature, economic beneficiary and collecting organisation; bundled bills need explicit decomposition.

**Proposed answer:** Decompose bundled bills by service nature (trading, post-trade, market data, connectivity, membership) before aggregating; the creditor is the venue operator and the collecting organisation (often the CCP) is recorded separately (LD-R0022). CCPs and clearing members publicly disclose prices and fees per service, including discounts and rebates (EMIR Art. 38(1), to verify). Confidence high. Firm decision: treatment of market data, connectivity and rebate netting. [05, 02]

### LD-A0014 — Revenue by client (Open, narrowed 2026-09-20 and 2026-11-01, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Use the approved management perspective; specify client attribution, consolidation, internal eliminations, measurement period, reporting currency and any separately defined cost deductions.

**Proposed answer:** Sponsor decision 2026-11-01: execution-only, clearing-only and execute-and-clear are all offered; give-up and give-in are both supported; revenue is attributed per service. Proposed: attribute to the contractual client of each service relationship (LD-R0077), show the service mix, roll up to Commercial Group, flag intra-group clients for elimination; mandatory context is observing entity, management basis, period and currency. Open: cost perimeter for Contribution (LD-C0074), pass-through and collateral income treatment. [05, 01, 04]

### LD-A0018 — Fee Charge and Commission types (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Validate estimated versus enforceable charges and avoid additive counting of overlapping fee categories.

**Proposed answer:** An estimated charge is a Fee Charge whose amount certainty is "estimated"; billing stage is a facet. Proposed additive key: creditor × service nature × chargeable event; "commission", "execution fee" and bundled labels are non-additive views over it. Confidence high. Firm decision: Finance confirms the additive key. [05, 08]

### LD-A0033 — Volume (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Per side versus round turn versus trades versus notional; single- or double-counted; executed versus cleared; published versus firm data.

**Proposed answer:** State unit (contract side, round turn, trade, notional), counting basis (single or double), stage (executed or cleared) and source (published or firm). Confidence high. [05, 01]
