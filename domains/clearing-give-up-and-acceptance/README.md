# Clearing: give-up and acceptance

> Generated by `tools/build_domain_views.py`; edit `overview.md`, `company-questions.md` or the registers instead.

Many clients execute listed futures and options with one broker and clear them with another. The step that moves a trade from the broker that executed it to the broker that will clear it is the **give-up**. Seen from the receiving side, the same step is a **give-in**. This domain describes that hand-over, the decisions that accept or refuse it, and the moment a central counterparty (CCP) legally steps into the trade.

## How it flows

ETD: [Allocation Instruction](../execution/concepts.md#allocation-instruction) → give-up ([Give-up / Take-up Process](concepts.md#give-up--take-up-process)) → member take-up or claim ([Clearing Member Acceptance](concepts.md#clearing-member-acceptance)) before the [Cut-off](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#cut-off) → [Clearing Acceptance](concepts.md#clearing-acceptance) → [CCP Interposition](concepts.md#ccp-interposition) → trade in the receiving member's [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account) and [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position); if rejected or lapsed: [Operational Exception](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#operational-exception) and the trade stays with the giving member. OTC, for comparison: bilateral [Trade](../execution/concepts.md#trade) → [Clearing Submission](concepts.md#clearing-submission) → clearing broker limit check ([Clearing Member Acceptance](concepts.md#clearing-member-acceptance)) → [Clearing Acceptance](concepts.md#clearing-acceptance) → interposition by Novation ([CCP Interposition](concepts.md#ccp-interposition), [Novation](concepts.md#novation))

## Concepts at a glance

- **[Give-up Agreement](concepts.md#give-up-agreement)** — The signed agreement that lets a client trade with one broker and clear with another, and says who pays and who is responsible if the clearing broker does not take the trade.
- **[Give-up / Take-up Process](concepts.md#give-up--take-up-process)** — The hand-over of an executed listed trade, in whole or in part, from the clearing member that holds it to the clearing member that will clear it for the client; give-in is the same hand-over seen by the receiver.
  - **[Give-up Designated in Advance](concepts.md#give-up-designated-in-advance)** — A give-up where the clearing broker is named up front and the CCP books the trade straight to it.
  - **[Give-up after First Registration](concepts.md#give-up-after-first-registration)** — A give-up where the trade first lands with the executing side's clearing member and is handed over afterwards.
  - **[Clearing Member Acceptance](concepts.md#clearing-member-acceptance)** — The receiving clearing member's yes or no (or partial yes) to taking clearing responsibility for a trade, whether by an explicit take-up, automatically within a limit, or by consenting to an OTC clearing submission.
    - **[Take-up](concepts.md#take-up)** — The receiving clearing member saying 'yes, I take this give-up line' (or part of it, or no).
    - **[Submission Acceptance](concepts.md#submission-acceptance)** — The OTC clearing broker's yes or no to clearing a swap that has been sent for clearing.
  - **[Give-up Line](concepts.md#give-up-line)** — One request to hand a stated quantity of a trade to one other clearing member's account; give-up progress is tracked line by line.
  - **[Give-up Registration](concepts.md#give-up-registration)** — The moment the CCP actually books the given-up quantity into the receiving member's account; this is what makes a give-up complete.
- **[Clearing Acceptance](concepts.md#clearing-acceptance)** — The CCP accepting a trade into one of its clearing services and registering it in a clearing member's account.
- **[CCP Interposition](concepts.md#ccp-interposition)** — The legal moment when the CCP becomes the buyer to every seller and the seller to every buyer for a trade; this is what 'cleared' really means.
- **[Novation](concepts.md#novation)** — Replacing an existing contract or obligation with new ones, as the law allows; in clearing it is one way the CCP steps in, and outside clearing it is also how parties step in or out of a bilateral trade.
- **[Giving Clearing Member](concepts.md#giving-clearing-member)** — The clearing member that gives up this line.
- **[Receiving Clearing Member](concepts.md#receiving-clearing-member)** — The clearing member named to receive this line.
- **[Clearing Limit](concepts.md#clearing-limit)** — The pre-agreed ceiling up to which a clearing broker accepts a client's trades without a manual check.
- **[Take-up Deadline](concepts.md#take-up-deadline)** — The CCP's latest time on a clearing date for sending and accepting a give-up, and what happens if it is missed.
- **[Clearing Submission](concepts.md#clearing-submission)** — The request sent to a CCP, usually for an OTC trade, asking it to clear the trade through named clearing brokers.
- **[Execution Agreement for Cleared Trades](concepts.md#execution-agreement-for-cleared-trades)** — The OTC agreement that says what happens to a swap meant for clearing if the clearing broker or CCP refuses it.
