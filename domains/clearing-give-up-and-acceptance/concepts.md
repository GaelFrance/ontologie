### Give-up Agreement

*The signed agreement that lets a client trade with one broker and clear with another, and says who pays and who is responsible if the clearing broker does not take the trade.*

**Definition.** Agreement among a client, which may sign and instruct through its investment manager acting as its authorised representative, one or more executing brokers and one or more clearing brokers, that sets how the client's listed-derivative executions are given up to and taken up by the clearing broker: the accounts and products covered, acceptance conditions and any clearing limits it refers to, responsibility for trades not taken up or rejected, executing brokerage terms, billing and payment. It permits give-ups; it does not by itself oblige the clearing broker to accept any particular trade, which remains a Clearing Member Acceptance.

**Example.** Client A, Executing Broker X and Clearing Broker Y sign a give-up agreement so that X can give up Client A's futures trades to Y.

**Not to be confused with.** Client Clearing Agreement: that one governs the clearing service Y provides to Client A; the give-up agreement only governs the hand-over from X to Y.

**Kind of:** [Agreement](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#agreement)

**Connected to:**

- [Give-up / Take-up Process](#give-up--take-up-process) *governed by* Give-up Agreement — Where one exists; a missing or inapplicable agreement is a common rejection cause.
- [Access Agreement](../service-offering-and-market-access/concepts.md#access-agreement) *is distinct from* Give-up Agreement
- [Standing Allocation Instruction](../execution/concepts.md#standing-allocation-instruction) *is often agreed alongside* Give-up Agreement
- [Take-up](#take-up) *is checked against* Give-up Agreement
- [Clearing Limit](#clearing-limit) *may be referred to in* Give-up Agreement
- [Execution Agreement for Cleared Trades](#execution-agreement-for-cleared-trades) *is the otc counterpart of* Give-up Agreement
- [Client Pricing Terms](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#client-pricing-terms) *may set brokerage terms together with* Give-up Agreement

### Give-up / Take-up Process

*The hand-over of an executed listed trade, in whole or in part, from the clearing member that holds it to the clearing member that will clear it for the client; give-in is the same hand-over seen by the receiver.*

**Definition.** Process by which all or part of an executed listed-derivative trade, including a trade registered on a venue after private negotiation and leg executions of a listed strategy, is attributed for clearing from the clearing member to which it was first registered or would be registered (the giving clearing member) to a receiving clearing member, through one give-up line per allocation line routed to another clearing member, and that member's acceptance or rejection. Depending on the clearing service it is designated in advance, so that the CCP registers the trade straight to the receiving member, or happens after first registration to the giving member. Each give-up line ends accepted in full, accepted in part, rejected, lapsed, cancelled or reversed; a give-up is complete when the full quantity is registered to the receiving member's CCP account per the CCP record. Give-in is the same process seen by the receiving side. Instructions, acceptance decisions, registrations and legal effects remain distinct. — *meaning changed in 2026-11, accepted as Draft*

**Example.** Executing Broker X fills 100 futures for Client A on Venue V; X's clearing member gives them up to Clearing Broker Y, which takes them up before the deadline.

**Not to be confused with.** Position Transfer: a transfer moves open positions between accounts later, under transfer rules; a give-up attributes a newly executed trade.

**Breaks down into:** [Give-up Designated in Advance](#give-up-designated-in-advance) (kind of), [Give-up after First Registration](#give-up-after-first-registration) (kind of), [Clearing Member Acceptance](#clearing-member-acceptance) (part of), [Give-up Line](#give-up-line) (part of), [Give-up Registration](#give-up-registration) (step in)

**Words people use:** “give-in” (Listed give-up, receiving side's view); “give-up (OTC or FX)” (OTC and FX prime brokerage)

**Note.** Listed derivatives. Qualifier "attribution timing": at registration (member designated at trade time) or after registration to the executing member. Both directions (give-up out, give-in) are in scope (sponsor decision 2026-11-01). OTC trades reach a CCP by clearing submission (backlog). [01, 02, 03]

**Connected to:**

- *concerns* [Trade](../execution/concepts.md#trade) — Carry quantity, counterparties and lifecycle history.
- *has receiving member* [Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-member) — An intended recipient is not proof of acceptance (Clearing Member Acceptance). Label changed from receiving_member on 2026-11-01; meaning unchanged.
- *has giving member* [Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-member) — The clearing member holding the trade before give-up, normally the executing side's clearing member.
- *governed by* [Give-up Agreement](#give-up-agreement) — Where one exists; a missing or inapplicable agreement is a common rejection cause.
- [Allocation](../execution/concepts.md#allocation) *may initiate* Give-up / Take-up Process — Only when the target account is carried by a clearing member other than the one to which the execution is registered; otherwise the allocation is internal.
- [Account Identifier](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#account-identifier) *is used to route* Give-up / Take-up Process
- [Block Trade](../execution/concepts.md#block-trade) *can be given up like any other execution* Give-up / Take-up Process
- [Executed Trade](../execution/concepts.md#executed-trade) *may be given up through* Give-up / Take-up Process
- [Giving Clearing Member](#giving-clearing-member) *gives up in* Give-up / Take-up Process

### Give-up Designated in Advance

*A give-up where the clearing broker is named up front and the CCP books the trade straight to it.*

**Definition.** Kind of Give-up / Take-up Process in which the receiving clearing member is designated before or at execution (on the order or through a standing arrangement) and accepts, so that the CCP registers the trade directly in the receiving member's account and the giving side never carries it. Where it exists depends on each clearing service's rules (to verify per CCP).

**Example.** Client A's order on Venue V names Clearing Broker Y; Y has pre-approved Client A within a limit, so CCP Z registers the fill directly in Y's account.

**Not to be confused with.** Give-up after Registration: there the executing side's clearing member holds the trade first and hands it over later.

**Kind of:** [Give-up / Take-up Process](#give-up--take-up-process)

**Words people use:** “give-up at registration” (former working name); “CMTA” (US listed options usage, to verify)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service and its rules on designation; whether the designation was accepted before registration.

**Connected to:**

- *usually relies on* [Clearing Limit](#clearing-limit)
- *coincides with* [Clearing Acceptance](#clearing-acceptance)

### Give-up after First Registration

*A give-up where the trade first lands with the executing side's clearing member and is handed over afterwards.*

**Definition.** Kind of Give-up / Take-up Process in which the CCP first registers the trade to the executing side's clearing member, which carries it, with its margin, fees and default exposure, until the receiving member takes it up and the CCP records the accepted quantity in the receiving member's account. Where it applies depends on each clearing service's rules (to verify per CCP).

**Example.** CCP Z registers Client A's fill in the account of Executing Broker X's clearing member at matching; Clearing Broker Y takes it up two hours later.

**Not to be confused with.** Position Transfer: a transfer moves open positions after the give-up window, under different rules and often for a fee.

**Kind of:** [Give-up / Take-up Process](#give-up--take-up-process)

**Words people use:** “give-up after registration” (former working name)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service, clearing date, deadline and its consequence; who carries margin while the line is pending.

**Connected to:**

- *is carried meanwhile by* [Giving Clearing Member](#giving-clearing-member)
- *must finish before* [Take-up Deadline](#take-up-deadline)
- *ends with* [Give-up Registration](#give-up-registration)

### Clearing Member Acceptance

*The receiving clearing member's yes or no (or partial yes) to taking clearing responsibility for a trade, whether by an explicit take-up, automatically within a limit, or by consenting to an OTC clearing submission.*

**Definition.** Decision by a clearing member to accept, wholly or partly, or to reject clearing responsibility for an identified trade or allocated quantity, for an identified client or account in a specified clearing service. It is explicit (the take-up or claim of a listed give-up line, or consent to an OTC clearing submission) or automatic within a clearing limit set before execution. Outcome values: accepted in full, accepted in part, rejected. Distinct from CCP acceptance or registration (Clearing Acceptance) and from CCP Interposition.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y takes up a give-up line for Client A's account after checking the give-up agreement and Client A's limit.

**Not to be confused with.** Clearing Acceptance: that is the CCP accepting or registering the trade; the member's decision can come before it or at the same moment.

**Part of:** [Give-up / Take-up Process](#give-up--take-up-process)

**Breaks down into:** [Take-up](#take-up) (kind of), [Submission Acceptance](#submission-acceptance) (kind of)

**Words people use:** “take-up, claim” (Listed give-up, receiving member)

**Connected to:**

- *decided by* [Clearing Member](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-member) — Record outcome (accepted, partially accepted, rejected), quantity and time.
- *concerns* [Trade](../execution/concepts.md#trade) — Or an allocated quantity of it; designation of a member is not its acceptance.
- [Give-up Line](#give-up-line) *is answered by* Clearing Member Acceptance
- [Receiving Clearing Member](#receiving-clearing-member) *decides* Clearing Member Acceptance

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Cleared” (open, answer proposed); “Successful give-up” (decided 2026-11-01 (sponsor; see decision log)); “Novation and acceptance” (open, answer proposed); “Break, exception, reject, fail” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Take-up

*The receiving clearing member saying 'yes, I take this give-up line' (or part of it, or no).*

**Definition.** Kind of Clearing Member Acceptance in which the receiving clearing member explicitly accepts (claims) all or part of the quantity of an identified give-up line for an identified account, in the CCP's system or a give-up facility, before the take-up deadline. Outcome: accepted in full, accepted in part or rejected, with a reason. Where it is made in the CCP's own system it may itself be the give-up registration.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y claims 60 of the 100 lots on Client A's give-up line and rejects 40 because the account is not set up.

**Not to be confused with.** Lapse: a line nobody takes up by the deadline lapses; a lapse is not a rejection and is counted apart.

**Kind of:** [Clearing Member Acceptance](#clearing-member-acceptance)

**Values (draft list):** outcome: accepted in full; accepted in part; rejected; non-acceptance reason (seed values for Phase 3): unknown account or give-up reference; no give-up agreement; clearing limit exceeded; product not covered; price or quantity mismatch; submitted after the deadline (only when actively rejected for lateness)

**Words people use:** “claim” (listed, contextual); “accept give-in” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service, deadline, whether the facility is the CCP's own or a third party's, partial take-up allowed or not.

**Connected to:**

- *answers* [Give-up Line](#give-up-line)
- *may be applied automatically within* [Clearing Limit](#clearing-limit)
- *is checked against* [Give-up Agreement](#give-up-agreement)

### Submission Acceptance

*The OTC clearing broker's yes or no to clearing a swap that has been sent for clearing.*

**Definition.** Kind of Clearing Member Acceptance in which a clearing member, or the clearing broker acting through it, accepts or refuses to clear an identified OTC trade submitted for clearing, for an identified client or account in a specified clearing service, usually after checking it against a clearing limit, manually or automatically.

**Example.** Client A's swap with Dealer D is sent to CCP Z; Clearing Broker Y's limit check passes and Y consents automatically.

**Not to be confused with.** Take-up: take-up answers a listed give-up line; submission acceptance answers an OTC clearing submission.

**Kind of:** [Clearing Member Acceptance](#clearing-member-acceptance)

**Values (draft list):** outcome: accepted; rejected; non-acceptance reason (seed values for Phase 3): clearing limit exceeded; product not eligible; missing or invalid account; no execution agreement for cleared trades

**Words people use:** “consent” (OTC clearing, contextual); “limit check or credit check” (OTC, contextual); “clearing broker approval” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service, regime and its acceptance-timing rules, whether the check was pre-execution or post-execution.

**Connected to:**

- *answers* [Clearing Submission](#clearing-submission)
- *applies* [Clearing Limit](#clearing-limit)
- *precedes* [Clearing Acceptance](#clearing-acceptance)

### Give-up Line

*One request to hand a stated quantity of a trade to one other clearing member's account; give-up progress is tracked line by line.*

**Definition.** Part of a Give-up / Take-up Process: the request, made by or for the giving clearing member through the CCP or a give-up facility, to attribute a stated quantity of an identified trade, at a stated price, to a named receiving clearing member and account (usually through a give-up reference). One give-up line arises from one allocation line routed to another clearing member, or from a designation made before execution. It is the unit at which give-up status, member acceptance and registration are recorded. Its status takes the harmonised values below, is asserted by a named source record with the source's native status kept beside it, and is never set by inference; the line is complete only when its full quantity is recorded in the receiving member's CCP account, as the CCP record shows.

**Example.** Client A's 100 lots are allocated 60 to Clearing Broker Y and 40 to Clearing Broker W: the giving member sends two give-up lines.

**Not to be confused with.** Allocation Line: the allocation line is the client's split of the quantity; a give-up line exists only for the part routed to another clearing member.

**Part of:** [Give-up / Take-up Process](#give-up--take-up-process)

**Values (draft list):** status: pending; accepted in full; accepted in part; rejected; lapsed (not taken up by the deadline; the quantity stays with the giving member); cancelled; reversed; unknown

**Words people use:** “give-up instruction” (listed, giving side); “allocation” (CCP or give-up facility screens, contextual); “alleged give-in” (receiving side, contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service and clearing date; which side the firm is on (giving or receiving); whether the status comes from the CCP record or an internal record.

**Connected to:**

- *is created from* [Allocation Line](../execution/concepts.md#allocation-line)
- *carries out part of* [Allocation Instruction](../execution/concepts.md#allocation-instruction)
- *concerns a quantity of* [Trade](../execution/concepts.md#trade)
- *names* [Receiving Clearing Member](#receiving-clearing-member)
- *is answered by* [Clearing Member Acceptance](#clearing-member-acceptance)
- *is completed by* [Give-up Registration](#give-up-registration)
- [Take-up](#take-up) *answers* Give-up Line

### Give-up Registration

*The moment the CCP actually books the given-up quantity into the receiving member's account; this is what makes a give-up complete.*

**Definition.** Step in a Give-up / Take-up Process: the CCP's recording of an accepted quantity of a give-up line in the receiving clearing member's CCP account. Under the CCP's rules it ends or reduces the giving member's CCP-facing trade and creates a linked successor CCP-facing trade for the receiving member. Completion is measured on it, per the CCP record. A reversal of an accepted give-up, or a late give-up, is a further step of the give-up process under the CCP's rules, not a Trade Amendment or a Position Transfer.

**Example.** After Clearing Broker Y's claim, CCP Z moves the 60 lots from Clearing Member M's account to Y's client account.

**Not to be confused with.** Clearing Acceptance: clearing acceptance is the first registration of the trade into the service; give-up registration re-books an already registered (or designated) quantity to another member.

**Step in:** [Give-up / Take-up Process](#give-up--take-up-process)

**Words people use:** “transfer” (CCP reports, contextual); “claim processed” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service; clearing date; CCP record as the authoritative source.

**Connected to:**

- [Give-up Line](#give-up-line) *is completed by* Give-up Registration
- [Give-up after First Registration](#give-up-after-first-registration) *ends with* Give-up Registration
- *records the quantity in* [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account)
- *changes* [Position](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#position)

### Clearing Acceptance

*The CCP accepting a trade into one of its clearing services and registering it in a clearing member's account.*

**Definition.** Acceptance or registration of a trade by a central counterparty into a specified clearing service, in an identified clearing member's CCP account, under the service's acceptance criteria; rejection is an outcome value. It is evidence of, not identical to, CCP Interposition. The later recording of a given-up quantity in the receiving member's account is a Give-up Registration, and a clearing member's take-up or consent is a Clearing Member Acceptance. — *meaning changed in 2026-11, accepted as Draft*

**Example.** CCP Z registers the trade matched on Venue V in the house account of Executing Broker X's clearing member.

**Not to be confused with.** CCP Interposition: registration is the evidence; interposition is the legal moment the CCP becomes counterparty, which the rulebook fixes and may come earlier.

**Words people use:** “registration” (CCP side of clearing)

**Note.** Contextual alias "registration". Rejection is an outcome value, not a separate concept. [02, 08]

**Connected to:**

- *concerns* [Trade](../execution/concepts.md#trade) — Specify service, acceptance criteria and time.
- *accepted by* [Central Counterparty](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#central-counterparty) — Within an identified Clearing Service and for an identified member account; acceptance is evidence of, not identical to, CCP Interposition.
- [CCP Interposition](#ccp-interposition) *evidenced by* Clearing Acceptance — Order and timing are set per clearing service; under open offer interposition can precede the acceptance message.
- [Give-up Designated in Advance](#give-up-designated-in-advance) *coincides with* Clearing Acceptance
- [Submission Acceptance](#submission-acceptance) *precedes* Clearing Acceptance
- [Clearing Submission](#clearing-submission) *leads to* Clearing Acceptance
- [Activity Volume](../fees-revenue-and-contribution/concepts.md#activity-volume) *counts* Clearing Acceptance

### CCP Interposition

*The legal moment when the CCP becomes the buyer to every seller and the seller to every buyer for a trade; this is what 'cleared' really means.*

**Definition.** Event by which a central counterparty becomes counterparty to each side of a trade under a clearing service's rules, at the moment and by the mechanism (novation, open offer or other) that those rules define; the legal meaning of 'cleared', of which acceptance or registration messages are evidence. A later give-up registration, transfer or porting ends the CCP-facing trade of one member and creates a linked successor for another under the service's rules; it is not a new interposition.

**Example.** Under CCP Z's rules the CCP becomes counterparty when the trade is matched on Venue V, before any give-up happens.

**Not to be confused with.** Clearing Acceptance: the CCP's acceptance or registration message is evidence of interposition, not the interposition itself.

**Words people use:** “cleared” (Any clearing service); “open offer” (Clearing)

**Connected to:**

- *concerns* [Trade](../execution/concepts.md#trade) — Interposition creates the CCP-facing trades; under novation the predecessor is extinguished.
- *evidenced by* [Clearing Acceptance](#clearing-acceptance) — Order and timing are set per clearing service; under open offer interposition can precede the acceptance message.
- *effected through* [Novation](#novation) — Only when the mechanism is novation; open offer replaces no prior bilateral contract.
- *within* [Clearing Service](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-service) — Mechanism and legal moment are rulebook facts per clearing service and date.
- [CCP-facing Trade](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-facing-trade) *is created by* CCP Interposition

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Cleared” (open, answer proposed); “Novation and acceptance” (open, answer proposed); “The trade (clearing tier)” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Novation

*Replacing an existing contract or obligation with new ones, as the law allows; in clearing it is one way the CCP steps in, and outside clearing it is also how parties step in or out of a bilateral trade.*

**Definition.** Legal replacement of an obligation or contract by new obligations; applicability and effect depend on the governing framework.

**Example.** A bilateral swap between Client A and Dealer D is novated when CCP Z registers it: the original swap ends and two new swaps with the CCP replace it.

**Not to be confused with.** Open offer: under open offer the CCP is counterparty from the start, so no earlier contract is replaced.

**Note.** Generic legal concept: one possible mechanism of CCP interposition, and also used for bilateral step-in and step-out outside clearing. Never infer novation from "cleared" or "ported". [02, 03, 06]

**Connected to:**

- *transforms* [Trade](../execution/concepts.md#trade) — Preserve predecessor and successor obligations/trades with directional lineage.
- [CCP Interposition](#ccp-interposition) *effected through* Novation — Only when the mechanism is novation; open offer replaces no prior bilateral contract.

**Open questions on its meaning:** “Cleared” (open, answer proposed); “Novation and acceptance” (open, answer proposed); “Give-up, transfer, porting” (open, answer proposed) — see the [review sheet](review.md).

### Giving Clearing Member

*The clearing member that gives up this line.*

**Definition.** Role held on an identified give-up line by the party that holds the Clearing Member role in that clearing service and to which the executed trade is first registered, or would be registered, on the executing side: the clearing member of the trading venue member that entered the order (the executing broker itself, or the Third-party Executing Member it used). It carries the trade at the CCP until take-up and keeps any quantity rejected or lapsed. It is a role on one line, not a membership category: the same firm can be giving on one line and receiving on the next.

**Example.** Executing Broker X enters Client A's order through Member M, a third-party executing member on Venue V; Member M's clearing member is the giving clearing member for the line given up to Clearing Broker Y.

**Not to be confused with.** Executing Broker: the executing broker serves the client; the giving clearing member may be a different firm.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#party-role)

**Words people use:** “executing clearing member” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service; whether the give-up happens at or after registration; whether the executing broker is itself a clearing member.

**Connected to:**

- [Give-up after First Registration](#give-up-after-first-registration) *is carried meanwhile by* Giving Clearing Member
- *clears for* [Executing Broker](../execution/concepts.md#executing-broker)
- *gives up in* [Give-up / Take-up Process](#give-up--take-up-process)
- *holds the trade in until take up* [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account)

### Receiving Clearing Member

*The clearing member named to receive this line.*

**Definition.** Role held on an identified give-up line by the party, holding the Clearing Member role in that clearing service, that is named to receive the quantity, acting for itself or for the clearing broker that serves the client. Being named is not acceptance; acceptance is a separate Clearing Member Acceptance. It is a role on one line, not a membership category.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y is named on Client A's give-up line and takes it up into Client A's omnibus account at CCP Z.

**Not to be confused with.** Clearing Broker: the clearing broker is the client's service provider; it may clear through a different receiving clearing member.

**Kind of:** [Party Role](../parties-and-roles/concepts.md#party-role)

**Words people use:** “claiming member” (listed, contextual); “give-in side” (firm's view, contextual); “carrying broker” (US, contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service; account and give-up reference named; whether the clearing broker is itself a member of the service.

**Connected to:**

- [Give-up Line](#give-up-line) *names* Receiving Clearing Member
- *acts for* [Clearing Broker](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-broker)
- *decides* [Clearing Member Acceptance](#clearing-member-acceptance)
- *receives the trade in* [CCP Account](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#ccp-account)

### Clearing Limit

*The pre-agreed ceiling up to which a clearing broker accepts a client's trades without a manual check.*

**Definition.** Limit set by a clearing member or clearing broker, usually by its risk function, for an identified client, account, executing broker or product scope, with an owner, a basis (for example quantity, notional or risk), a version and an effective period. The same limit can screen orders before execution (in a pre-trade control check) and decide automatic acceptance after execution (at take-up or submission acceptance); beyond it trades are referred or rejected. A give-up agreement or client clearing agreement may refer to it or allow it; it does not usually contain its value. It is not a rule provision, and a change to a limit is not a rule change. New concept without a level-1 parent.

**Example.** Clearing Broker Y lets Executing Broker X give up Client A's trades in one futures contract up to a set number of lots per day.

**Not to be confused with.** Margin Requirement: the amount of margin owed on positions already held; a clearing limit caps what is accepted for clearing.

**Kind of:** [Rule Provision](../regulation-and-rule-changes/concepts.md#rule-provision)

**Words people use:** “clearing member credit limit” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Who set the limit, for which scope and basis, version in force at the time of the trade.

**Connected to:**

- [Give-up Designated in Advance](#give-up-designated-in-advance) *usually relies on* Clearing Limit

### Take-up Deadline

*The CCP's latest time on a clearing date for sending and accepting a give-up, and what happens if it is missed.*

**Definition.** Kind of Cut-off set by a CCP or venue for the steps of a give-up: the latest time to submit a give-up line and the latest time for the receiving member to take it up, stated as a time on the CCP's clearing date or as a period after the line is submitted, and read against the CCP's Processing Window for give-ups, with the consequence of missing it (lapse, automatic rejection, trade stays with the giving member, late route or charge). A firm's earlier internal deadline is a Firm Cut-off.

**Example.** Under CCP Z's rules a give-up not claimed by the evening deadline of the clearing date lapses and stays with the giving member.

**Not to be confused with.** Allocation instruction deadline: that is the time by which the client must give its split; the take-up deadline governs the clearing members.

**Kind of:** [Cut-off](../post-trade-operations/concepts.md#cut-off)

**Words people use:** “give-up deadline” (contextual); “claim deadline” (contextual)

**Note.** Depends on the proposed meaning change to Cut-off, awaiting the sponsor's decision.

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service, rulebook version, clearing date and the CCP's time zone.

**Connected to:**

- [Give-up after First Registration](#give-up-after-first-registration) *must finish before* Take-up Deadline
- *applies to* [Give-up / Take-up Process](#give-up--take-up-process)
- *is set against* [Business Calendar](../instruments-venues-and-calendars/concepts.md#business-calendar)

### Clearing Submission

*The request sent to a CCP, usually for an OTC trade, asking it to clear the trade through named clearing brokers.*

**Definition.** Instruction, sent by an executing party, a trading venue or an affirmation or middleware platform, requesting that an identified OTC trade, or the allocated parts of a block, be cleared in a named clearing service for named clearing members and accounts; it is answered by each clearing member's Submission Acceptance and then by the CCP's Clearing Acceptance. New level-1 concept promoting the backlog item for the OTC phase.

**Example.** A swap between Client A and Dealer D is affirmed on a platform and submitted to CCP Z naming Clearing Broker Y for Client A.

**Not to be confused with.** Give-up / Take-up Process: a listed give-up hands an already registered trade to another member; a clearing submission asks for clearing in the first place.

**Values (draft list):** new trade; after allocation of a block; backload of an existing bilateral trade; resubmission after rejection

**Words people use:** “request for clearing” (OTC, contextual); “submission” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Clearing service; submitting party; designated clearing members; regime acceptance-timing rules. No parent: there is no broader instruction concept in the catalogue that it is a kind of.

**Connected to:**

- *concerns* [Trade](../execution/concepts.md#trade)
- *targets* [Clearing Service](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-service)
- *is answered by* [Submission Acceptance](#submission-acceptance)
- *leads to* [Clearing Acceptance](#clearing-acceptance)
- [Execution Agreement for Cleared Trades](#execution-agreement-for-cleared-trades) *governs* Clearing Submission

### Execution Agreement for Cleared Trades

*The OTC agreement that says what happens to a swap meant for clearing if the clearing broker or CCP refuses it.*

**Definition.** Agreement between a client and an executing dealer or broker governing OTC derivative trades executed with the intention of being cleared: naming of the clearing broker, the consequences if a trade is not accepted for clearing, and submission timing. It is the OTC counterpart of the Give-up Agreement but provides for no give-up.

**Example.** Client A and Dealer D sign it before trading swaps that will be cleared at CCP Z through Clearing Broker Y.

**Not to be confused with.** Give-up Agreement: used for listed futures and options, and it governs a hand-over between clearing members that does not exist in OTC clearing.

**Kind of:** [Agreement](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#agreement)

**Words people use:** “cleared derivatives execution agreement” (contextual)

**Before using it, know:** Regime; product scope; version of any industry standard used.

**Connected to:**

- *governs* [Clearing Submission](#clearing-submission)
- *is the otc counterpart of* [Give-up Agreement](#give-up-agreement)
- *works alongside* [Client Clearing Agreement](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#client-clearing-agreement)
