| **[Give-up Agreement](concepts.md#give-up-agreement)** | Agreement among a client, which may sign and instruct through its investment manager acting as its authorised representative, one or more executing brokers and one or more clearing brokers, that sets how the client's listed-derivative executions are given up to and taken up by the clearing broker: the accounts and products covered, acceptance conditions and any clearing limits it refers to, responsibility for trades not taken up or rejected, executing brokerage terms, billing and payment. It permits give-ups; it does not by itself oblige the clearing broker to accept any particular trade, which remains a Clearing Member Acceptance. | |
| **[Give-up / Take-up Process](concepts.md#give-up--take-up-process)** | Process by which all or part of an executed listed-derivative trade, including a trade registered on a venue after private negotiation and leg executions of a listed strategy, is attributed for clearing from the clearing member to which it was first registered or would be registered (the giving clearing member) to a receiving clearing member, through one give-up line per allocation line routed to another clearing member, and that member's acceptance or rejection. Depending on the clearing service it is designated in advance, so that the CCP registers the trade straight to the receiving member, or happens after first registration to the giving member. Each give-up line ends accepted in full, accepted in part, rejected, lapsed, cancelled or reversed; a give-up is complete when the full quantity is registered to the receiving member's CCP account per the CCP record. Give-in is the same process seen by the receiving side. Instructions, acceptance decisions, registrations and legal effects remain distinct. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[Clearing Member Acceptance](concepts.md#clearing-member-acceptance)** | Decision by a clearing member to accept, wholly or partly, or to reject clearing responsibility for an identified trade or allocated quantity, for an identified client or account in a specified clearing service. It is explicit (the take-up or claim of a listed give-up line, or consent to an OTC clearing submission) or automatic within a clearing limit set before execution. Outcome values: accepted in full, accepted in part, rejected. Distinct from CCP acceptance or registration (Clearing Acceptance) and from CCP Interposition. | |
| **[Clearing Acceptance](concepts.md#clearing-acceptance)** | Acceptance or registration of a trade by a central counterparty into a specified clearing service, in an identified clearing member's CCP account, under the service's acceptance criteria; rejection is an outcome value. It is evidence of, not identical to, CCP Interposition. The later recording of a given-up quantity in the receiving member's account is a Give-up Registration, and a clearing member's take-up or consent is a Clearing Member Acceptance. ⚠️ *meaning changed 2026-11* | |
| **[CCP Interposition](concepts.md#ccp-interposition)** | Event by which a central counterparty becomes counterparty to each side of a trade under a clearing service's rules, at the moment and by the mechanism (novation, open offer or other) that those rules define; the legal meaning of 'cleared', of which acceptance or registration messages are evidence. A later give-up registration, transfer or porting ends the CCP-facing trade of one member and creates a linked successor for another under the service's rules; it is not a new interposition. | |
| **[Novation](concepts.md#novation)** | Legal replacement of an obligation or contract by new obligations; applicability and effect depend on the governing framework. | |
| **[Give-up Line](concepts.md#give-up-line)** *(level 2)* | Part of a Give-up / Take-up Process: the request, made by or for the giving clearing member through the CCP or a give-up facility, to attribute a stated quantity of an identified trade, at a stated price, to a named receiving clearing member and account (usually through a give-up reference). One give-up line arises from one allocation line routed to another clearing member, or from a designation made before execution. It is the unit at which give-up status, member acceptance and registration are recorded. Its status takes the harmonised values below, is asserted by a named source record with the source's native status kept beside it, and is never set by inference; the line is complete only when its full quantity is recorded in the receiving member's CCP account, as the CCP record shows. | |

| **[Give-up after First Registration](concepts.md#give-up-after-first-registration)** *(level 2)* | Kind of Give-up / Take-up Process in which the CCP first registers the trade to the executing side's clearing member, which carries it, with its margin, fees and default exposure, until the receiving member takes it up and the CCP records the accepted quantity in the receiving member's account. Where it applies depends on each clearing service's rules (to verify per CCP). | |
| **[Giving Clearing Member](concepts.md#giving-clearing-member)** *(level 2)* | Role held on an identified give-up line by the party that holds the Clearing Member role in that clearing service and to which the executed trade is first registered, or would be registered, on the executing side: the clearing member of the trading venue member that entered the order (the executing broker itself, or the Third-party Executing Member it used). It carries the trade at the CCP until take-up and keeps any quantity rejected or lapsed. It is a role on one line, not a membership category: the same firm can be giving on one line and receiving on the next. | |

| **[Take-up](concepts.md#take-up)** *(level 2)* | Kind of Clearing Member Acceptance in which the receiving clearing member explicitly accepts (claims) all or part of the quantity of an identified give-up line for an identified account, in the CCP's system or a give-up facility, before the take-up deadline. | **[Submission Acceptance](concepts.md#submission-acceptance)** *(level 2)* | Kind of Clearing Member Acceptance in which a clearing member, or the clearing broker acting through it, accepts or refuses to clear an identified OTC trade submitted for clearing, for an identified client or account in a specified clearing service, usually after checking it against a clearing limit, manually or automatically. | |

A give-up agreement or client clearing agreement may refer to it or allow it; it does not usually contain its value. It is not a rule provision, and a change to a limit is not a rule change. New concept without a level-1 parent. | |

Under the CCP's rules it ends or reduces the giving member's CCP-facing trade and creates a linked successor CCP-facing trade for the receiving member. Completion is measured on it, per the CCP record. A reversal of an accepted give-up, or a late give-up, is a further step of the give-up process under the CCP's rules, not a Trade Amendment or a Position Transfer. | |

| **[Clearing Submission](concepts.md#clearing-submission)** | Instruction, sent by an executing party, a trading venue or an affirmation or middleware platform, requesting that an identified OTC trade, or the allocated parts of a block, be cleared in a named clearing service for named clearing members and accounts; it is answered by each clearing member's Submission Acceptance and then by the CCP's Clearing Acceptance. | **[Execution Agreement for Cleared Trades](concepts.md#execution-agreement-for-cleared-trades)** *(level 2)* | Agreement between a client and an executing dealer or broker governing OTC derivative trades executed with the intention of being cleared: naming of the clearing broker, the consequences if a trade is not accepted for clearing, and submission timing. ## 3. Open questions on meaning

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### Cleared (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate acceptance status from legal effect and record effective time.

**Proposed answer:** "Cleared" is a derived status meaning that CCP Interposition has occurred in a named clearing service. Three timestamps stay distinct: Clearing Member Acceptance, CCP acceptance or registration (Clearing Acceptance), interposition. Under open offer interposition can occur at matching, before any registration message; under novation it occurs at registration. A completed give-up changes which member holds the trade, not whether it is cleared. Confidence high on the principle, medium on each CCP's moment. Legal decision: the interposition moment per clearing service, from the rulebook. [02, 03, 06, 08]

<sub>reference LD-A0009</sub>

### Successful give-up (Decided 2026-11-01 (sponsor; see decision log))

**To decide:** Define completion, partial acceptance, rejection, correction and current versus historical status.

**Proposed answer:** States per give-up line: pending, accepted (in full or in part), rejected, lapsed (not taken up by the cut-off, so the trade stays with the giving member), cancelled, reversed where permitted, and unknown (no record), which is not "pending". Proposed definition of complete: the full allocated quantity is registered to the receiving clearing member's CCP account, as evidenced by the CCP record; partial take-ups are reported separately and both member acceptance time and registration time are kept. Where the take-up is made in the CCP's system the two coincide. Current versus historical follows the open question “Current, as of, as known at”. Confidence high on the pattern, medium on state names. Firm decision: definition of complete and source of record. **Decided 2026-11-01: the proposed definition is accepted and the CCP record is authoritative; discrepancies with internal records are reported as breaks** (see the [decision log](decision-log.md)). [01, 02, 07]

<sub>reference LD-A0010</sub>

### Novation and acceptance (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Do not assume all clearing uses the same legal mechanism or timestamp.

**Proposed answer:** Four distinct steps: clearing member acceptance, CCP acceptance or registration, CCP interposition with its mechanism (novation, open offer, other), and settlement finality (outside current scope). Under the EU principal-to-principal model the client's contract is with the clearing member and arises from the client clearing agreement, not from the CCP's novation; in US models the FCM guarantees performance, and who is principal to the CCP contract varies by product and rulebook. OTC "novation" also names bilateral step-in and step-out, which is not clearing. Confidence high. [02, 03, 06]

<sub>reference LD-A0017</sub>

### The trade (clearing tier) (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Pre-clearing (executed) trade versus CCP-facing trade versus member-client trade; which tier, identifier and counterparty view a question uses.

**Proposed answer:** State the tier: pre-clearing trade, CCP-facing trade, member–client trade (principal model). Successor trades are linked by a “succeeds” link and may carry new identifiers. Confidence high. [03]

<sub>reference LD-A0025</sub>

### Give-up, transfer, porting (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Listed give-up versus voluntary position transfer versus default porting versus OTC or FX prime-brokerage "give-up"; trigger, consent, rule, fees and whether collateral moves.

**Proposed answer:** Give-up (Give-up / Take-up Process) attributes a listed trade to a receiving clearing member; a position transfer moves open positions between CCP accounts under transfer rules, usually voluntarily; porting is the default-triggered transfer of a defaulting member's client positions and collateral to a back-up member, or their close-out (EMIR Art. 48(5) to (7), to verify). Positions and collateral port as separate legs. OTC or FX "give-up" usually means prime-brokerage give-up. Position Transfer and Porting are backlog concepts. Confidence high. [02, 03, 04, 06]

<sub>reference LD-A0026</sub>

### Break, exception, reject, fail (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Reconciliation break versus operational exception versus rejected or lapsed give-up versus settlement fail versus technology incident.

**Proposed answer:** A reconciliation break says records disagree, not which is wrong; an Operational Exception is a situation requiring handling and may be raised by a break; a rejected or lapsed give-up is an event and may raise an exception but is not a trade failure; a settlement fail concerns Settlement Obligation; a technology incident is out of scope. Confidence high. [07, 02]
