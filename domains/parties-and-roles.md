### Usage notes and required context

- **LD-C0002 Legal Entity**
  - Note: Identified by LEI (ISO 17442); certain international branches can also hold an LEI (to verify). An LEI never identifies an account, desk or commercial group, and public parent data describe accounting consolidation, not a commercial group. Holder of entity-level regulatory classifications. [06, 07]
- **LD-C0003 Party Role**
  - Note: Read as the role assignment (party-in-role); there is no separate role-assignment concept. Regulatory relationship statuses attach here. Alignment to verify: FIBO party-in-role pattern, CDM party role. [08, 06]
- **LD-C0004 Client**
  - Note: Sponsor definition unchanged. Linked to a Service Offering through LD-R0081. Client segment is a Participants taxonomy axis. [05, 06]
- **LD-C0010 Account**
  - Note: Has identifiers per maintaining party, a lifecycle status and an effective period. A give-up reference is an identifier pointing to the receiving member's account, not an account. Regulatory qualifications (for example active-account status) are time-bounded classifications. [07, 06]

## 3. Ambiguities and proposed answers

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### LD-A0006 — Client (Open, narrowed 2026-09-20, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Separate contractual role, legal entity, commercial group and beneficial owner.

**Proposed answer:** Entity-level statuses attach to the Legal Entity, relationship-level statuses (such as MiFID client category) to the role assignment (LD-C0003); revenue rolls up from the contractual client of each service relationship to a Commercial Group (LD-C0073). Regulatory texts use "client" differently: EMIR Art. 2(15) is a clearing relationship with a clearing member, the MiFID II client (Art. 4(1)(9)) is broader, and the US "customer" is the FCM relationship. Confidence high. [06, 05]

### LD-A0007 — Account (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify function, maintaining party, holder, beneficiary and account-to-account relationships.

**Proposed answer:** Three levels: the CCP Account (LD-C0069) of each clearing service; the member's internal client account; execution-side and give-up references, which are identifiers rather than accounts. Levels are related only by explicit, time-bounded LD-R0065. Segregation regime and regulatory account statuses are separate, time-bounded dimensions. Confidence high. [02, 07, 06]

### LD-A0013 — Position (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Specify gross/net, quantity/notional/exposure, account, instrument, CCP and as-of basis.

**Proposed answer:** Key: account (CCP or internal) × instrument × clearing service × side or net basis, as of a business date and cycle, with the asserting source (CCP statement or internal). House accounts are generally net; client omnibus accounts are net or gross by account type. CCP-reported and internal positions are two records of one position; a difference is a break. OTC positions are sets of open cleared trades measured by count, notional or risk sensitivity. Confidence high. Firm decision: default basis and source, or return both labelled. [02, 03, 07]

### LD-A0014 — Revenue by client (Open, narrowed 2026-09-20 and 2026-11-01, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Use the approved management perspective; specify client attribution, consolidation, internal eliminations, measurement period, reporting currency and any separately defined cost deductions.

**Proposed answer:** Sponsor decision 2026-11-01: execution-only, clearing-only and execute-and-clear are all offered; give-up and give-in are both supported; revenue is attributed per service. Proposed: attribute to the contractual client of each service relationship (LD-R0077), show the service mix, roll up to Commercial Group, flag intra-group clients for elimination; mandatory context is observing entity, management basis, period and currency. Open: cost perimeter for Contribution (LD-C0074), pass-through and collateral income treatment. [05, 01, 04]

### LD-A0019 — Clearing broker, clearing member, FCM (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Service role toward the client versus admission at a CCP versus a regulatory registration (FCM in the US).

**Proposed answer:** Clearing Broker (LD-C0057) is the service role toward the client and may exist without membership of the clearing service; Clearing Member (LD-C0007) is admission at the CCP; FCM is a US regulatory registration held by a legal entity, recorded as a Regulatory Classification (LD-C0068), and only a contextual alias. Confidence high. [02, 03, 05, 06, 08]

### LD-A0028 — Omnibus, house and client (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** EMIR omnibus (net or gross) versus US customer omnibus versus indirect omnibus versus internal pooled account; CCP account type versus economic relationship of affiliates.

**Proposed answer:** EMIR omnibus and individual client segregation (Art. 39(2) and (3), to verify), US futures-customer omnibus, indirect-client omnibus account types (Delegated Regulations (EU) 2017/2154 and 2017/2155, to verify) and internal pooled accounts are different things; the CCP account type does not determine the economic relationship (affiliates may be cleared as clients or in the house account). Confidence high. [02, 04, 03]

### LD-A0031 — Group (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Regulatory consolidation group versus commercial group versus CCP affiliate versus legal parent in public entity-relationship data.

**Proposed answer:** A regulatory consolidation group (used in some threshold calculations), a Commercial Group (LD-C0073), a CCP's "affiliate" and a legal parent in public entity-relationship data are different; regulatory statuses never attach to a commercial group. Confidence high. [06, 05, 07]
