**Why it matters:** the onboarding agent (credit approval and limits before a client is cleared, CCP review before a new membership), the give-up and clearing acceptance agent (limit checks at take-up, risk kept on trades not taken up) and the change-impact agent (CCP rule changes to the default waterfall, assessments and loss allocation).

## How it flows

Three areas, read separately. Risk we take on the clients we clear: [Client Credit Assessment](concepts.md#client-credit-assessment) → [Client Risk Rating](concepts.md#client-risk-rating) → [Risk Limit](concepts.md#risk-limit), such as the [Clearing Limit](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#clearing-limit) → [Limit Utilisation](concepts.md#limit-utilisation) of the [Client Clearing Exposure](concepts.md#client-clearing-exposure) → [Limit Breach](concepts.md#limit-breach) → [Client Default](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#client-default) → [Client Default Management](concepts.md#client-default-management). Execution risk: [Pre-trade Limit](concepts.md#pre-trade-limit) → [Pre-trade Control Check](../execution/concepts.md#pre-trade-control-check) → [Kill Switch Activation](concepts.md#kill-switch-activation); [Execution Error](concepts.md#execution-error) → [Error Position](concepts.md#error-position); [Unaccepted Give-up Exposure](concepts.md#unaccepted-give-up-exposure) until member take-up ([Clearing Member Acceptance](../clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#clearing-member-acceptance)). CCP risk: [CCP Risk Assessment](concepts.md#ccp-risk-assessment) → [CCP Exposure](concepts.md#ccp-exposure) → [Default Waterfall](concepts.md#default-waterfall) with [CCP Dedicated Own Resources](concepts.md#ccp-dedicated-own-resources) and [Default Fund Contribution](../margin-and-collateral/concepts.md#default-fund-contribution) → [Clearing Member Default](../clearing-services-and-accounts/concepts.md#clearing-member-default) → [Default Management Auction](concepts.md#default-management-auction) → [Default Fund Assessment](concepts.md#default-fund-assessment) → [CCP Loss Allocation](concepts.md#ccp-loss-allocation).

## Concepts at a glance

- **[Risk Exposure](concepts.md#risk-exposure)** — What we could lose toward someone if they fail, measured at a point in time.
  - **[Client Clearing Exposure](concepts.md#client-clearing-exposure)** — What we could lose on a client we clear if it stopped paying.
  - **[Unaccepted Give-up Exposure](concepts.md#unaccepted-give-up-exposure)** — The risk the executing side keeps on trades the clearing broker has not taken up yet.
  - **[CCP Exposure](concepts.md#ccp-exposure)** — What we could lose toward a CCP if it failed or used our resources after another member's default.
  - **[Stress Loss](concepts.md#stress-loss)** — What a portfolio would lose in a named extreme scenario.
- **[Risk Limit](concepts.md#risk-limit)** — A cap our risk function sets on how much risk or activity a client, trader or CCP may represent.
  - **[Pre-trade Limit](concepts.md#pre-trade-limit)** — A limit checked on each order before it reaches the market.
- **[Limit Utilisation](concepts.md#limit-utilisation)** — How much of a limit is used right now.
- **[Limit Breach](concepts.md#limit-breach)** — The moment a limit is exceeded, and what was done about it.
- **[Client Credit Assessment](concepts.md#client-credit-assessment)** — The risk team's review that decides whether, and on what terms, we clear for a client.
- **[Client Risk Rating](concepts.md#client-risk-rating)** — Our internal grade for how risky a client is.
- **[Credit Support Arrangement](concepts.md#credit-support-arrangement)** — A third party's promise to pay if our client does not.
- **[Client Default Management](concepts.md#client-default-management)** — What we do after declaring a client in default: close out, hedge or transfer, and use its collateral.
- **[Kill Switch Activation](concepts.md#kill-switch-activation)** — Pulling all of a participant's orders at once and blocking new ones.
- **[Execution Error](concepts.md#execution-error)** — A trade done or booked wrongly by the executing side.
- **[Error Position](concepts.md#error-position)** — The position the broker keeps at its own risk after an execution error.
- **[CCP Risk Assessment](concepts.md#ccp-risk-assessment)** — Our review of a CCP before and while we are a member.
- **[Default Waterfall](concepts.md#default-waterfall)** — The order in which a CCP uses its resources to absorb a defaulting member's losses.
  - **[Default Fund Assessment](concepts.md#default-fund-assessment)** — A further payment a CCP can demand from surviving members after the default fund is used.
  - **[CCP Dedicated Own Resources](concepts.md#ccp-dedicated-own-resources)** — The CCP's own money placed in its default waterfall.
- **[Default Management Auction](concepts.md#default-management-auction)** — The auction in which a CCP sells a defaulted member's positions to other members.
- **[CCP Loss Allocation](concepts.md#ccp-loss-allocation)** — How a CCP passes on losses once its prefunded resources are used.
