## Usage notes and required context

- **LD-C0009 Trading Venue**
  - Note: Identify by ISO 10383 MIC; operating MIC for the venue, segment MIC for a segment (to verify per use). Venue type (regulated market, MTF, OTF; US DCM, SEF) is a Regulatory Classification. [01, 05, 06]
- **LD-C0013 Product Family**
  - Note: Label changed from "Product" on 2026-11-01; meaning unchanged. OTC classification can align with the ISDA OTC taxonomy, CFI (ISO 10962) and UPI (ISO 4914), to verify. [03, 05, 07]
- **LD-C0014 Instrument**
  - Note: An OTC derivative has an Instrument only where a standard instrument identifier is assigned; trades with the same identifier can still differ in rate and notional, so the Trade carries the full terms. Listed strategies are instruments with legs. [01, 03, 07]
- **LD-C0015 Business Date**
  - An agent must know: Date role, business calendar, time zone

## Ambiguities and proposed answers

A proposed answer comes from the expert review; it is **not a decision** until the owner confirms it.

### LD-A0015 — Business date (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Distinguish trade, clearing, value and accounting dates and calendars/time zones.

**Proposed answer:** Every date has a role (trade date, CCP clearing date, value date, booking date, reporting date, contract key dates such as last trading day or expiry, as-of date), resolved against a named Business Calendar (LD-C0066) and time zone; event timestamps are kept in UTC. Evening or overnight sessions can carry the next day's trade date (venue-specific). Extended trading hours move cut-offs, margin runs and give-up deadlines relative to the clearing date. Confidence high. Firm decision: default role and time zone of "today". [01, 02, 07]

### LD-A0016 — Product, instrument and OTC contract (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Validate identity boundaries for listed series and bespoke OTC agreements.

**Proposed answer:** Proposed (implemented in the Draft definitions of LD-C0013, LD-C0014 and LD-C0062): Product Family is a classification (CFI ISO 10962 and the UPI ISO 4914 are classification and product identifiers); Contract Specification is a venue's versioned listing template; Instrument is a standardised, counterparty-independent specification at the level at which it is listed or assigned an instrument identifier (a listed series; an OTC ISIN where assigned, with the Trade still carrying full terms); a bespoke OTC contract is a Trade, not an Instrument. Confidence high. Reference Data and OTC Product to confirm the meaning change of LD-C0014. [01, 03, 07, 08]

### LD-A0021 — Product (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Product family classification versus a venue's contract specification versus a listed series versus a service offering ("our clearing product").

**Proposed answer:** "Product" returns candidates LD-C0013, LD-C0062, LD-C0014 and LD-C0052 unless context selects one; a venue's "product code" usually designates a Contract Specification. Confidence high. [01, 05, 07]

### LD-A0022 — Dates of a rule change (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Publication, entry into force, application, phase-in and end of transition versus the date the firm recorded the change.

**Proposed answer:** Each Rule Change (LD-C0064) carries role-qualified dates: publication, entry into force, application, phase-in and end of transition, plus the date the firm recorded it. "Effective date" without a role is ambiguous. Confidence high. [02, 05, 06, 08]

### LD-A0023 — Current, as of, as known at (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Valid time versus record time: valid now and latest non-superseded record, valid at a business date, or known by a timestamp.

**Proposed answer:** Current = valid now and latest non-superseded record from the stated source; as of D = valid at business date D; as known at T = only records received by T. Every answer states source, as-of date and freshness. See the time convention in [modelling guidelines](../docs/modelling-guidelines.md). Confidence high. Firm decision: whether "as known at" history is needed. [07, 08, 06]

### LD-A0029 — Eligible, mandatory, offered, covered (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** CCP clearing eligibility versus a regulatory clearing obligation for a counterparty pair versus the firm's offering versus "we cover market X" (membership, third-party access or actual offering).

**Proposed answer:** Eligibility is a CCP fact (LD-R0050); a clearing obligation applies to a counterparty pair, a product class including trade-term ranges, and a date; offering and coverage are firm facts in the internal overlay, through a Market Access Arrangement. Never infer one from another. Confidence high. [03, 05, 06]

### LD-A0032 — OTC and listed (Open, answer proposed)

**To decide:** Regulatory "OTC" (derivatives not executed on a regulated market or equivalent) versus off-venue versus not exchange-listed; cleared is a separate dimension.

**Proposed answer:** Under EMIR Art. 2(7) a derivative not executed on a regulated market or an equivalent third-country market is OTC, so MTF and OTF trades count as OTC; venue type is a Regulatory Classification of LD-C0009. Scope modules by listing and clearing structure, not by that label. Confidence high. [06, 02, 03]
