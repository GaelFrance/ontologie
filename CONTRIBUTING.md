# Contributing

The Phase 1 foundation was approved on 2026-09-20. Phase 2 concept development is authorised in the agreed sequence; individual definitions remain Draft until reviewed. Respect the later phase boundaries.

1. Identify the concept, relationship or ambiguity ID affected.
2. Explain the business problem and relevant domain/context.
3. Check for existing shared concepts before adding a concept.
4. Preserve IDs and document changes in the change log.
5. Record sources and unresolved questions; do not invent approval.
6. Obtain review from the relevant business owner and semantic steward.

All canonical artefacts must be in English. Markdown is currently authoritative. Use synthetic examples and no credentials or client data. Do not invent tables, columns, endpoints, tools or application names.

Before submitting: check local links, uniqueness of IDs, valid relationship references, terminology consistency and preservation of the phase boundary. Detailed concept fiches should follow the template after Phase 1 approval.
