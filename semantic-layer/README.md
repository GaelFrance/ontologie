# Semantic layer

Status: Draft — architecture only

A semantic contract makes a business measure or query interpretable independently of its source. No executable metrics or source mappings are provided in Phase 1.

For each future metric, specify: ID, concept references, population, grain, dimensions, observing entity, recognition basis, time basis, currency/FX policy, sign convention, aggregation, exclusions, reversal handling, internal elimination, double-counting controls, freshness and permissions.

Execution Revenue is not automatically the sum of Client Execution Fee amounts. Recognition, accruals, reversals, pass-through treatment and gross/net policies require Finance validation. No formula is approved at this stage.

Resolution must return candidate concepts and missing context when ambiguous. It must not pick a source or invent a metric from the label alone. A missing required business distinction should lead to clarification or a recorded, approved default.
