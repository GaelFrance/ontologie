# Semantic layer

Status: Draft — management perspective selected by the sponsor on 2026-09-20

A semantic contract makes a business measure or query interpretable independently of its source. No executable metrics or source mappings are provided in Phase 1.

For each future metric, specify: ID, concept references, population, grain, dimensions, observing entity, management measurement basis, time basis, currency/FX policy, sign convention, aggregation, exclusions, reversal handling, internal elimination, double-counting controls, freshness and permissions.

## Management revenue and profitability

Prioritise business steering: what an activity earns and what a client brings to the business. Statutory/accounting revenue is not the current design priority; do not silently substitute it for a management measure.

Keep distinct:

- Revenue attributable to execution or clearing services under a defined management measurement basis.
- Contribution after explicitly identified costs.
- Profitability after any further agreed cost allocation.

The latter two are requirements to clarify, not newly approved concept definitions or formulas. The sponsor's phrase about what the business earns does not establish which costs to deduct.

Execution Revenue is not automatically the sum of Client Execution Fee amounts. Timing, estimates, adjustments, rebates, pass-through treatment, gross/net presentation, broker/exchange/CCP costs and shared-cost allocation require explicit business decisions with relevant Finance input. No formula or default deduction policy is approved at this stage.

Resolution must return candidate concepts and missing context when ambiguous. It must not pick a source or invent a metric from the label alone. A missing required business distinction should lead to clarification or a recorded, approved default.
