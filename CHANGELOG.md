## v0.2.0 — 2026-11-01

First tagged release: Phase 2 foundation, 222 concepts, all Draft.

- Expert review by eight specialists, then a level-2 review by ten domain experts with a supervisor (AI work is evidence, not approval). Concepts LD-C0051 to LD-C0222 added; 57 foundation definitions clarified; meaning changes applied as Draft after sponsor decisions (Instrument narrowed to listed or identified specifications; Give-up / Take-up redefined; Collateral became a State; 13 level-2 meaning changes including Party, Broker, Position, Commission and Cut-off).
- Sponsor decisions 1 to 22 recorded in the decision log (service mix, handling channel, give-up completion, reference CCPs, regime priority, default date, governance and format).
- Ten review domains, each with an owner, reviewers and one primary domain per concept; readable domain folders and glossary generated by `tools/build_domain_views.py`; French labels removed.
