- **[Allocation Line](domains/execution/concepts.md#allocation-line)** — One row of a split: so many lots, at this price, to this account. *(Listed derivatives execution)*

- **[Average Price Residual](domains/execution/concepts.md#average-price-residual)** — The small leftover amount created when an average price is rounded. *(Listed derivatives execution)*

## G

- **[Give-up / Take-up Process](domains/clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#give-up--take-up-process)** — The hand-over of an executed listed trade, in whole or in part, from the clearing member that holds it to the clearing member that will clear it for the client; give-in is the same hand-over seen by the receiver. *(Clearing: give-up and acceptance)*
- **[Give-up after First Registration](domains/clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#give-up-after-first-registration)** — A give-up where the trade first lands with the executing side's clearing member and is handed over afterwards. *(Clearing: give-up and acceptance)*
- **[Give-up Agreement](domains/clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#give-up-agreement)** — The signed agreement that lets a client trade with one broker and clear with another, and says who pays and who is responsible if the clearing broker does not take the trade. *(Clearing: give-up and acceptance)*
- **[Give-up Designated in Advance](domains/clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#give-up-designated-in-advance)** — A give-up where the clearing broker is named up front and the CCP books the trade straight to it. *(Clearing: give-up and acceptance)*
- **[Give-up Line](domains/clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#give-up-line)** — One request to hand a stated quantity of a trade to one other clearing member's account; give-up progress is tracked line by line. *(Clearing: give-up and acceptance)*
- **[Give-up Registration](domains/clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#give-up-registration)** — The moment the CCP actually books the given-up quantity into the receiving member's account; this is what makes a give-up complete. *(Clearing: give-up and acceptance)*
- **[Giving Clearing Member](domains/clearing-give-up-and-acceptance/concepts.md#giving-clearing-member)** — The clearing member that gives up this line. *(Clearing: give-up and acceptance)*

## K

## L
