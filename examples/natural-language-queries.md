# Competency questions

Status: Captured requirements — resolution demonstrations deferred to Phase 4

The ontology must eventually support these questions or identify the missing business context:

1. Show me execution revenue by client.
2. How much brokerage did we pay executing brokers?
3. What clearing fees did we charge this client?
4. What were our exchange fees last month?
5. Which clients generate the most revenue?
6. Which trades have not been successfully given up?
7. What margin is required for this client?
8. What are the client's cleared positions at this CCP?

Future checks must distinguish revenue from fees and cash, fees paid from pass-through charges, contractual clients from commercial groups, complete from partial give-ups, CCP/member from member/client margin, and gross from net positions. Record missing date, currency, scope and viewpoint rather than inventing defaults.
