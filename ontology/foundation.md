# Foundational concept catalogue

Status: Draft for all entries (LD-C0001 to LD-C0230). Type assignments and definitions are proposals.

This catalogue is the single index of concept IDs: one row per ID, including later additions and any future deprecations. It does not claim to complete the [full concept template](../templates/concept-template.md). IDs are stable across future renaming, relocation and format migration. Common means shared stewardship, not exclusive domain use.

Definitions changed by the 2026-11 expert review are listed with their rationale in the [change log](../CHANGELOG.md) and the [arbitration record](../governance/reviews/2026-11-expert-review/ARBITRATION.md). Aliases, specialisations, usage notes, French labels and standard alignments are kept in [concept annotations](concept-annotations.md) so that this table keeps its five-column layout.

## Phase 1 foundation (LD-C0001 to LD-C0050)

| ID | Canonical English name | Owning module | Proposed type | Working business definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LD-C0001 | Party | Common | Entity | Person or organisation that can be identified and can act, or hold rights and obligations, in a business relationship: a natural person, a legal entity, or a unit without its own legal personality that is named separately in agreements or records (for example a branch or an investment fund). The firm's internal teams, desks and systems are not parties. |
| LD-C0002 | Legal Entity | Common | Entity | Organisation, fund or other arrangement recognised under an applicable legal or regulatory framework as a distinct legal person, able to hold rights and obligations or to be identified in its own name (for example a company, a partnership, a public body or an investment fund); usually identified by a Legal Entity Identifier. A branch is part of its legal entity, not a separate one, even when it holds its own identifier. |
| LD-C0003 | Party Role | Common | Role | The holding of one function, such as client, executing broker or clearing member, by one party toward identified other parties, within a service and, where one exists, under an agreement, for a stated period. Client, Broker, Clearing Member and the other role names are each a kind of Party Role. |
| LD-C0004 | Client | Common | Role | Role held by a party that receives a service from an identified provider under an agreement with that provider (the contractual client). The party that instructs on its behalf, the party that bears the economic result and the Commercial Group it belongs to are recorded separately and are not the client by default. |
| LD-C0005 | Broker | Common | Role | Role of a party that provides execution of orders, or clearing or carrying of trades and positions, as a service to clients under an identified agreement, whether it acts as agent or as principal toward them. Executing Broker and Clearing Broker are its kinds. |
| LD-C0006 | Executing Broker | Common | Role | Role of a party that provides an execution service to a client for identified orders and is responsible to that client for how they are executed, whether it enters the orders on the venue itself as a trading venue member or through another member. It is not necessarily the venue member, the executing entity named in a regulatory transaction report, or the clearing member to which the trade is first registered. |
| LD-C0007 | Clearing Member | Common | Role | Role of a party admitted to a Clearing Service under a membership category defined in that service's rules, which entitles it to have trades registered in its own name in CCP accounts and makes it directly answerable to the central counterparty for them, within the clearing scope its category allows (own business, affiliates, clients, non-clearing members). Held per clearing service and for an effective period. A participant admitted under a sponsored or direct-client model holds the Sponsored Clearing Participant role; it also holds Clearing Member only where that CCP's rules call it a member (to verify per rulebook). |
| LD-C0008 | Central Counterparty | Common | Role | Role of a legal entity that interposes itself between the counterparties to trades accepted in one or more of its clearing services, becoming the buyer to every seller and the seller to every buyer, by the legal mechanism (novation, open offer or other) and at the moment its rules define. Its authorisation, recognition or registration in each regime is a separate Regulatory Classification. |
| LD-C0009 | Trading Venue | Common | Entity | Multilateral trading system or facility, operated under its own rules by an identified market operator, in which multiple third-party buying and selling interests meet, or privately negotiated trades are registered under its rules, so that contracts result; identified by a market identifier code and possibly divided into segments. Includes regulated markets, MTFs, OTFs, designated contract markets and swap execution facilities. Distinct from its operator, from a central counterparty, from a systematic internaliser or single-dealer platform, and from trade reporting services. |
| LD-C0010 | Account | Common | Entity | Record-keeping unit, opened and maintained by an identified party for an identified holder and a stated function, in which trades, positions, cash, collateral or charges are recorded and attributed; it carries identifiers issued by the maintaining party, a status and an effective period. The firm's record of an account maintained by another party (for example a settlement account at a settlement bank) refers to that real account; an identifier alone, such as a give-up reference, is not an account. |
| LD-C0011 | Segregation Model | Common | Reference Concept | Set of rules, set by a regulation, a CCP's rules or an agreement, for keeping the positions and assets of one party or group of parties apart from those of others (for example clients from the clearing member, or one client from all other clients), at a stated level (CCP books or clearing member books) and under a stated regime. It describes how records and assets are separated; the legal protection that results is a separate conclusion of Legal. |
| LD-C0012 | Agreement | Common | Agreement | Arrangement creating rights and obligations between identified parties, for example for a service, documented by one or more signed documents or by adherence to published rules (such as a CCP rulebook or a venue's rules), with an effective period. |
| LD-C0013 | Product Family | Common | Reference Concept | Classification grouping of contract specifications, instruments or trades that share economic and contractual characteristics (asset class, underlying type, contract form such as future, option, forward or swap, settlement form), independent of any venue and of any counterparty; it may be organised in levels from asset class down to the finest product level at which an OTC product identifier is assigned. Not a venue's contract specification and not a service offering; a classification code such as a CFI code is a value of the classification, not the family's identity. |
| LD-C0014 | Instrument | Common | Entity | Standardised derivative specification, identifiable independently of any counterparty pair and any single trade, at the level at which it is listed or assigned an instrument identifier: for listed derivatives the series (contract, expiry and, for options, strike and call or put), including a strategy listed by the venue, whose legs are other instruments, and a flexible series created on request; for OTC derivatives only where a standard instrument identifier is assigned, including OTC derivatives traded on a venue that carry one. A bespoke OTC contract is not an instrument and its terms belong to the Trade. The asset, index or rate a derivative refers to is its underlying; it is an instrument only when it is itself a listed derivative. |
| LD-C0015 | Business Date | Common | Reference Concept | Calendar day (not a timestamp) assigned to a business object or event under a stated date role, such as trade date, CCP clearing date, value date or last trading day, resolved against a named business calendar and time zone; the event's UTC timestamp is kept beside it and need not fall on the same calendar day. |
| LD-C0016 | Order | Execution | Instruction | Instruction from one party to another, or to a trading venue, to buy or sell a specified quantity of an instrument under stated execution conditions (such as order type, price limit, time in force and handling instructions). An order given by a client to its broker and each order the broker derives from it to work it are separate orders, linked parent to child. |
| LD-C0017 | Execution | Execution | Event | Event by which all or part of an order is filled at an agreed price and time, either by matching on a trading venue or by the venue's registration of a privately negotiated trade under its rules (such as a block trade or an exchange for related position). An execution is recorded per order concerned: a match on a venue yields an execution for each participating order, and a broker's venue-facing and client-facing executions are separate events. Corrections and cancellations are later events that refer to it. |
| LD-C0018 | Trade | Common | Agreement | Agreement between one pair of counterparties on a derivative contract with stated economic terms (instrument or bespoke terms, direction, quantity, price and trade date), resulting from one or more executions or from a later deriving event such as CCP interposition, allocation or transfer. A trade is distinct from the records that represent it; clearing and porting create successor trades linked to it rather than changing it, and compression ends or reduces trades and creates zero or more successor trades. Which clearing tier is meant (pre-clearing, CCP-facing or member-client) must be stated. |
| LD-C0019 | Transaction | Common | Event | Business operation affecting rights, obligations or balances; this broad working definition requires validation and must not become a catch-all synonym. |
| LD-C0020 | Allocation | Common | Process | Process by which an executed or average-priced quantity is assigned to one or more target accounts, in allocation lines, following an allocation instruction or a standing rule. An allocation to an account carried by a clearing member other than the executing side's clearing member (whether the trade is registered first to that member or designated to the receiving member in advance) starts a give-up; otherwise it is internal. Allocation distributes quantity; averaging, which may come before it, sets the price. |
| LD-C0021 | Average Price Group | Common | Entity | Set of executions, normally in one instrument and on one side for one client or order, that are combined so that the resulting trades or client-facing executions carry a single average price, under an averaging method applied either by a venue or CCP average-price facility or by the broker. Which executions are eligible, how the average is rounded and how any residual is handled follow the facility's rules or the client agreement. |
| LD-C0022 | Give-up / Take-up Process | Common | Process | Process by which all or part of an executed listed-derivative trade, including a trade registered on a venue after private negotiation and leg executions of a listed strategy, is attributed for clearing from the clearing member to which it was first registered or would be registered (the giving clearing member) to a receiving clearing member, through one give-up line per allocation line routed to another clearing member, and that member's acceptance or rejection. Depending on the clearing service it is designated in advance, so that the CCP registers the trade straight to the receiving member, or happens after first registration to the giving member. Each give-up line records the receiving member's decision (accepted in full, accepted in part, rejected, or lapsed when no decision is made by the deadline) apart from its registration in the CCP record, and can be cancelled or reversed; a give-up is complete when the full quantity is registered to the receiving member's CCP account per the CCP record, and a decision to accept does not by itself complete it. Give-in is the same process seen by the receiving side. Instructions, acceptance decisions, registrations and legal effects remain distinct. |
| LD-C0023 | Clearing Acceptance | Common | Event | Acceptance or registration of a trade by a central counterparty into a specified clearing service, in an identified clearing member's CCP account, under the service's acceptance criteria; rejection is an outcome value. It is evidence of, not identical to, CCP Interposition. The later recording of a given-up quantity in the receiving member's account is a Give-up Registration, and a clearing member's take-up or consent is a Clearing Member Acceptance. |
| LD-C0024 | Novation | Common | Event | Legal replacement of an obligation or contract by new obligations; applicability and effect depend on the governing framework. |
| LD-C0025 | Lifecycle Event | Common | Event | Dated occurrence that creates, changes or ends a trade, position, give-up, obligation or other business object of the trading and clearing lifecycle, or its legal effect, recorded with when it happened, when it takes effect and when it was recorded, plus business dates by role. A correction or cancellation is a new event that references the original; nothing is deleted. Changes to rules, calendars or fee schedules are Rule Changes, not lifecycle events. |
| LD-C0026 | Position | Common | State | Open quantity of a listed contract series, or set of open cleared OTC trades, recorded for an identified account (CCP account, CCP position account or internal account) within a clearing service, on a stated basis (long and short kept apart, or net) as of a stated business date and processing cycle, as asserted by an identified source. Exposure, notional totals and valuations are measures derived from a position, not the position itself. |
| LD-C0027 | Portfolio | Common | Entity | Collection of positions or other business objects, delimited for a stated purpose (for example margining, compression or reporting) by an identified party, as of a time; membership has no legal effect by itself. |
| LD-C0028 | Settlement Obligation | Common | Obligation | Duty of an identified debtor toward an identified creditor to transfer a stated amount of cash in a currency, or a stated quantity of an asset, by a due date and settlement deadline through a stated settlement route, arising from an identified source: variation margin settled to market, price alignment, option premium, final cash settlement, physical delivery, a margin call to be met in cash or assets, a fee invoice or interest. It can be settled in part and netted with other obligations into one payment. Distinct from the requirement or charge it comes from and from the cash movement that settles it. |
| LD-C0029 | Cash Movement | Common | Event | Transfer of funds in one currency, of a stated amount and value date, from a payer to a payee, evidenced by an identified source: a payment system, settlement bank or CCP for an external payment, or the firm's books for a transfer between accounts the firm maintains. Distinct from the settlement obligation it settles (it may settle several netted obligations, or part of one) and from the payment instruction that asked for it. A cash collateral transfer is both a cash movement and a collateral movement and is counted once. |
| LD-C0030 | Operational Exception | Common | State | Situation, raised against one or more identified business objects (trade, give-up line, position, settlement obligation, cash movement, reconciliation break), in which an expected outcome or rule has not been met and which needs action by an identified owner, usually before a stated deadline; it holds from when it is raised until it is resolved or closed. A reconciliation break, a rejected or lapsed give-up, a missed cut-off or a settlement fail can raise one but is not one. Its handling is recorded in an exception case. Technology incidents are out of scope. |
| LD-C0031 | Fee Charge | Common | Obligation | Obligation of exactly one debtor toward exactly one creditor to pay an amount for a service, arising from a chargeable event or a period under an applicable fee schedule or agreed terms; it carries its amount certainty (estimated or final) and billing stage (accrued, invoiced, disputed, adjusted, settled, written off), and may be paid by a party other than the debtor or collected by a party other than the creditor. Includes late and penalty charges arising from a missed cut-off or a settlement fail. Distinct from the revenue it may contribute to and from the cash movement that settles it. |
| LD-C0032 | Fee Schedule | Common | Reference Concept | Versioned set of rates, charge bases, conditions, discounts, caps, minimums and incentive programmes, issued by an identified issuer (a trading venue operator, a CCP, a regulator or self-regulatory body, or a broker in its client pricing terms) for a defined scope (service, clearing service, venue or segment, contract specification, client or client segment) and effective period, under which fee charges are determined. |
| LD-C0033 | Client Execution Fee | Execution | Obligation | Fee charge owed by a client to the provider of an execution service for executing its orders, stated on a charge basis (per contract side, round turn, ticket, allocation or notional) and under a pricing form (all-in, including venue charges, or fees-plus, with venue charges passed on separately). When the client's trades are given up to another clearing broker, the same charge is also executing brokerage. Distinct from the venue's own fee. |
| LD-C0034 | Client Clearing Fee | Common | Obligation | Fee charge owed by a client to its clearing broker for clearing its trades in one or more clearing services, arising from clearing events (registration of a listed lot or an OTC trade, give-in, exercise, assignment or delivery, transfer) or from a period (OTC maintenance on open trades or notional, account fees), stated on a charge basis and pricing form. Distinct from CCP fee charges passed on to the client and from income earned on the client's collateral. |
| LD-C0035 | Executing Broker Brokerage Charge | Common | Obligation | Fee charge owed to an executing broker for executing orders, most often for executions given up to a clearing broker, whose debtor (the client, or the broker that engaged the executing broker) and actual payer (often the clearing broker, which then recharges the client) are stated. From the firm's view it is brokerage received when the firm is the executing broker and brokerage paid when the firm owes or pays another executing broker. |
| LD-C0036 | Exchange Fee Charge | Common | Obligation | Fee charge whose creditor is the operator of a trading venue, for a stated service nature (trading, registration of negotiated trades, market data, connectivity, membership or other), owed by the venue member, clearing member or other debtor that the venue's fee schedule names, and possibly collected by another organisation such as the CCP. Distinct from the CCP's own fees, from regulatory transaction fees and from any charge passing it on to a client. |
| LD-C0037 | CCP Fee Charge | Common | Obligation | Fee charge whose creditor is a central counterparty, for a service of one of its clearing services (clearing or registration of trades, OTC maintenance, exercise, assignment or delivery, account and position services, holding of non-cash collateral, membership), owed by a clearing member or other participant under the CCP's fee schedule. Distinct from margin, default fund contributions, interest on cash collateral and any client charge passing it on. |
| LD-C0038 | Commission | Common | Reference Concept | Commercial label for the remuneration a broker charges a client per contract or per trade, quoted per side or per round turn, for execution, clearing or both; not an economic category of its own, so every use must be resolved to the client execution fee, the client clearing fee or a bundled execution and clearing fee. Deferred by the sponsor; resolution stays blocked. |
| LD-C0039 | Revenue | Common | Measure | Management measure of the income an observing entity earns from its business over a period, in a stated currency and under a stated management reporting basis (including whether pass-through charges are shown gross or net), attributable to a target such as a client, commercial group, service offering or order handling channel; built from fee charges earned and other income streams the basis includes. Distinct from cash received, from contribution after costs and from accounting revenue. |
| LD-C0040 | Cost | Common | Measure | Management measure of expense that an observing entity incurs for its business over a period, in a stated currency and under a stated management reporting basis, classified by cost category (venue and CCP fees not recharged, third-party brokerage, third-party clearing, connectivity and market data, funding and collateral, default fund funding, attributed shared cost) and attributable to a target only under a stated attribution rule; which costs a contribution measure deducts is set by an approved cost perimeter. |
| LD-C0041 | Execution Revenue | Execution | Measure | Revenue an observing entity earns from execution services, attributed to the Service Offering (and its service kind) under which the client is served and to the contractual client of the execution relationship, and split by order handling channel; it includes client execution fees and executing brokerage received, with venue charges passed on shown gross or net as the management reporting basis states. |
| LD-C0042 | Clearing Revenue | Common | Measure | Revenue an observing entity earns from clearing services, attributed to the Service Offering (and its service kind) under which the client is served, to the contractual client of the clearing relationship, and also per Clearing Service of the CCP; it includes client clearing fees, with CCP charges passed on shown gross or net as the management reporting basis states. Collateral income is a separate stream whose inclusion is decided by the Collateral Manager and is never implicit. |
| LD-C0043 | Margin Requirement | Common | Measure | Amount of margin that a margin provider must provide to a margin taker for an identified account or risk scope, stated by tier, component, run and as-of time, and currency basis, and determined under an identified margin methodology or contractual rule. For variation margin the amount is paid by whichever party the value has moved against, which can be the margin taker (for example a CCP paying a clearing member); the provider and taker roles stay those set by the clearing tier. It is an amount owed in principle, distinct from the margin call that demands it and the collateral that covers it. |
| LD-C0044 | Initial Margin Requirement | Common | Measure | Margin requirement intended to cover the loss the margin taker could suffer in closing out or hedging the provider's positions after its default, over an assumed close-out period at a stated confidence level under the applicable margin methodology, including the add-ons that methodology applies. Where a CCP reports the current liquidation value of premium-style options inside its initial margin total, that amount is recorded as a separate component. |
| LD-C0045 | Variation Margin Requirement | Common | Measure | Margin requirement reflecting the current market value of positions, owed by the party the value has moved against: under settled-to-market treatment, the change in value since the previous margin cycle, paid outright as a settlement of the exposure rather than posted as collateral; under collateralised-to-market treatment, the cumulative mark-to-market exposure, covered by collateral that is returned as the exposure falls. For listed contracts positions are valued at the daily settlement price. Direction is stated from the observing party. Price alignment is a separate amount and never part of variation margin. |
| LD-C0046 | Margin Call | Common | Instruction | Demand issued by a margin taker to a margin provider, from a scheduled end-of-day, scheduled intraday or ad hoc margin run, for an amount due by a stated deadline in a stated calendar and time zone, to cover the part of one or more margin requirements not covered by collateral already held after haircuts, subject to thresholds, minimum transfer amount and rounding. One call may combine several requirements and currencies, and a CCP may collect it by direct debit. Which collateral can cover a requirement depends on its type and on the agreement or CCP rules: variation margin is usually settled in cash, so securities eligible only for initial margin do not cover it. |
| LD-C0047 | Collateral | Common | State | Quantity of a collateral asset provided by a collateral provider to a collateral taker at a stated tier, under an identified collateral arrangement, by title transfer or security interest, for an account as of a time, with its market value and its value after haircuts and concentration limits; distinct from assets that are merely eligible. |
| LD-C0048 | Collateral Asset | Common | Entity | Asset that can be delivered or pledged as collateral: cash in a given currency, a security identified by its issue, or another form a collateral taker accepts under its rules (for example gold or a bank guarantee, to verify). Whether it is eligible depends on a version of a collateral eligibility schedule; whether it is committed depends on collateral actually held. |
| LD-C0049 | Collateral Movement | Common | Event | Observed transfer of collateral assets between a collateral provider and a collateral taker, or between their accounts, under a specified arrangement: delivery, return, substitution or reallocation, including a book-entry pledge or release; distinct from the collateral instruction that requests it. A cash collateral transfer is also a Cash Movement and is counted once. |
| LD-C0050 | Reconciliation | Common | Process | Process of comparing two or more records of the same business objects held by different sources (for example the firm's books and a CCP report), of a stated type (trade, give-up, position, cash, margin, collateral, fee), scope, business date by role and cycle, using stated matching keys and tolerances, to establish which items match and which differ (reconciliation breaks). It does not by itself decide which record is right; where a rule names an authoritative record, the difference is still reported as a break, never silently resolved. |

## Additions from the 2026-11 expert review (LD-C0051 to LD-C0074)

All Draft and priority P1 for the pilot use cases (change impact, market onboarding, give-up and clearing acceptance) or the management revenue priority. Source reviews and decisions: [arbitration record](../governance/reviews/2026-11-expert-review/ARBITRATION.md).

| ID | Canonical English name | Owning module | Proposed type | Working business definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LD-C0051 | Clearing Service | Common | Entity | Distinct line of clearing operated by one central counterparty under its rules for a defined product scope (its eligible contracts or OTC products), with its own membership categories, CCP account types, margin methodology, fee schedule and default-management arrangements; default resources may be shared with other services of the same CCP. Not the firm's Service Offering. |
| LD-C0052 | Service Offering | Common | Entity | Defined execution, clearing or related post-trade service that a contracting entity makes available to clients under stated terms, identified by its service kind (execution-only, clearing-only, execute-and-clear, or a related post-trade service such as average pricing or handling of trades given in), its product scope stated by product family, contract specification or clearing contract, the trading venues, venue segments or clearing services it relies on, its order handling channels and the client segments it is offered to, with a status and an effective period. The service kind indicates the usual give-up direction (execution-only usually means give-up out, clearing-only usually means give-in, by clearing submission for OTC); the actual direction of each trade is recorded on the give-up line and can differ. Distinct from a central counterparty's clearing service, from the Client role a client holds toward the contracting entity for this offering, and from the prices agreed with that client. |
| LD-C0053 | Clearing Member Acceptance | Common | Event | Decision by a clearing member to accept, wholly or partly, or to reject clearing responsibility for an identified trade or allocated quantity, for an identified client or account in a specified clearing service. It is explicit (the take-up or claim of a listed give-up line, or consent to an OTC clearing submission) or automatic within a clearing limit set before execution. Outcome values: accepted in full, accepted in part, rejected. Distinct from CCP acceptance or registration (Clearing Acceptance) and from CCP Interposition. |
| LD-C0054 | CCP Interposition | Common | Event | Event by which a central counterparty becomes counterparty to each side of a trade under a clearing service's rules, at the moment and by the mechanism (novation, open offer or other) that those rules define; the legal meaning of 'cleared', of which acceptance or registration messages are evidence. A later give-up registration, transfer or porting ends the CCP-facing trade of one member and creates a linked successor for another under the service's rules; it is not a new interposition. |
| LD-C0055 | Give-up Agreement | Common | Agreement | Agreement among a client, which may sign and instruct through its investment manager acting as its authorised representative, one or more executing brokers and one or more clearing brokers, that sets how the client's listed-derivative executions are given up to and taken up by the clearing broker: the accounts and products covered, acceptance conditions and any clearing limits it refers to, responsibility for trades not taken up or rejected, executing brokerage terms, billing and payment. It permits give-ups; it does not by itself oblige the clearing broker to accept any particular trade, which remains a Clearing Member Acceptance. |
| LD-C0056 | Client Clearing Agreement | Common | Agreement | Agreement, made of one or more documents, under which a clearing broker clears an identified client's trades in one or more clearing services, setting the client clearing legal model (principal-to-principal or agency), the segregation election per clearing service, margin and collateral terms, the basis on which the clearing broker may set clearing limits, charges, whether the client may itself clear for indirect clients, and what happens on the default of either party, including porting and close-out. It also documents the non-clearing member model, where a general clearing member clears for a venue member. |
| LD-C0057 | Clearing Broker | Common | Role | Role of a party that provides clearing of an identified client's trades in a clearing service under a Client Clearing Agreement and is responsible to that client for them, either as a clearing member of that service or through another clearing member, including a client of a clearing member that clears for its own indirect clients; distinct from admission at the CCP (Clearing Member) and from regulatory registrations such as futures commission merchant. |
| LD-C0058 | Trading Venue Member | Common | Role | Role of a party admitted under a trading venue's rules, as member or participant, to enter orders and register trades in its own name; distinct from clearing membership and from the client-facing Executing Broker role. |
| LD-C0059 | Market Access Arrangement | Common | Agreement | Access relationship by which one party can execute on one trading venue or venue segment, or clear in one clearing service, under one access model (execution: own venue membership by category, through a third-party executing member, or direct electronic access as direct market access or sponsored access; clearing: own clearing membership by category, as a non-clearing member through a general clearing member, client clearing, indirect clearing, or sponsored clearing or a direct-client model), naming the intermediary through which the party reaches the venue or clearing service; where clearing passes through a chain, each link is a separate arrangement and the chain is recorded in order. It records the role it confers, a status (only a live arrangement can be relied on) and an effective period, and is documented by one or more agreements, which are separate objects and may serve several arrangements. Execution access and clearing access are always separate arrangements. |
| LD-C0060 | Allocation Instruction | Common | Instruction | Instruction, given by a client or by a party authorised to act for it (such as its investment manager), specifying how an executed or average-priced quantity is split across target accounts and, where relevant, the receiving clearing brokers. It may be standing (agreed in advance for a defined scope of activity), given with the order before execution, or given after execution; it is versioned and can be amended until the applicable cut-off. |
| LD-C0061 | Order Handling Channel | Execution | Reference Concept | Governed classification of how a given order is worked by the executing broker and reaches the venue: care (worked by a person at the broker), broker algorithm, direct market access (orders generated by the client and routed through the broker's order infrastructure and controls) or sponsored access (orders sent by the client directly to the venue under the venue member's identity). It describes one order at a point in time and can change while the order is worked; it is not the client's permission to use direct electronic access, which belongs to the market access arrangement, and it is unrelated to a CCP's sponsored clearing model. Whether the client itself uses an algorithm, and how the price was formed (execution method), are separate attributes. |
| LD-C0062 | Contract Specification | Common | Reference Concept | Versioned set of standard terms, published by the trading venue that lists a derivative contract and usually designated by that venue's product code, from which the contract's tradeable instruments (series) are created: underlying, size or multiplier, currency, price quotation and tick, expiry cycle and key-date rules, settlement method, option terms where relevant (call or put, exercise style, strike rules, premium or futures-style), adjustment rules and trading hours. Each version has an effective date and states the series to which it applies. |
| LD-C0063 | Rule Provision | Common | Reference Concept | Versioned, citable unit of a legal act, technical standard, official guidance or relief, a CCP or venue rulebook or procedure, or the firm's own procedure, with its issuer, binding force, scope and effective period, that imposes a requirement, grants a permission, exemption or relief, defines a term or sets a parameter (such as a threshold or a deadline). Provisions can contain provisions; the citable level used is stated. A version is never edited: a new version supersedes it. Industry association guidance is not a rule provision. The firm's own procedures are recorded only in the internal layer. |
| LD-C0064 | Rule Change | Common | Event | Proposed or adopted change to one rule provision, or to another published, versioned specification (fee schedule, contract specification, business calendar, cut-off set by a rule, margin methodology, margin parameter set announced by notice, collateral eligibility schedule, clearing eligibility schedule, access requirement), by which it is created, amended, replaced or withdrawn. It is followed from proposal to application, with a lifecycle status (proposed or in consultation, adopted, approved or self-certified where the issuer's supervisor must act, in force, applicable, superseded, withdrawn) and dates qualified by their role (publication, consultation close, adoption, entry into force, application, phase-in, end of transition, and the date the firm recorded it). Contract terms agreed with a client, routine parameter publications under an unchanged method, and new series listed under an unchanged contract specification are not rule changes; nor is a change of one party's regulatory classification. One published notice can announce several rule changes, and one rule change can be announced in several notices. |
| LD-C0065 | Cut-off | Common | Reference Concept | Rule fixing the latest time by which a stated process step must be completed, set by a rule provision (regulation, CCP or venue rulebook, payment system rule, firm procedure) or by a term of an agreement, the source kind being recorded on each cut-off; expressed either as a local time on a business date of a named business calendar, read against the relevant processing window, or as a period after a stated triggering event; it states the consequence of missing it (rejection, lapse, late handling, charge, default procedure) and the authority that sets it. Only cut-offs set by a rule provision follow the Rule Change lifecycle. Versioned; on a given day it resolves to one exact time, which can move with early closes or extended trading hours. |

## Proposed specialisations

- Legal Entity is a specialisation of Party, subject to validation of the wider Party scope.
- Client, Broker, Clearing Member, Central Counterparty and Trading Venue Member specialise Party Role.
- Executing Broker and Clearing Broker specialise Broker.
- Client Execution Fee, Client Clearing Fee, Executing Broker Brokerage Charge, Exchange Fee Charge and CCP Fee Charge specialise Fee Charge.
- Execution Revenue and Clearing Revenue specialise Revenue.
- Initial Margin Requirement and Variation Margin Requirement specialise Margin Requirement.
- Give-up Agreement, Client Clearing Agreement, Market Access Arrangement and Collateral Arrangement specialise Agreement.
- CCP Account specialises Account. Cut-off specialises Rule Provision.
- Execution, Clearing Acceptance, Clearing Member Acceptance, CCP Interposition and Novation specialise Lifecycle Event (proposed by the 2026-11 review).

The full specialisation table, with status and source, is in [concept annotations](concept-annotations.md#specialisations). Service Offering and Clearing Service are deliberately not given a common "Service" parent (see LD-A0020).

Do not infer that these categories are mutually exclusive or that fee categories are additive. One economic charge may have multiple classifications; aggregation requires an explicit identity and double-counting policy. Commission is deliberately not assigned a fee parent until its intended meaning is validated.

## Coverage and deferred detail

The absence of domain-owned ETD/OTC entries here does not exclude those domains: they initially reuse shared clearing concepts. Domain-specific acceptance, lifecycle and legal rules require later specialisation.

Currencies, units and date roles are required reference vocabularies; Business Calendar (LD-C0066) is now a concept because circulars change calendars and cut-offs are expressed against them. Compression, porting, position transfer, default fund, expiry, exercise, assignment, reporting, connectivity and clearing submission remain in the roadmap; their status is tracked in the [review backlog](../governance/reviews/2026-11-expert-review/ARBITRATION.md#6-backlog). No additional concept IDs are implied by this list.
