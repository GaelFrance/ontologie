# Clearing review of the domain classification

## 1. Verdict

- Split Clearing by **business function, not by ETD versus OTC**. Almost every clearing concept is Common. The OTC-specific concepts are still in the backlog, so an OTC page would be nearly empty. Make two pages instead: **access, accounts and client clearing models** (the onboarding and legal reviewers, UC-002) and **trade flow: give-up, acceptance and registration** (the operations reviewers, UC-003 and AG-004, wave 1).
- Clearing Member (LD-C0007) and CCP (LD-C0008) are not on the Clearing page today. Neither are cut-offs, allocation instructions or exceptions, which drive every give-up question. Add them.
- The reviewer questions are too general to answer. Ask each reviewer role its own question, against a synthetic trade at the reference CCPs, and state the ETD and OTC answer separately.

Why each change:
- **A/B split.** Two reviewer populations with different timing. B is the AG-004 wave 1 review pack and matches UC-003's concept list, plus Clearing Service and Operational Exception. A matches UC-002. Reconsider an ETD/OTC split once Clearing Submission, Porting and Compression are promoted.
- **Portfolio (LD-C0027) moves to Margin.** In clearing it is used mainly for margin and cross-margin portfolios.
- **OTC Clearing product owner is added** wherever an OTC answer differs: party roles, instrument versus trade, eligibility, and margin tiers.
- **Out of my scope, flagged only:** the Fees page should list the OTC Clearing product owner as a reviewer of LD-C0034 and LD-C0037, because OTC clearing fees have a different structure (per ticket, per notional maintenance, CCP membership fees). The Regulation page should show the backlog Regulatory Obligation (clearing obligation) to the OTC Clearing product owner.

## 3. Additions to the domain pages

1. **One question block per reviewer role.** The generator splits the question cell on "Role:" and renders a short list under each role's name. A busy expert then reads only their own block.
2. **"Not yet in the catalogue" box.** Add a column to the table, "Backlog items shown". It holds names from ARBITRATION §6, because backlog items have no IDs yet. The page asks the reviewer: "Do you need this before the next release?"
   - A: Porting; Position Transfer; Clearing Submission.
   - B: Clearing Submission; Execution Method (block, EFRP); Source Record and Reconciliation Break; the ambiguities "block trade" and "trade identifiers".
   - Margin: Default Fund Contribution; Margin Excess or Shortfall; Cash Collateral Interest; Porting.
   - Post-trade: Expiry, Exercise, Assignment; Position Transfer; Portfolio Compression; Source Record and Reconciliation Break; the ambiguities "source of record" and "unwind, termination and amendment".
3. **Generic sequence on page B.** It carries concept IDs and is labelled "generic; order and moment are CCP-specific; confirm per reference CCP".
   - ETD: execution (C0017) → registration to the executing side's clearing member, or to a member designated at trade time (C0023, interposition C0054) → allocation instruction (C0060) → give-up (C0022) → member take-up or claim (C0053) before the cut-off (C0065) → trade recorded in the receiving member's CCP account (C0069) and position (C0026). If the give-up is rejected or lapses, an exception is raised (C0030) and the trade stays with the giving member.
   - OTC: bilateral trade, "alpha" (C0018) → clearing submission (backlog) → each side's clearing broker accepts after a limit check (C0053) → CCP registration (C0023) → interposition by novation (C0054, C0024). The alpha trade is extinguished and CCP-facing successor trades are created (LD-R0034). Under the principal model, a client-facing trade with the member is also created (LD-A0025).
4. **Synthetic worked example on page B.** Client A executes 100 lots through executing broker EB1 on venue V, for give-up to clearing broker CB1. CB1 takes up 60 by the cut-off and 40 lapse. Result: the line is accepted in part (60). The give-up is *not complete* under decision 5. The 40 lots stay with EB1's clearing member. If the internal record shows 100 lots at CB1, that is a break. The reviewer is asked: "Is this how your teams describe it?"
5. **Markers.** Mark each concept ETD, OTC or both, so that the OTC reviewer can skip LD-C0021, LD-C0022 and LD-C0055. Put a wave marker in the page header: B is wave 1.

## 4. Definition issues (for the Ontology Lead; not changed here)

- **LD-C0069 CCP Account.** The definition says the account is opened "in a clearing member's name". Under sponsored or direct-client models, the account may be in the sponsored participant's name, with the sponsor guaranteeing it.
- **LD-C0053 Clearing Member Acceptance.** The forms it names (take-up, claim, automatic within a limit) are ETD wording. The OTC form, a limit or credit check on clearing submission, should be named or annotated.
- **LD-C0059 Market Access Arrangement.** "Sponsored" means two things here: sponsored execution access (DEA) and a CCP's sponsored clearing model.
- **LD-C0022 Give-up / Take-up Process.** The definition says "executed". Confirm that it also covers trades registered off-book (block, EFRP). This links to the Execution Method backlog item.
- **LD-C0007 and LD-R0026 membership category.** Membership categories (general, direct or individual, a non-clearing member's relationship, sponsored) exist only as a relationship qualifier. A governed vocabulary is needed.
- **Outside scope.** The generated pages still open with an "En bref (FR)" paragraph from `tools/build_domain_views.py`. This may be inconsistent with the recent removal of French labels.
