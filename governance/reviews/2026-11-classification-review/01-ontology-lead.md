# Domain classification review: Ontology Lead view

Scope: the whole classification in `governance/review-domains.md` and the generated `domains/` pages, checked against `foundation.md` (74 concepts), the registers, the decision log, the governance proposal, ARBITRATION §6 and hub UC-001 to UC-006.

## 1. Verdict

The business cut is broadly right and every concept is covered, but the table does not say **who approves** a concept that sits in two or three domains. It also overloads Clearing (18 concepts, 19 ambiguities on one page) and has no home for the sponsor's own object, the service offering.
Fix: give each concept **exactly one primary domain** (its owner approves it) plus secondary domains where it is only shown; split Clearing into two pages; add "Service offering and market access"; filter each page's ambiguities to its primary concepts.
With these changes each page covers 4 to 8 ambiguities for its own concepts (Clearing went from 19 to 6 and 11), and most pages map to one pilot use case.

## 3. Additions to the domain pages (generator)

1. **Two concept sections:** "Concepts you approve" and "Concepts owned elsewhere: check them from your angle", the second with a link to the owning page. The `--check` option fails unless each concept has exactly one primary. The index states "each concept has exactly one primary domain".
2. **Ambiguities and decisions filtered by primary concepts.** An ambiguity is shown in full only on the pages where one of its affected concepts is primary; elsewhere it is one line with a link. Today the margin page shows LD-A0002 (fee versus revenue) and decision 5 (give-up completion) only because Settlement Obligation is on it.
3. **Sequence line** on process pages (D4, D5, D7, D10). Example for D5: Execution → Trade → Average Price Group → Allocation Instruction → give-up instruction → Clearing Member Acceptance → Clearing Acceptance → CCP Interposition → Position in a CCP Account. It is a plain-text line, readable in exported documents.
4. **Synthetic walk-through** (5 to 8 lines) per page. It uses invented actors (Client A, Executing Broker X, Clearing Broker Y, CCP Z) and tags each step with its LD-C ID. It has no amounts, no formulas and no question-to-answer resolution, so it stays within the Phase 4 gate. For D8, it shows only the stages of a charge (accrued, invoiced, settled), not a revenue calculation.
5. **"Questions you must answer"**, read from `open-questions-for-owner.md`: question 4 goes to D4 and D8, questions 9 and 10 to D8, question 11 to D7.
6. **"Known gaps (backlog)"** with the promotion trigger, so that a reviewer can say "we need this now". It is fed by a small "Backlog item → domain" table in `review-domains.md`:
   - D2: Identifier Scheme
   - D4: Execution Method, order parent/child, block trade, DMA/DEA
   - D5: Clearing Submission, Position Transfer
   - D6: Porting, Position Transfer, Portfolio Compression, Default Fund Contribution, clearing tier
   - D7: Default Fund Contribution, Margin Excess/Shortfall, Cash Collateral Interest, margin components
   - D8: Fee Rebate, Activity Volume, Cash Collateral Interest, rebate/discount/waiver
   - D9: Regulatory Obligation, Change Notice, Regulatory Report, fact versus interpretation
   - D10: Source Record, Reconciliation Break, Expiry/Exercise/Assignment, unwind/termination/amendment, source of record, trade identifiers
7. **"Why it matters"**: the pilot use cases and agents that depend on the page (D5 → UC-003/AG-004; D3 → UC-002/AG-003; D9 → UC-001/AG-002; D8 → UC-004/AG-005), and the wave. The index lists pages in wave order: D5 and D9 first.
8. **Response column**: an empty "Your verdict (OK / Change / Unsure)" column in the concept table, so that a printed or exported page doubles as the response sheet.

## 4. Definition issues (for the owners, not changed here)

- LD-C0006 Executing Broker ("party providing the execution service concerned") is thin and close to circular next to the refined LD-C0005, LD-C0057 and LD-C0058.
- LD-C0041 and LD-C0042 say "under a defined semantic contract", which is jargon a business reviewer will not understand.
- LD-C0059 Market Access Arrangement is "documented by one or more agreements" yet is a specialisation of Agreement (is_a LD-C0012). Is it the agreement or the set of agreements?
- LD-C0027 Portfolio is generic enough to overlap Account and Position scope. It could be flagged as parked, like LD-C0019.
- LD-C0004 Client could point to LD-C0073 now that Commercial Group exists.
- Consistency: the generated pages still carry an "En bref (FR)" block although decision 12 removed French labels. Confirm whether this is intended; this review does not propose extending it.
