# Review domains

Status: Draft, proposed 2026-11-01. This table is the single place that groups concepts into business domains for review. The pages in [`domains/`](../domains/README.md) are generated from it and from the catalogue; edit this table, not the generated pages.

A concept can appear in several domains (for example Segregation Model is reviewed by Clearing, Margin & Collateral and Regulation). The **owner role** answers for the domain; reviewer roles come from the [governance model proposal](governance-model-proposal.md).

| Domain | Page | Owner role | Reviewer roles | Concept IDs | What the reviewer is asked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parties, roles and clients | parties-and-roles | Reference & Client Data | Legal; ETD Clearing product owner | LD-C0001 to LD-C0008, LD-C0057, LD-C0058, LD-C0073 | Are the roles (client, broker, executing broker, clearing member, clearing broker, venue member) distinguished the way your teams use them? Is the split between legal entity, commercial group and contractual client right? |
| Products, instruments and reference data | products-and-reference-data | Reference & Client Data | ETD post-trade operations lead; Listed Execution product owner | LD-C0009, LD-C0013, LD-C0014, LD-C0015, LD-C0062, LD-C0065, LD-C0066 | Is the line between product family, contract specification and instrument workable? Are business dates, calendars and cut-offs described the way operations use them? |
| Listed derivatives execution | execution | Listed Execution product owner | ETD post-trade operations lead; Compliance | LD-C0016 to LD-C0021, LD-C0033, LD-C0041, LD-C0058 to LD-C0061 | Do order, execution, trade, allocation and average pricing match how desks and middle office talk? Is the handling channel list (care, algo, DMA, sponsored) complete? |
| Clearing (ETD and OTC) | clearing | ETD Clearing product owner | OTC Clearing product owner; ETD post-trade operations lead; Legal | LD-C0010 to LD-C0012, LD-C0018, LD-C0022 to LD-C0027, LD-C0051, LD-C0053 to LD-C0057, LD-C0059, LD-C0069 | Do give-up, take-up, clearing acceptance and CCP interposition describe the real sequence at the reference CCPs? Are accounts, positions and agreements described at the right level? |
| Margin and collateral | margin-and-collateral | Collateral Manager | Risk; Legal; Business Finance | LD-C0011, LD-C0028, LD-C0043 to LD-C0049, LD-C0070 to LD-C0072 | Are margin requirement, initial and variation margin, margin call and collateral defined correctly for both the CCP tier and the client tier? Is anything essential missing? |
| Fees, revenue and contribution | fees-revenue-and-contribution | Revenue domain owner | Collateral Manager (collateral revenue); Business Finance; Listed Execution and ETD Clearing product owners | LD-C0031 to LD-C0042, LD-C0052, LD-C0073, LD-C0074 | Do the fee types, revenue and contribution concepts let you answer "what does this client bring us, per service"? Which items count in revenue, gross or net? |
| Regulation and rule changes | regulation-and-rule-changes | Regulatory Affairs | Legal; Compliance; business management | LD-C0011, LD-C0024, LD-C0054, LD-C0063, LD-C0064, LD-C0067, LD-C0068 | Can a CCP circular, an exchange notice or a regulation be described as a rule change with its dates and status? Are regimes and regulatory classifications attached to the right parties? |
| Post-trade operations | post-trade-operations | ETD post-trade operations lead | Reference & Client Data; OTC Clearing product owner | LD-C0015, LD-C0025, LD-C0028 to LD-C0030, LD-C0050, LD-C0065, LD-C0066 | Do lifecycle events, settlement, exceptions and reconciliation match operational practice? Which statuses and deadlines must an operations agent know? |
