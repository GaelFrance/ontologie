# Review domains

Status: Draft, proposed 2026-11-01, refined the same day after the [classification review](reviews/2026-11-classification-review/ARBITRATION.md) and extended with the level-2 concepts of the [domain review](reviews/2026-11-level2-domain-review/SUPERVISION.md). This file is the single place that groups concepts into business domains for review. The pages in [`domains/`](../domains/README.md) are generated from it and from the catalogue; edit these tables, not the generated pages.

## Rules

1. **One primary domain per concept.** The owner of the primary domain approves the concept's meaning. Every concept of the catalogue has exactly one primary domain; `tools/build_domain_views.py --check` fails otherwise.
2. **"Also shown" means reviewed from this angle, approved elsewhere.** A reviewer may comment on it; the comment goes to the primary owner. A meaning change to a concept shown in several domains needs comments from the owners of those domains before approval.
3. IDs are listed in reading order, not numeric order. Ranges ("LD-C0031 to LD-C0042") are allowed.
4. Owner and reviewer roles come from the [governance model proposal](governance-model-proposal.md). Until a role is named, the sponsor arbitrates.
