# Domain experts

Status: maintained by the Ontology Lead.

Two or three experts per domain answer the questions for our firm in the domain's shared workbook (`domains/<domain>/questions-for-experts.xlsx`, initials in the "Answered by" and "Expert" columns), review their domain and propose changes. Experts propose changes through pull requests; the Ontology Lead merges. How to start: [guide for domain experts](../docs/expert-guide.md).
