# Domain owners and experts

Status: maintained by the Ontology Lead.

Each domain has one **owner**, who approves meanings in the domain and merges pull requests, and two or three **experts**, who answer the questions for our firm in the domain's shared workbook (`domains/<domain>/questions-for-experts.xlsx`, initials in the "Answered by" and "Expert" columns), review the domain and propose changes. Who may merge what: [governance and contributing](../docs/governance.md#who-merges). How to start: [contributor guide](../docs/contributor-guide.md).

## Domain owners

One person may own several domains. Owner roles per domain are in [review domains](review-domains.md).
