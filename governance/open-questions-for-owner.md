## Level-2 domain review (2026-11-01): decided

**Decided 2026-11-01: all accepted** (see [decision log](decision-log.md), decisions 16 to 22). The table is kept as the record of what was proposed.

The ten domain experts and the supervisor ([report](reviews/2026-11-level2-domain-review/SUPERVISION.md)) proposed 13 changes of meaning to foundation concepts. They are **not applied**: the current definition stays until the sponsor decides. Clarifications that keep the meaning were applied as Draft (see the [change log](../CHANGELOG.md)).

| # | Concept | Current definition | Proposed definition | Why | Who is affected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **CCP Account** (LD-C0069) | Account opened by a CCP within a clearing service in a clearing member's name, of a defined type (house; omnibus client, net or gross; individually segregated client; indirect-client variants; other regimes' customer account classes), in which positions, margin and collateral are recorded. | Account opened by a central counterparty within one clearing service, in the name of a clearing member or, under a sponsored or direct-client model, of the participant admitted in its own name, of a type defined by that service's rules (house; client omnibus, net or gross; individually segregated client; indirect client, basic or gross omnibus; regime-specific customer account classes such as the US futures, foreign futures and cleared swaps customer accounts), to which the CCP attributes positions and, directly or through a linked margin or collateral unit defined by its rules, margin requirements and collateral. | Lets a CCP account be opened in a sponsored participant's own name, names regime-specific account classes, and states the margining basis per account kind. | Clearing services, margin and the operations agent. |
| M2 | **Position** (LD-C0026) | Resulting exposure or quantity for a defined scope, aggregation basis and as-of time. | Open quantity of a listed contract series, or set of open cleared OTC trades, recorded for an identified account (CCP account, CCP position account or internal account) within a clearing service, on a stated basis (long and short kept apart, or net) as of a stated business date and processing cycle, as asserted by an identified source. Exposure, notional totals and valuations are measures derived from a position, not the position itself. | Ties a position to an account and a source, and excludes exposure, notional totals and valuations, which become measures derived from a position. | Clearing, margin, post-trade reconciliation and the operations agent. |
| M3 | **Allocation Instruction** (LD-C0060) | Instruction, given before or after execution by a client or its investment manager, specifying how an executed or average-priced quantity is split across target accounts and, where relevant, clearing brokers; versioned and amendable until the applicable cut-off. | Instruction, given by a client or by a party authorised to act for it (such as its investment manager), specifying how an executed or average-priced quantity is split across target accounts and, where relevant, the receiving clearing brokers. It may be standing (agreed in advance for a defined scope of activity), given with the order before execution, or given after execution; it is versioned and can be amended until the applicable cut-off. | Widens Allocation Instruction to standing instructions held by the broker and to any party authorised to act for the client. | Execution, give-up; Standing Allocation Instruction depends on it. |
| M4 | **Allocation** (LD-C0020) | Assignment of a quantity or amount to target beneficiaries or accounts; instruction and completed event are later specialisations. | Process by which an executed or average-priced quantity is assigned to one or more target accounts, in allocation lines, following an allocation instruction or a standing rule. An allocation to an account carried by a clearing member other than the executing side's clearing member (whether the trade is registered first to that member or designated to the receiving member in advance) starts a give-up; otherwise it is internal. Allocation distributes quantity; averaging, which may come before it, sets the price. | Narrows Allocation to executed or average-priced quantities, so that cost, revenue or collateral allocation no longer resolve to it; the instruction is now a separate concept. | Execution and give-up; fees and margin pages switch to 'attribution'. |
| M5 | **Commission** (LD-C0038) | Contractual remuneration category whose boundaries and overlap with fee types require business validation. | Commercial label for the remuneration a broker charges a client per contract or per trade, quoted per side or per round turn, for execution, clearing or both; not an economic category of its own, so every use must be resolved to the client execution fee, the client clearing fee or a bundled execution and clearing fee. Deferred by the sponsor; resolution stays blocked. | Gives Commission a meaning (a commercial label to be resolved to execution, clearing or bundled fees) although the sponsor deferred it; resolution stays blocked. | Fees page and the revenue agent. |
| M6 | **Trading Venue** (LD-C0009) | Organised trading facility or framework, distinguished from its legal operator and reporting facilities. | Multilateral trading system or facility, operated under its own rules by an identified market operator, in which multiple third-party buying and selling interests meet, or privately negotiated trades are registered under its rules, so that contracts result; identified by a market identifier code and possibly divided into segments. Includes regulated markets, MTFs, OTFs, designated contract markets and swap execution facilities. Distinct from its operator, from a central counterparty, from a systematic internaliser or single-dealer platform, and from trade reporting services. | Narrows Trading Venue to multilateral systems; excludes systematic internalisers, single-dealer platforms and internal crossing systems. | Execution, market access and fee pages; venue-traded OTC derivatives on MTFs, OTFs and swap execution facilities stay in. |
| M7 | **Variation Margin Requirement** (LD-C0045) | Amount payable or receivable reflecting the change in value of positions since the previous margin cycle, with direction stated from the observing party; under settled-to-market treatment it gives rise to a Settlement Obligation, under collateralised-to-market treatment to collateral that must be returned as exposure changes. | Margin requirement reflecting the current market value of positions, owed by the party the value has moved against: under settled-to-market treatment, the change in value since the previous margin cycle, paid outright as a settlement of the exposure rather than posted as collateral; under collateralised-to-market treatment, the cumulative mark-to-market exposure, covered by collateral that is returned as the exposure falls. For listed contracts positions are valued at the daily settlement price. Direction is stated from the observing party. Price alignment is a separate amount and never part of variation margin. | Covers both variation margin treatments: settled-to-market (change since the previous cycle, settled outright) and collateralised-to-market (cumulative exposure, covered by collateral). | Margin, post-trade settlement and collateral revenue. |
| M8 | **Party** (LD-C0001) | Identifiable actor that can participate in a business relationship. | Person or organisation that can be identified and can act, or hold rights and obligations, in a business relationship: a natural person, a legal entity, or a unit without its own legal personality that is named separately in agreements or records (for example a branch or an investment fund). The firm's internal teams, desks and systems are not parties. | Narrows Party: internal teams, desks and systems are excluded and the kinds of party are named. | Parties, accounts and roles; agents can no longer treat a desk as a counterparty. |
| M9 | **Broker** (LD-C0005) | Role of a party that acts on behalf of others to execute orders and/or to clear or carry their trades under an identified agreement. | Role of a party that provides execution of orders, or clearing or carrying of trades and positions, as a service to clients under an identified agreement, whether it acts as agent or as principal toward them. Executing Broker and Clearing Broker are its kinds. | Broker currently implies agency only, but clearing brokers under the principal model and riskless principal executing brokers face the client as principal. | Executing Broker and Clearing Broker stay kinds of Broker in both legal models. |
| M10 | **Cut-off** (LD-C0065) | Rule provision setting the time by which a process step must be completed, expressed as a local time relative to a business date of a named business calendar, with the consequence of missing it (rejection, lapse, late handling, charge) and the authority that sets it. | Rule fixing the latest time by which a stated process step must be completed, set by a rule provision (regulation, CCP or venue rulebook, payment system rule, firm procedure) or by a term of an agreement, the source kind being recorded on each cut-off; expressed either as a local time on a business date of a named business calendar, read against the relevant processing window, or as a period after a stated triggering event; it states the consequence of missing it (rejection, lapse, late handling, charge, default procedure) and the authority that sets it. Only cut-offs set by a rule provision follow the Rule Change lifecycle. Versioned; on a given day it resolves to one exact time, which can move with early closes or extended trading hours. | A cut-off may be set by an agreement term and may be a period after a triggering event; Cut-off is therefore no longer a kind of Rule Provision but is 'set by' a Rule Provision or an Agreement term, and only rule-set cut-offs follow the Rule Change lifecycle. | Settlement Deadline, Firm Cut-off and Take-up Deadline depend on it; regulation, post-trade, give-up and margin pages. |
| M11 | **Rule Change** (LD-C0064) | Proposal, adoption, amendment, withdrawal or replacement of a rule provision or of another published versioned specification (fee schedule, contract specification, business calendar, margin methodology, eligibility schedule), with a status and role-qualified dates; the trigger object of change-impact analysis. | Proposed or adopted change to one rule provision, or to another published, versioned specification (fee schedule, contract specification, business calendar, cut-off set by a rule, margin methodology, margin parameter set announced by notice, collateral eligibility schedule, clearing eligibility schedule, access requirement), by which it is created, amended, replaced or withdrawn. It is followed from proposal to application, with a lifecycle status (proposed or in consultation, adopted, approved or self-certified where the issuer's supervisor must act, in force, applicable, superseded, withdrawn) and dates qualified by their role (publication, consultation close, adoption, entry into force, application, phase-in, end of transition, and the date the firm recorded it). Contract terms agreed with a client, routine parameter publications under an unchanged method, and new series listed under an unchanged contract specification are not rule changes; nor is a change of one party's regulatory classification. One published notice can announce several rule changes, and one rule change can be announced in several notices. Type: Process (proposed). | Changes type from Event to Process with a lifecycle status, widens the changed objects, and states what is not a rule change. | Regulatory change agent; routine parameter files, client contract terms and new series no longer flood it. |
| M12 | **Regulatory Classification** (LD-C0068) | Time-bounded status of a legal entity, role assignment, account, trading venue, CCP or clearing service under a named regulatory regime, with its basis (self-assessment, notification, authority decision, calculation), asserting source and evidence; "unknown" is a value, never a default. | Time-bounded status that a named regulatory regime gives to a legal entity (or a branch where the regime says so), a role assignment, an account, a trading venue or venue segment, a CCP or clearing service, an instrument, contract specification or product family, or a trade, with its basis (authority decision or registration, notification, calculation, self-assessment, representation by the counterparty), the source that asserts it and the evidence; valid time and record time are both kept, and 'unknown' is a value, never a default. | Widens the attachment levels to instruments, contract specifications, product families, trades, trading venue segments and branches (where the regime says so). | Regulation, parties and instruments pages; product-level obligations can now be expressed. |
| M13 | **Market Access Arrangement** (LD-C0059) | Arrangement, documented by one or more agreements, by which a party can execute on a trading venue or clear in a clearing service, with an access model (direct membership by category, through a third-party member, direct electronic access, client clearing, indirect clearing, sponsored or direct-client model), any intermediary, a status and an effective period. | Access relationship by which one party can execute on one trading venue or venue segment, or clear in one clearing service, under one access model (execution: own venue membership by category, through a third-party executing member, or direct electronic access as direct market access or sponsored access; clearing: own clearing membership by category, as a non-clearing member through a general clearing member, client clearing, indirect clearing, or sponsored clearing or a direct-client model), naming the intermediary through which the party reaches the venue or clearing service; where clearing passes through a chain, each link is a separate arrangement and the chain is recorded in order. It records the role it confers, a status (only a live arrangement can be relied on) and an effective period, and is documented by one or more agreements, which are separate objects and may serve several arrangements. Execution access and clearing access are always separate arrangements. | Turns the arrangement into one access relationship documented by separate agreements, lists the access models, and records intermediary chains link by link. | Market access, parties, clearing services and give-up pages. |

2. Decide whether third-party execution also covers an executing broker that is a venue member but chooses not to use its membership. Today both pages are aligned on decision 3: only where the firm is not a member.
