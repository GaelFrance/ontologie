# Scope

Status: Draft

## Domains

| Domain | In scope |
|---|---|
| Listed Derivatives Execution | High-touch and electronic execution; orders, fills, instruments, venues, clients and accounts; brokers; revenue and costs; allocation, average pricing, give-up; corrections and cancellations; reporting; connectivity, routing, algorithms and market access; exceptions and reference data |
| ETD Clearing | Client clearing; CCP/member/broker relationships; registration and take-up; accounts and segregation; positions, margin, collateral and relevant default-fund concepts; fees and brokerage; settlement, cash movements, expiry, exercise, assignment and transfers; reconciliation, exceptions and reporting |
| OTC Clearing | Client clearing of IRS and other eligible OTC derivatives; submission, acceptance and legal transformations; accounts, positions, segregation, margin and collateral; settlement, compression and portability; fees, reconciliation, exceptions and reporting |

Regulatory concepts are contextual and versioned by jurisdiction, regime and effective date. This foundation does not assert that any particular obligation applies universally.

## Iterative delivery

1. **Current:** architecture, concept template, approximately 30–50 concepts, relationships, ambiguities and repository structure. Stop for business review.
2. After approval: complete and expand Common, Execution, ETD Clearing and OTC Clearing concepts separately.
3. Develop detailed participant, product, lifecycle, fee, account, position, margin, collateral, settlement and regulatory taxonomies.
4. Demonstrate natural-language resolution and test ambiguity handling.
5. Only after source schemas are supplied: define and validate physical mappings.

The 50-concept catalogue is a prioritised foundation, not a completeness claim. Routing, strategies, compression, portability, default funds, reporting and detailed lifecycle events remain planned scope.
